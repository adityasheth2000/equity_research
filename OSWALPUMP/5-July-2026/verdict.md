## 1. Company Overview

Oswal Pumps Limited (incorporated 2003) is a vertically integrated manufacturer of solar-powered and grid-connected pumps, electric motors, and solar PV modules under the **'Oswal'** brand. The company is a fully integrated turnkey provider of solar pumping systems, with backward integration spanning pumps, motors, solar panels, mounting structures, and Balance of System (BoS) kits.

**Manufacturing Facilities:**
- **Pumps & Motors:** One of India's largest single-site facilities (Karnal, Haryana), 41,076 sq. mt., operating since 2010
- **Solar Modules:** 570 MW capacity (commissioned Jan 2024), 11,002 sq. mt., expanding to 2.1 GW

**Certifications:** ISO 9001:2015, ISO 45001:2018, ISO 14001:2015. MNRE-approved for solar modules.

**Distribution:** 1,383 distributors across India (as of Mar 31, 2026); 402 "Oswal Shoppe" exclusive retail outlets.

**Brand Heritage:** 23+ years in pump engineering, design, manufacturing, and testing. The Oswal brand has ~55 years of agricultural sector presence.

### 7.2 Capacity Expansion (from IPO proceeds)

**Pump & Motor Plant:**
- Automation, modernization, and capacity expansion from ~200,000 to 500,000+ pumps/year
- Expected completion: **Q3 FY27**

**Solar Module Plant (via Oswal Solar):**
- Capacity expansion from 570 MW to 2,100 MW (1,500 MW addition)
- Backward integration: Aluminium extrusion plant (₹434 Mn), EVA manufacturing (₹268 Mn)
- Phase 1 (1 GW): expected **Q1 FY27**
- Phase 2 (0.5 GW): expected **Q3 FY27**
- Solar module plant relocated to land parcel adjacent to existing facility (28,000 sq. mt., leased for 15 years) — better logistics, shared R&D/admin infra

**New Product Development:**
- On-grid solar inverters (in-house manufacturing by H2 FY27)
- Junction Box Back Sheet integration
- Helical rotor pumps (developed, in performance testing)
- Progressive Cavity Pumps (PCP), Industrial Centrifugal Pumps, Pressure Pumps, Reciprocating Pumps — targeting oil & gas, food processing, wastewater, power generation

### 7.3 Diversification into New Solar Segments

**Rooftop Solar (PM Surya Ghar: Muft Bijli Yojana):**
- FY26: first order received, marking segment entry
- SPV formed: Oswal Doon Baran Bundi Solar Projects Ltd (60% Oswal Solar, 40% Doon Infrapower)
- Executing rooftop solar PV projects on Rajasthan state government buildings under Hybrid Annuity Model (HAM)
- PM Surya Ghar: ₹75,000 Cr outlay, target 1 crore households, ₹22,000 Cr allocated in Union Budget 2026-27
- Scheme performance (as of May 15, 2026): 6.09 Mn applications, 11.34 GW installed, 3.14 Mn installations, ₹21,744 Cr subsidy released

**Utility & C&I Solar:**
- 300 MW combined pipeline across rooftop, utility, and C&I segments
- Management expects these new verticals to contribute **~₹1,000 Cr** in FY27

**Strategic Rationale:** Reduce single-scheme dependency (PM KUSUM), broaden addressable market, build a more resilient revenue base.

### 7.4 Distribution Expansion

- Distributors grew from 574 (FY23) to 1,383 (Mar'26)
- "Oswal Shoppe" exclusive retail outlets: 402 (launched Mar 2024)
- Target states for expansion: Chhattisgarh, Karnataka, Assam, Kerala, Andhra Pradesh, Telangana, Tamil Nadu, Gujarat
- North region has the largest distributor base (621 in 9M FY26)
