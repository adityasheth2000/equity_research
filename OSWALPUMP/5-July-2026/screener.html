      Incorporated in 2003, Oswal Pumps Limited is a leading manufacturer and distributor of a wide range of pumps.[1]

      Key Points

      Business Overview:[1]
      OPL is one of the vertically integrated
      solar pump manufacturers. The company manufactures solar-powered and grid-connected submersible and monoblock pumps, induction and submersible electric motors, and solar modules under the ‘Oswal’ brand. It also provides turnkey solar pumping systems under the PM Kusum Scheme, directly or through empaneled partners.
