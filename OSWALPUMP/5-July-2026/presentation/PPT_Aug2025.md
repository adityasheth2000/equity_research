The slide details a manufacturing flow chart demonstrating "Raw materials processed in-house" capabilities across three streams:

*   **General Flow:** Inbound raw materials $\rightarrow$ Raw materials processed in-house $\rightarrow$ Assembly of processed raw materials $\rightarrow$ Quality testing $\rightarrow$ Packaging $\rightarrow$ Transportation & installation.
*   **Stream A (Pump):**
    *   Inputs: Farmer bolts, Steel sheets.
    *   In-house Processing: Manufacturing stator and rotor, copper wires, impellers, bearings, and couplings; casting (melting steel sheets); molding (molten steel parts).
*   **Stream B (Solar Panel):**
    *   Inputs: Solar cells (acquired from domestic vendor).
    *   In-house Processing: Laser cell cutting, connecting solar cells in series/inspection (String EL), bus bar, lamination, trimming, framing.

    *   Inputs: Components (PCBs - noted as manufactured in-house in the flowchart context).
    *   In-house Processing: PCB manufacturing, assembly of components, API and chip integration.
*   **Final Assembly:** Integration of the Pump (Stream A), Solar Panel (Stream B), and Controller (Stream C) to create the Solar Pump with Solar Panel.
