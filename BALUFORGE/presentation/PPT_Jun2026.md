**Technological Capabilities & Competitive Advantages**
The company utilizes advanced **GERB technology** (Germany) for its forging operations. Key strategic benefits include:
*   **Energy Efficiency:** The Hydraulic Power Head enhances hammer capacity, resulting in significant energy savings.
*   **Operational Uptime:** Anti-vibration technology reduces vibration by up to **90%** and eliminates breakdowns, increasing equipment uptime.
*   **Asset Longevity:** The technology enhances the life of the presses and hammers and increases accuracy.
*   **Foundation Technology:** Utilizes spring-supported hammer foundations (as opposed to conventional foundations) to manage vibration.
