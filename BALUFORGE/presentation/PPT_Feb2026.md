**Slide Title:** Q3 and 9M FY2026 Earnings Press Release – Management Commentary

**Financial Performance Metrics:**
*   **Revenue (9M FY26):** Rs. 8,438 million (Year-on-Year Growth: 29.0%)
*   **EBITDA (3Q FY26):** Rs. 2,396 million (Year-on-Year Growth: 36.1%, Margin: 28.4%)
*   **Profit After Tax - PAT (3Q FY26):** Rs. 1,932 million (PAT Margin: 22.6%)

**Strategic Initiatives & Operational Updates:**
*   **Precision Machining Capacity:** Successfully operationalised a high-impact strategic asset equipped with advanced 7-Axis and 11-Axis CNC machining lines. This allows for the manufacture of highly complex components from specialised alloys with micron-level accuracy, moving the company up the value chain.
*   **Defense Manufacturing:** Commercialised a dedicated artillery shell production line with a specific focus on high-demand 155mm large calibre shells.
*   **Geographic Expansion/Validation:** Formal induction into the NATO Supply Chain, providing access to demanding defence markets and validating adherence to global quality/compliance standards.
*   **Capacity Utilization/Order Visibility:** Positioned to service engagements for up to five years due to advanced precision machining facilities and production lines.

**Management Commentary:**
*   **Driver of Performance:** Growth driven by sustained demand momentum, stable cost structures, and operating leverage benefits.
*   **Strategic Positioning:** Emphasizes "disciplined execution, margin resilience, and the ongoing strengthening of Balu Forge’s integrated manufacturing platform."
*   **Long-term Outlook:** The conversion of strategic investments into operational assets places the company on a trajectory of "scalable and sustainable growth in defence and other critical segments."
*   **Policy Alignment:** Reinforces commitment to the "Atmanirbhar Bharat vision" by building indigenous capabilities in a strategically critical segment.

**Company Profile and Strategic Overview (About Balu Forge Industries)**

**Business Description:**
*   **Core Business:** Leading Indian precision engineering company delivering forged and machined components.
*   **Product Capabilities:** Comprehensive portfolio ranging from 1 kg to 1,000 kg and up to 3 meters in length.
*   **Applications/Sectors:** Automotive, industrial vehicles, earthmoving equipment, wind energy, aerospace, defence, oil and gas, railways, marine, and agriculture.

**Operational Capabilities & Facilities:**
*   **Location:** Operations based in Belgaum, Karnataka, on a campus over 46+ acres.
*   **Production Model:** Fully integrated forging and machining capabilities.
*   **Equipment:** Equipped with high-capacity hydraulic hammers, forging presses, CNC machining units, an in-house tool room, and metallurgical labs.

**Strategy and Initiatives:**
*   **R&D:** Specialized division focusing on new materials and rapid prototyping.
*   **Growth Focus:** Expanding defence production, enhancing automation, and strengthening global partnerships.
*   **Key Priorities:** Operational scalability, customer diversification, and ESG commitments.
