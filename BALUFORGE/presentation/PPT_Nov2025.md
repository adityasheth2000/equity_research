**Key Strategic & Financial Observations:**

*   **Asset Efficiency & Turnover:** The company maintains a relatively high asset turnover ratio compared to peers. This is due to a historical strategy of acquiring used assets through global auctions, private treaties, and liquidations, which keeps capital expenditures low and improves asset efficiency.
*   **CWIP vs. Depreciated Assets:** A low capitalized PP&E base temporarily inflates the asset turnover ratio. The chart tracks the transfer of assets from CWIP to fixed assets.
    *   **FY25:** Approximately 74% of total fixed assets were classified as CWIP.
    *   **H1 FY26:** This proportion reduced to 48% as a significant portion of CWIP was capitalized.
*   **Future Outlook on Ratios:** As assets continue to be capitalized over time, the asset base is expected to increase, causing the asset turnover ratio to gradually normalize.
*   **Tax & Duty Advantages (MOOWR Scheme):**
    *   Most machinery is imported under the Government of India's MOOWR (Manufactures under Overseas Workers / Bonded Manufacturing) scheme via the Hattargi Facility (bonded manufacturing unit).
    *   **Cash Flow Benefit:** Machinery imported under this scheme is exempt from customs duty and IGST, improving upfront cash flow management.
    *   **Depreciation Benefit:** Crucially, assets acquired under the MOOWR scheme are **not subject to depreciation**, even after being commissioned and in use.
