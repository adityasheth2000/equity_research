## Detailed Rationale
Crisil Ratings has reaffirmed its ratings on the bank facilities of Balu Forge Industries Limited (BFIL) and has simultaneously withdrawn the ratings upon receipt of a withdrawal request from the company and a No-objection certificate from the bankers. The rating reflects BFIL's established market position in the crankshaft manufacturing industry, its improving scale of operations, and a comfortable financial risk profile. These strengths are partially offset by a large working capital cycle and susceptibility to volatility in raw material prices.

## Key Rating Drivers — Strengths
*   **Established Market Position:** The company benefits from extensive promoter experience (Mr. Chandock has >3 decades of experience) and a presence across 80 countries. This has led to healthy customer/supplier relationships and repeated order flow.
*   **Revenue Growth:** Operating income grew significantly from Rs. 562 Crores (FY2024) to Rs. 924 Crores (FY2025).
*   **Increasing Scale of Operations:** Scale has grown for the last three years. Estimated growth is expected to be around 30% to 35% YoY in FY2026 over FY2025. Group revenues were around Rs. 533 Crores in H1 of FY2026.
*   **Comfortable Financial Risk Profile:**
    *   Improved Net Worth: Rs. 1,020 Crores as on March 31, 2025 (led by equity infusion).
    *   Improved Capital Structure: Gearing ratio stood at 0.10 times and Total Outside Liability to Adjusted Net Worth at 0.26 times (as on March 31, 2025), down from 0.20 times and 0.41 times a year ago.
    *   Debt Protection: Interest cover at 23 times and NCATD at 2.04 times for FY2025.

## Key Rating Drivers — Weaknesses
*   **Large Working Capital Cycle:** Gross current assets (net of cash) ranged around 212 days on March 31, 2025 (down from 297 days a year ago). Debtor days stood at 158 days. Dependence on bank limits remains high despite improvement in debtor days.
*   **Susceptibility to Raw Material Volatility:** Key raw materials (steel and aluminum) are commodities. Operating margins are susceptible to price fluctuations due to intense competition which constrains the ability to pass on cost increases.
*   **Ongoing Capex Risk:** Aggressive capital expenditure for setting up forging capacities. Any delay in completion, ramp-up, or cost overrun may impact profitability.

## Liquidity
*   **Bank Limits Utilisation:** Utilisation on the packing credit facility averaged around **99%** for the last 12 months ended September 2025.
*   **Cash Accruals:** Net cash accruals expected to be over **Rs. 200 Crore**.
*   **Term Debt Obligation:** Term debt obligation estimated at **Rs. 1–8 Crore** over the medium term.
*   **Current Ratio:** Stood at 2.5 times as on March 31, 2025.
*   **Bank Balance:** Around Rs. 96 Crore as on March 31, 2025.
*   **Assessment:** Adequate liquidity; however, accretion to cash balance remains moderate due to high working capital intensity and aggressive capital expenditure.

## Outlook & Rating Sensitivity
*   **Outlook:** **Negative**
    *   *Reasoning:* Business & liquidity risk profile may get impacted on account of large working capital requirements amidst growing scale of operations.
*   **Upward Factors:**
    *   Improvement in working capital cycle (debtor days sustained below 90–100 days).
    *   Sustained double-digit growth in scale of operations and operating profitability leading to higher cash accruals.
*   **Downward Factors:**
    *   Sizeable stretch in working capital cycle (debtors sustaining above 130 days).
    *   Deterioration in financial risk profile due to debt-funded capex or larger dividend payout.
    *   Significant decline in scale of operations or operating margins leading to lower cash accruals.
