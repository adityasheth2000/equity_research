**Business Model Evolution & Portfolio Metrics**

The presentation outlines the company's transition through three distinct phases of evolution:

*   **Phase 1: Contract Manufacturer (Historical Context)**
    *   Market-led Company focusing on plain vanilla generic products.
    *   Introduced products that plugged marketplace gaps.
    *   Operating model characterized by negative working capital.
    *   Geographically focused on 2 main geographies (Latin America & Francophone Africa) across 10 countries.

*   **Phase 2: Becoming a Responsible Pharma Company (Transition)**
    *   Evolved into a technology and research-led marketing company.
    *   Product mix shifted to include generics, branded generics, specialty molecules, and injectables.
    *   Strategy involves launching specialty niche products to create new markets in varied therapy segments.
    *   **Financial Strategy:** Selective use of Credit strategy to increase market share while remaining cash surplus.
    *   **Geography:** Extended presence to Regulated markets like USA; recent entries into Canada, Australia, Mexico, and Brazil (upcoming).

*   **Phase 3: Diversified Product Portfolio (Current/Future Focus)**
    *   **Product Count:** 5,000+ Products registered and 650+ Pharmaceutical formulations.
