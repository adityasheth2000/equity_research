# Evolution Over the Decades | Caplin Point Laboratories Limited

### Strategic Milestones & Timeline (2014–2026+)
*   **2014:** Commenced work on Regulated Markets Injectable unit.
*   **2017:** Liquid injectable facility received EU & USFDA Approval.
*   **2018:** Initiated first commercial shipments to the US.
*   **2019:** Fidelity investment made into Caplin Steriles.
*   **2021:** Executed Rs. 600 Cr. Expansion project. Amaris Clinical (CRO wing) secured USFDA approval.
*   **2022:** Completed acquisition of API plant in Vizag (backward integration). Amaris Clinical secured ISP Chile approval.
*   **2023:** Incorporated Caplin Steriles USA Inc. to launch own label in the US.
*   **2024:** Oncology facility began production at SIDCO Kakkalur, Chennai.
*   **2025:** Scheduled commencement of CSU (Contract Sales Organization?) operations.
*   **2026+ (Outlook):** Planned acquisition of 10 ANDAs with multiple USFDA approvals expected; Chile subsidiary scheduled for commercial operation.

### Business Metrics & Portfolio Depth
*   **Registered Products:** 5,000+ products registered.
*   **Formulations:** 650+ Pharmaceutical formulations.
*   **Therapeutic Reach:** Covers 36 Therapeutic Segments.
*   **Market Coverage:** Product mix addresses over 65% of the WHO essential drug list.
*   **Product Mix Strategy:**
    *   Evolving from plain vanilla generic products to a mix including branded generics, specialty molecules, and injectables.
    *   Shift toward "complex spaces" and niche products to create new markets.

### Geographic Expansion & Market Access
*   **Historical Base:** Originally focused on 2 main geographies (Latin America & Francophone Africa) across 10 countries with a "Stock & Sale model."
*   **Regulated Markets Entry:** Expanded presence into regulated markets including the USA, Canada, Australia, Mexico, and Brazil (entry noted as "shortly" at time of presentation).

### Strategic Pillars & Financial Characteristics
*   **Working Capital Model:** Described as a "Negative-working capital business" (indicates efficient working capital management/cash conversion cycle).
*   **Credit Strategy:** Selective use of credit strategy employed to increase market share while maintaining a cash surplus position.
*   **Vertical Integration:** Moving from contract manufacturing to becoming a "responsible pharma company" with proprietary labels (e.g., Caplin Steriles USA Inc.).
*   **Focus Areas:** Transitioning from a market-led company with generic products to a technology and research-led marketing company with a diversified portfolio ensuring larger shelf share across pharmacies.
