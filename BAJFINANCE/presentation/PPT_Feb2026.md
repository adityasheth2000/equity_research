### **Update on accelerated ECL provision and one-time charge of New Labour Codes**

**1. Accelerated ECL provision:**
*   **Purpose:** To enhance balance sheet resilience amid a volatile global economic environment.
*   **Action:** Strengthened provisioning framework by implementing a minimum Loss Given Default (LGD) floor across all businesses.
*   **Financial Impact:** An accelerated ECL provision of **₹ 1,406 crore** was made in Q3 across stages.

**2. One-time charge of New Labour Codes:**
*   **Reason:** Increase in gratuity liabilities arising from past service cost due to New Labour Codes released on 21 November 2025.
*   **Financial Impact:** One-time exceptional charge of **₹ 265 crore**.
