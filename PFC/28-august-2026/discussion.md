# PFC — Discussion Notes — 28 August 2026

## Q: Is the disbursement falling YoY? Any particular reason?

Q1 FY27 disbursements: **₹20,176 Cr vs ₹36,152 Cr (–44% YoY).**

Three reasons, from management's concall commentary (Q4 FY26, May 2026):

**1. RBPF → medium-term loan shift (structural, mostly benign)**
DISCOMs used to take 6-month revolving bill-payment facilities (RBPF) which inflated disbursement counts — every rollover counted as a fresh disburse. Management shifted DISCOMs to 3–7 year medium-term loans. Same end-use, less churn in the headline number.

**2. Bank refinancing of commissioned/renewable assets (the real worry)**
The rate-cut cycle gives PSU banks (cheap CASA/G-sec funding) a pricing edge on low-risk, post-commissioning assets. Management: *"without these prepayments, our loan asset growth would have been within our guided range of 10–11%."* The best assets keep walking off PFC's book.

**3. LPS scheme wind-down**
The Late Payment Surcharge scheme drove ~₹62,500 Cr of short-term DISCOM lending. LIS had already ended, and LPS disbursements are now negligible. FY25 concall flagged this as materially behind them — Q1 FY27 is reflecting the gap.

**Context:** Management guided FY27 full-year loan growth at ~10%, implying disbursements should meaningfully accelerate from the Q1 trough as the sanctions pipeline (₹2.5–3 lakh Cr sanstd-but-not-disbursed) converts. Next 1–2 quarterly prints will tell if Q1 was a blip or the new normal.
