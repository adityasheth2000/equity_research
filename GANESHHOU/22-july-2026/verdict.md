**Peer Set:** DLF, Lodha (Macrotech), Phoenix Mills, Prestige Estates, Oberoi Realty, Godrej Properties, Anant Raj

## What the Company Does

Ganesh Housing is an Ahmedabad-based real estate developer with a 30+ year track record. Founded in 1991 by the Patel family, it is the flagship entity of the Ganesh Group. The company builds and sells residential and commercial properties exclusively in Ahmedabad, Gujarat.

**How they make money (3 verticals):**

1. **Project Sales (~70-90% of near-term revenue):** Develops and sells residential apartments/townships and commercial office/retail spaces. Revenue recognised on project completion (Ind AS 115 — percentage-of-completion method). Major projects: Malabar Retreat (premium residential, ~INR 450-500 Cr GDV), Malabar Exotica (completed, 100% sold), One 91 Thaltej (planned commercial, INR 2,100 Cr GDV).

2. **Annuity / Lease Rental (emerging):** Million Minds Tech City — a 65-acre IGBC Platinum-certified IT SEZ near SG Highway, Nirma University. Phase 1 (~0.85 msf leasable) completed in May 2026, inaugurated by Home Minister Amit Shah. Lease rentals expected to commence Q3 FY27 at ~INR 75+ Cr p.a. for Phase 1. 60-65% pre-leased via LOIs/EOIs. Multiple future phases planned.

3. **Land Monetisation:** Largest land bank among Ahmedabad-focused listed developers — ~518 acres fully paid, concentrated in high-growth corridors (Godhavi Township ~411 acres, Million Minds SEZ ~65 acres, Thaltej/Malabar corridors ~42 acres). Sells land parcels outright or through plotted development schemes. Godhavi Phase 1 (50 acres) — 46 acres monetised so far at avg. ~INR 14.1 Cr/acre.

**Customers:** Homebuyers (mid-to-premium residential, INR 1-5 Cr ticket sizes), corporate tenants/occupiers (IT/ITeS, GCCs, co-working for Million Minds), and other developers buying land parcels.

## Company Overview & Industry Positioning

- **Geographic focus:** 100% Ahmedabad. Management has stated a 7-10 year development runway within Ahmedabad alone, with no plans to expand outside Gujarat.
- **Market position:** One of Ahmedabad's largest land banks (518 acres, ~36-40 msf development potential). Strong brand recall in premium/semi-premium segments.
- **Industry backdrop:** Ahmedabad = ~42% of Gujarat's total real estate investments. Multiple structural tailwinds — 2030 Commonwealth Games, GIFT City proximity (financial services hub), Metro Phase II expansion, Swachh Survekshan #1 clean city ranking. Residential sales grew ~15%+ in FY25. Commercial leasing surged ~60% YoY.
- **Differentiation vs peers:** Near-zero debt throughout the cycle, all land fully paid at historical cost, precast construction technology enabling 2-2.5 year project delivery (vs 4-5 year industry norm), Tishman Speyer partnership for Million Minds SEZ (global credibility for tenant attraction).
- **Scale context:** FY26 revenue at Rs 511 Cr is dwarfed by peers (DLF Rs 1,814 Cr, Lodha Rs 4,714 Cr quarterly). Ganesh trades at P/E 21.5x — the cheapest among the peer set but also the smallest by market cap.

## Management Track Record

### Hits
- **Turnaround from near-insolvency (FY20-21):** PAT swung from -Rs 120 Cr (FY20) to +Rs 598 Cr (FY25). Debt reduced by ~Rs 650 Cr since FY19. Interest cost collapsed from Rs 80 Cr (FY20) to Rs 4 Cr (FY26). This is a genuinely impressive deleveraging and execution story.
- **On-time/ahead-of-schedule delivery:** Malabar Exotica completed 10 months ahead of schedule using precast tech. Malabar County 3 delivered in 2.5 years. Recognised by ICI as "India's Top Tech Structure 2023."
- **Million Minds Phase 1 completed on time:** Inaugurated in May 2026, within the guided completion window. Partnership with Tishman Speyer executed well.

### Misses
- **FY26 guidance miss (big one):** At Q4 FY25 call (May 2025), management guided 20-30% YoY PAT growth for FY26. At Q1 FY26 (Jul 2025), reiterated 30% PAT CAGR. By Q2 FY26 (Nov 2025), downgraded to "maintain FY25 levels." Actual FY26 PAT: Rs 316 Cr — a 47% decline YoY. This is a significant credibility dent.
- **One 91 Thaltej (INR 2,100 Cr GDV) — repeated delays:** Originally expected construction start post-monsoon 2025 (Q2 FY26), then H2 FY26 (Nov 2025), then "March 2026" (Feb 2026). At Q4 FY26 call (Jun 2026), pushed to Q2 FY27. ~12-15 month cumulative delay. Management justifies as "strategic patience" to optimise product configuration — but competitors launch faster.
- **Malabar Retreat bookings (slow ramp):** At Q4 FY25 (May 2025): 10-15% booked, target 80% by FY26 end. At Q1 FY26 (Jul 2025): ~30% booked. At Q2 FY26 (Nov 2025): 35-40% booked. At Q3 FY26 (Feb 2026): ~35-37%. At Q4 FY26 (Jun 2026): ~35-37% (no major progress). Still well below the 80% target with project at 79% construction completion.
- **Godhavi Phase 1 (50 acres) — protracted monetisation:** At Q4 FY25: 18 acres sold. At Q2 FY26: 33 acres. At Q3 FY26: 38 acres. At Q4 FY26: 46 acres. After 18 months, Phase 1 is still not fully monetised. Management declined to give completion timeline for the balance.
- **Dividend cut:** 110% in FY23, 50% in FY24, 20% in FY25, 15% in FY26. Cash conserved for growth — defensible, but returning shareholders are seeing diminishing payouts despite stated high profitability.

### Key Financial Observations

1. **FY26 was a "transition year" (management's words) — and the numbers show it.** Revenue halved (-47% YoY), PAT halved, ROE halved from 29% to 14%, ROCE more than halved from 44% to 19%. CWIP jumped to Rs 540 Cr (+50% YoY) as capex on Million Minds and One 91 Thaltej ramp up. This is the trough of a project cycle.

2. **Margins remain extraordinary (83% OPM, 59% PATM) despite revenue contraction.** The high OPM is real estate project-mix driven — land acquired at historical cost decades ago, on which premium projects are now being sold/recognised. The question is whether 80%+ OPM is sustainable as new land is acquired at market prices.

3. **CFO/FCF deterioration:** FY26 CFO at Rs 213 Cr (vs Rs 410 Cr in FY25). FCF barely positive at Rs 30 Cr (vs Rs 193 Cr in FY25). Working capital absorbed Rs 201 Cr. Heavy investment outflows of Rs 543 Cr — capex on Million Minds + land advances. This is a deliberate investment phase but worth monitoring.

4. **Debt ticked up:** Borrowings rose from Rs 248 Cr to Rs 305 Cr in FY26 — Rs 150 Cr LRD (Lease Rental Discounting) against Million Minds LOIs taken in late March 2026, plus Rs 149 Cr unsecured loans from group entities. Management maintains this is "moderate" and gearing is "extremely comfortable." D/E at 0.13x is indeed low, but the direction is worth noting given the historical "zero debt" positioning.

5. **Balance sheet strengthening continues:** Net worth crossed Rs 2,300 Cr (up from Rs 789 Cr in FY22). BVPS Rs 280 — stock trades at 2.9x book. All land fully paid, unencumbered. Even with FY26 borrowings, net debt position is near-zero.

## Strategy, Growth Outlook & Management Quality

### Bull Case Thesis

1. **Embedded land bank value:** 518 acres of fully paid land in Ahmedabad's prime growth corridors gives Ganesh a multi-decade development runway. At current market rates, the land bank is worth multiples of the Rs 6,793 Cr market cap. The pipeline of ~32 msf with ~Rs 17,250 Cr sales value over 10-15 years provides massive optionality.

2. **Million Minds annuity kicker:** Phase 1 (0.85 msf) at Rs 75+ Cr p.a. lease rental is a trial run. If leasing traction holds, 5-7 phases on the 65-acre campus could generate Rs 500+ Cr p.a. in annuity income — a genuine re-rating catalyst. REIT/spin-off potential in ~2 years.

3. **FY27 recovery thesis:** Three revenue pillars converge — (a) Million Minds lease income from Q3 FY27, (b) Malabar Retreat completion + ~Rs 450-500 Cr revenue recognition, (c) continued Godhavi land monetisation. Management guided FY27 to be "a very different year" from FY26.

4. **Structural Ahmedabad tailwinds:** Commonwealth Games 2030, GIFT City proximity, Metro Phase II, and migration trends support multi-year demand. Ahmedabad remains India's most affordable metro housing market (EMI-to-income ~18%).

5. **Balance sheet resilience:** Near-zero debt through the cycle is rare in Indian real estate. Even at FY26's elevated borrowings (Rs 305 Cr), D/E is 0.13x — negligible. This provides through-cycle resilience and firepower for opportunistic land acquisition.

### Bear Case Thesis

1. **FY26 credibility gap:** 30% PAT growth guidance → 47% PAT decline is a major miss. The "strategic patience" narrative for delays (One 91, Godhavi) may mask execution challenges. Competitors are launching faster.

2. **Revenue concentration and lumpiness:** 100% Ahmedabad. Revenue recognition on project completion creates multi-quarter dry spells. No visibility on FY27 revenue guidance yet — promised in Q1 FY27 call (late July 2026).

3. **Margin sustainability:** 83% OPM is unsustainable at scale. As the company acquires new land at market prices (management is actively acquiring) and shifts mix towards development (37-40% margins) vs land monetisation (80%+ margins), blended OPM will compress materially. Management acknowledged this.

4. **Malabar Retreat slow bookings:** At 79% construction complete but only ~35-37% sold, there is inventory risk if demand doesn't pick up post-completion. The ~INR 300 Cr unsold portion represents execution risk for FY27 revenue.

5. **Management governance flags:** Chairman and MD/CEO since 1991/1994. 73.06% promoter holding. Limited institutional investor engagement (FII = 0.63%, DII = 0.33%). SEBI clarification on related-party disclosures in FY25. Amalgamation of a promoter-owned subsidiary (GatIL Properties) — while framed as simplification, such transactions merit scrutiny.

6. **Peer undervaluation:** At P/E 21.5x, Ganesh appears cheap vs peers (DLF 38.6x, Lodha 34.5x). But FY26 EPS (Rs 37.93) is an earnings trough — forward P/E depends on FY27 recovery. BV at 2.9x is not particularly cheap for a single-city developer.

## Risks Including Credit Rating Trajectory

1. **Execution risk on ~32 msf pipeline:** The planned pipeline (30.1 msf) is larger than everything delivered over 30 years (~23 msf). Delays on One 91 Thaltej and Godhavi Phase 1 suggest the company may struggle to launch and deliver at the pace communicated.

2. **Credit rating trajectory:** The most recent credit rating on screener.in is from brickwork (March 2023) — dated. No recent CRISIL/ICRA/CARE ratings visible. The company's near-zero debt position means formal credit ratings may not be actively sought. However, the recent Rs 150 Cr LRD facility and Rs 149 Cr unsecured loans mark a shift from the "zero debt" philosophy. If borrowings continue to rise for capex, ratings will become more relevant.

3. **Single-city concentration risk:** Any Ahmedabad-specific shock (policy, earthquake, economic slowdown) has disproportionate impact. Competitor national developers (Godrej, Prestige, Lodha) entering Ahmedabad post-Commonwealth Games could erode Ganesh's local dominance.

4. **Commercial leasing execution:** Million Minds Phase 1 leasing at 60-65% (LOI stage, non-binding) — conversion to binding leases depends on Building Use certificate issuance. Tenant names undisclosed (NDA). Annuity income of Rs 75 Cr p.a. is meaningful but not yet proven.

5. **Land acquisition at market prices:** As Ganesh replenishes land bank (management targets 5-10% annual addition, ~25-50 acres/year), new parcels will be bought at current market prices — eroding the "low-cost land" margin advantage over time. Q4 FY26 saw 7 acres acquired; INR 200+ Cr in land advances outstanding.

6. **Succession risk:** Deep reliance on Chairman Dipak Patel (land/government liaison) and MD Shekhar Patel (day-to-day operations) — both associated since early 1990s. No succession plan discussed publicly.

7. **Regulatory risks:** SEZ policy changes could affect Million Minds attractiveness. TDR policy (inter-city) not yet formally notified. RERA compliance costs rising.

8. **Dividend policy** — payout has shrunk from 110% to 15% over 4 years. Any increase would signal management confidence in cash flow normalisation.

## Summary

Ganesh Housing is a debt-free, land-bank-rich Ahmedabad real estate developer with a genuinely impressive turnaround over FY22-25, industry-leading margins, and a multi-decade project pipeline. The FY26 earnings trough (-47% PAT) was driven by a project cycle transition — heavy capex into Million Minds and One 91 Thaltej while recognising minimal project completions. FY27 should see a recovery driven by Million Minds annuity income, Malabar Retreat completion, and continued land monetisation.

**The key debate is credibility vs. value.** Management's FY26 guidance miss (30% growth → 47% decline) and repeated project launch delays erode confidence. However, the embedded land bank value, near-zero debt, and structural Ahmedabad tailwinds provide a floor. At 21.5x trailing P/E (trough earnings) and 2.9x book, the stock is cheaper than peers — but reflects a rational discount for single-city concentration, limited institutional interest, and execution uncertainty.

**Verdict:** Worth tracking closely for the FY27 recovery thesis. Q1 FY27 results (due late July 2026) will be the make-or-break catalyst — specifically, FY27 revenue/PAT guidance, One 91 Thaltej launch timeline, and Million Minds lease conversion status. The business quality (land bank, balance sheet, margins) is strong; the question is whether management can translate it into consistent, guided financial performance.
