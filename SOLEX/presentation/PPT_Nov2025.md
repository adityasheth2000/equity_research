## Page 23

**Slide Topic: Strong Policy Tailwinds for Solar Sector (2/2)**

**Regulatory Framework & Policy Changes**
*   **Domestic Content Requirements (DCR):** Mandates that a certain percentage of materials used in solar projects must be sourced from domestic manufacturers.
*   **Approved List of Models and Manufacturers (ALMM):**
    *   **Objective:** Ensures the use of high-quality solar modules in government-approved projects.
    *   **Status:** Reimposed from **April 1, 2024**.
*   **Approved List of Cell Manufacturers (ALCM):**
    *   **Objective:** Ensures the use of high-quality solar cells in government-approved projects.
    *   **Status:** Expected to be implemented from **June 2026**.

**Company Positioning & Strategic Initiatives (Solex)**
*   **Competitive Advantage:** The company (Solex) has the **highest number of modules registered on the ALMM**.
*   **Strategic Expansion:** The company is "foraying into Solar Cell Manufacturing by **FY27**".

**Benefits of ALMM & ALCM Compliance (for Solar Module & Cell manufacturing companies)**
*   Quality Assurance & Cost Competitiveness.
*   Access to significant markets like Government tenders & projects.
*   Enhances consumer trust.
*   Compliant with regulatory standards, reducing the risk of penalties.
*   Promotes Domestic Manufacturing (economic growth).
*   Aligns with National & Global sustainability goals.

**Risk Factors & Regulatory Outlook**
*   **Capacity Gap:** India currently lacks sufficient cell manufacturing capacity.
*   **Implementation Risk:** If domestic capacity remains inadequate, the implementation of the ALCM program may be extended beyond the current target of June 2026.

*   **BIS (Bureau of Indian Standards) Certification:** Confirms that solar modules meet national quality standards in India.
*   **IEC certifications:** Serve as global benchmarks confirming the safety, reliability, and environmental resilience of PV modules under stress conditions.
*   **ISO & OHSAS Certifications:** Indicates adherence to rigorous standards in quality, environmental responsibility, and occupational health and safety.
