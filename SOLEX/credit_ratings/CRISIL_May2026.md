## Detailed Rationale

Crisil Ratings is monitoring the financial impact of a new Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) entered into by Solex Energy Limited (SEL) with the Government of Gujarat on May 1, 2026. Under this agreement, SEL plans to establish a 5 Gigawatt (GW) solar cell manufacturing plant and a 10 GW Battery Energy Storage System (BESS) facility in Gujarat. The total estimated project cost is Rs 4,000 crore, to be executed in a phased manner over the next 4-5 years.

Crisil believes that the planned capital expenditure (capex) is currently at a very nascent stage. While the initiative represents a strategic growth move, the agency will continue to monitor the potential impact on SEL's business scale and financial risk profiles. Appropriate need-based rating action will be taken in the future if warranted by the progression of the project and its financial implications.

*Note: Specific weaknesses were not detailed in this update bulletin, but the following risk factors associated with the announcement are noted:*

*   **Nascent Capex Stage:** Current stage offers limited data on project realization, making financial metrics difficult to assess immediately.
