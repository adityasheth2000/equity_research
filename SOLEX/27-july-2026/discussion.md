## Right Price to Buy — The Math

### Step 1: Normalize Earnings

FY26 PAT of Rs 98 Cr is inflated by a blowout Q4 (Rs 59 Cr — 60% of full year PAT from one quarter). That quarter had Lines 3 & 4 hitting 81% utilization in March — a seasonal peak that won't repeat every quarter.

| Quarter | PAT (Cr) | Context |
|---|---|---|
| Q1FY26 | 25 | Seasonally moderate |
| Q2FY26 | 6 | Monsoon; site delays |
| Q3FY26 | 9 | Ramp-up costs of new lines |
| Q4FY26 | 59 | Peak utilization + seasonal tailwind |

Normalized annual PAT: **Rs 70-80 Cr** (EPS Rs 64-73). This strips out the seasonal blowout and assumes a more even quarterly distribution as Lines 3 & 4 stabilize.

### Step 2: What Multiple is Fair?

| Factor | Direction |
|---|---|
| 11% EBITDA margin, 6% PAT margin — below-industry average | Discount |
| Product is commoditized modules — no technology moat | Discount |
| Rs 1,050 Cr capex ahead, mostly debt → FCF destruction for 18-24 months | Discount |
| Management misses revenue guidance 3 of last 3 years | Discount |
| CRISIL BBB/Stable — lowest investment grade, now "under monitoring" | Discount |
| No institutional ownership (DII 0.5%, FII 0.07%) | Discount |
| Regulatory moat — ALMM, BCD, proposed ALCM | Modest premium |

A fair P/E for this profile is **8-10x** on normalized earnings. Not 15-20x.

### Step 4: Stress Test the Buy Zone

At Rs 550-650, even if:
- FY27 PAT comes in at Rs 100 Cr (vs Rs 156-208 Cr guided) → **5.5-6.5x P/E**
- Cell line delays by 6 months → earnings stay flat → **still 8-9x P/E**
- Margins compress to 5% PAT → Rs 80 Cr PAT → **still 7-8x P/E**
- CRISIL downgrades to BBB- → cost of debt rises, but equity is cheap enough to absorb

At Rs 940, even a minor FY27 miss means you're paying 15-17x on actual earnings. No margin of safety.

### Why Rs 940 Isn't the Bottom

The stock is down 35% in the last year, below all SMAs, RSI at 29.7 (oversold). In a risk-off market or if FY27 H1 disappoints (very possible — monsoon quarters are structurally weak for this business), the 52-week low of Rs 795 is breakable. Retail shareholders have grown from 567 to 10,382 — this is panic-prone money that capitulates on bad news.

**The right price to buy is Rs 550-650.** At that level you're getting a 38% ROE business at 2.5x book, with a cell manufacturing optionality thrown in for free. You need patience — this price may come after one more guidance miss, a failed QIP, or general market weakness.
