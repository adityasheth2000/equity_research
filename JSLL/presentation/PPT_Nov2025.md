## Page 21

**Topic: Unique Business Model Positioning / JSLL’s Flywheel Effect**

The slide outlines the company's strategic "Flywheel Effect," a self-reinforcing cycle designed to drive higher growth and better Return on Capital Employed (ROCE). The operational logic flows as follows:

*   **Core Driver:** Quality services and effective treatment result in satisfied patients.
*   **Marketing & Scale:** Satisfied patients generate word-of-mouth marketing, which attracts more patient volumes. Higher volumes allow for larger-scale operations.
*   **Cost Efficiency:** Increased scale and efficiency enable cost-effective treatments.
*   **Financial Impact:** Cost efficiencies contribute to Better ROCE.
*   **Expansion Loop:** Improved financials and brand reputation support the opening of more facilities and hiring of better doctors, further increasing operating efficiencies and restarting the cycle toward Higher Growth.
