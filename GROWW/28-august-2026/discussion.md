## 1. Revenue contribution by segment

Groww discloses mix as **% of Total Income** (not ₹ amounts per segment). Q1 FY27 total income = **₹1,549 Cr** (revenue from ops ₹1,501 Cr). The ₹ figures below are approximations (mix % × ₹1,549 Cr).

| Segment | Q1 FY27 mix | ≈ ₹ Cr/qtr | Trend (Q2 FY25 → Q1 FY27) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Equity derivatives (F&O) | 52.0% | ~₹806 | 68% → 52% (falling) |
| Stocks (cash equity) | 16.4% | ~₹254 | 15% → 16–19% (stable) |
| Commodity derivatives | 8.1% | ~₹125 | 0% → 8% (new, fast ramp) |
| Float income | 8.0% | ~₹124 | 3% → 8% (rising) |
| MTF (margin trading) | 5.5% | ~₹85 | 1% → 5.5% (rising) |
| Personal loans + LAS | 3.0% | ~₹46 | 5% → 3% (LAS scaling) |
| Treasury | 2.1% | ~₹33 | ~3–5% (lumpy) |
| Other (Fisdom + AMC + wealth) | <2% | ~₹30 | new / negligible |

**Key concentration point:** Broking (F&O + stocks + commodities) ≈ **76%** of income; the broking subsidiary GIT = **~94%** of group revenue. Float + MTF + PL/LAS (the recurring/balance-sheet lines) ≈ **16.5%** and are the fastest-growing contributors.

## 2. Growth strategy & expectations per segment

| Segment | Strategy | Growth expectation (management's words) |
|---|---|---|
| **Equity derivatives** | *Deliberately de-emphasise.* Protect share, don't chase volume; harvest profitability from existing users. | Mix to fall **below 50%** (toward ~45%) as other lines grow. Growth purely volatility-linked; no incremental push. |
| **Stocks (cash)** | Grow market share via penetration + rising ticket size; MTF attaches onto cash users. | Cash ADTO share 11.8% → **15.1%** YoY; incremental share already exceeds average share, so share "can keep growing." |
| **Commodities** | New S-curve (launched Sep 2025); replicate the F&O playbook. | Already ~28% notional ADTO retail share; expects "fair share **sooner than MTF**" (transaction, not book, business). |
| **Float income** | Passive — earns on idle client cash; benefit from rate/yield tailwind. | Yields to rise **+1–2pp/quarter** as MTF penetration grows; near-pure margin, annuity-like. |
| **MTF** | Scale the book; experience (not price) as moat vs 7.99% competitors. | **"Double-digit market share in ~3 years"** (vs 2.7% today); adding **₹600–700 Cr/quarter**; yield ~14.95%. |
| **Personal loans + LAS** | Shift mix toward **secured (LAS)**; grow the NBFC book profitably. | LAS now **34% of disbursements** (from ~0); "good compounding business" — no hard target, credit cost is the watch item. |
| **AMC (Groww AMC)** | Build passive/global products; leverage **State Street** (~23% stake, ~₹580 Cr) for capability. | AUM needs to grow **5–6x** (from ₹5,491 Cr) to reach profitability; no numerical target — framed as a multi-year build. |
| **Wealth (Fisdom/W/Prime)** | Monetise the captive ~13M-client base (near-zero CAC); bank partnerships + HNI advisory. | Fisdom **profitable by FY28**; combined contribution today <2% of revenue — the "mammoth" optionality. |

**Cross-cutting expectations (from concalls):**
- Overall margin expansion is explicitly tied to **revenue growth >15%** (below that, ~90% fixed costs stop expanding margin).
- Cost-to-grow (marketing) held at **₹450–500 Cr/yr**; headcount flat (~1,800) — growth is meant to come from ARPU/cross-sell, not acquisition spend.
- The whole strategy is a **rotation out of cyclical F&O (~52%) into recurring float + MTF + lending + AMC/wealth** — that mix shift, not top-line speed, is the investment thesis.

## 3. Management guidance — revenue / EBITDA / PAT growth targets

**No. Management deliberately gives no numerical revenue, EBITDA, or PAT targets** — stated explicitly and consistently across all four concalls.

### Explicit refusals
- **EBITDA as a target** (Ishan Bansal, Q3 FY26): *"we don't look at EBITDA as like a metric that we want to drive. This is more of an output."*
- **Revenue/AUM/wealth targets** (Lalit Keshre, Q1 FY27): *"it's a bit early to give those."*
- **No dividend** in the near future (growth phase).
- Gave **no forward numerical guidance** at all in any quarter ("difficult to predict," "difficult to project").

### The only "guidance" is directional — the margin framework

> "If our revenue grows beyond ~15% then the margin will keep expanding; if it grows 30% the expansion will be more; if it grows slightly lower, then margin will not expand." — Ishan Bansal (Q4 FY26 call)

This is the single most useful statement they've made on the P&L — it anchors margin to a **~15% revenue-growth threshold**, driven by ~90% fixed / 10% variable cost structure.

### The few concrete commitments they *have* given (all segment-level, not company-level)

| Item | Target |
|---|---|
| MTF book | +₹600–700 Cr/quarter, on-going |
| MTF market share | "double-digit" in ~3 years (vs 2.7%) |
| Fixed cost growth | +10–20% p.a. |
| Marketing (cost-to-grow) | held ~₹450–500 Cr/yr |
| Fisdom | profitable by FY28 |
| Headcount | no big increase (~1,800 flat) |
| Cash/float yield | +1–2pp/quarter improvement |

### Bottom line

You cannot model Groww off guidance — management views it as anti-target ("output, not driver"). The market's ~50x multiple is therefore built on **actual quarterly delivery** (revenue >15%, derivatives share falling, MTF/LAS scaling), not on any committed script. That's why the "~15% revenue growth" line matters so much — it's the only quantitative anchor they've offered.
