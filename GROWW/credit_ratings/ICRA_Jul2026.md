## Rationale for Upgrade
- **Sustained market leadership:** GIT has been the **leading player in securities broking for the last 11 quarters (since Q2 FY2024)**; NSE active-client **market share ~29% as of Jun 2026** (vs ~27% Oct 2025).
- **Healthy profitability:** Operating profitability on an improving trajectory despite one-off items and regulatory changes in broking.
- **Strong capitalisation + liquidity:** Augmented by the **$202M Series F** (Jun 2025) and net **IPO proceeds of Rs 1,060 Cr** (post Rs 44 Cr expenses, Nov 2025); consolidated tangible net worth ~Rs 7,530 Cr; negligible borrowings.
- **Strong parentage:** GIT is a wholly-owned subsidiary of BGV (now listed, IPO Nov 2025).

## Outlook
- **Stable:** Expectation that GIT will maintain strong market position in equity broking, supporting healthy profitability and comfortable capitalisation.

## Key Monitorables (per ICRA)
- Ability to **scale new businesses (MTF, lending, AMC) meaningfully and profitably** — key for medium-term consolidated profitability.
- Ability to maintain **prudent credit costs across the lending business**.
- High dependence on capital markets remains the key constraint to profitability.
