# ICRA Rating — Billionbrains Garage Ventures Ltd (Groww Group)

**Date:** November 28, 2025
**Action:** Issuer rating **reaffirmed** at **[ICRA]AA- (Stable)**

## Rating Action
| Instrument | Previous Rated Amount (Rs Cr) | Current Rated Amount (Rs Cr) | Rating Action |
|---|---|---|---|
| Issuer rating | - | - | [ICRA]AA- (Stable); reaffirmed |

## Group Ratings (Nov 2025)
- **BGV (holding co / issuer):** [ICRA]AA- (Stable) — reaffirmed
- **Groww Invest Tech (GIT; broking):** [ICRA]AA- (Stable) / [ICRA]A1+
- **Groww CreditServ (GCS; NBFC arm):** [ICRA]A (Stable) / [ICRA]A1

## Key Drivers — Strengths
- **Market leadership:** GIT is the leading discount broker with the **highest NSE active-client market share since Q2 FY2024** (~27% as of Oct 2025).
- **Healthy capitalisation:** Consolidated net worth improved to **Rs 5,676.9 Cr (Jun 30, 2025)** from Rs 4,536.8 Cr (Mar 2025) and Rs 2,224.0 Cr (Mar 2024); supported by the **$202M Series F** raise in H1 FY2026.
- **Negligible borrowings** as of Jun 30, 2025; cumulative equity raised ~Rs 1,900 Cr till FY2024; no accumulated losses.
- **Strong liquidity:** Unencumbered cash + liquid investments of **over Rs 3,016 Cr** (Jun 2025, consolidated).

## Key Drivers — Weaknesses
- High dependence on capital-market-linked (broking) revenues.
- New businesses (MTF, lending, AMC) yet to contribute meaningfully to profitability.
- Evolving regulatory landscape (SEBI F&O measures, RBI capital-market lending directions).

## Capital Deployment (Mar 31, 2025 equity investments)
- Rs 568.9 Cr in GIT; Rs 678.2 Cr in GCS; Rs 349.5 Cr in Groww Asset Management.

## Liquidity
- Unencumbered cash + liquid investments ~Rs 3,016 Cr (Jun 2025).
- Requirements largely limited to broking margin buffers, MTF book scale-up, platform working capital, and GCS growth.
- Fresh IPO issue of **Rs 1,060 Cr (gross)** expected to support near-term growth capital.

## Company & Subsidiaries (ownership as on Jun 30, 2025)
- BGV (holding co), GIT, Groww AMC, Groww Trustee, Groww Pay Services, Groww Wealth Tech, Groww Serv, Neobillion Fintech, Billionblocks Finserv, Groww Insurance Broking (all 100%); Groww IFSC (step-down, 100%); Saafe Fintech (associate, 34.66%); GCS (subsidiary, limited consolidation).
