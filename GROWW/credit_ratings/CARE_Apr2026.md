## Page 4

**Page Title: Susceptibility towards regulatory changes / Liquidity Assessment**

**Specific Risks Flagged:**
*   **Regulatory Changes (SEBI):** Continuous revisions in the capital market industry. Specific measures introduced in October 2024 effective between Nov 2024 and April 2025 include:
    *   Reduction in derivatives with weekly expiry (highest F&O contributor).
    *   Increase in lot size.
    *   Increase in extreme loss margin by 2% on short options contracts on expiry day.
    *   Upfront collection of option premiums.
    *   Removal of Calendar Spread Treatment on Expiry Day.
*   **Regulatory Changes (RBI):** Amended directions dated Feb 13, 2026 (effective July 1, 2026) governing bank credit to capital market intermediaries. Mandates include:
    *   Fully collateralized lending.
    *   Higher collateral quality (significant cash components).
    *   Minimum haircuts on equity securities.
    *   Restriction on bank funding for proprietary trading.
    *   Margin trading facilities backed by high-quality liquid collateral.
    *   Stricter monitoring and concentration limits on exposures.
    *   **Impact:** Expected to increase funding costs and necessitate stronger capitalization/liquidity management.

**Business Risk Profile Assessment:**
*   **Revenue Concentration:** About 75% of BGV’s revenue in Q3FY26 came from brokerage, exposing earnings to stock market volatility and trading volumes.
*   **Diversification Trend:** Share of brokerage income declined from 81% a year back (Q3FY25), indicating gradual moderation in revenue concentration.
*   **Competitive Landscape:** Operates in a highly competitive brokerage space with consistently competitive rates. Ability to maintain market share is a key monitorable.
*   **Emerging Businesses:** Initiatives to support revenue diversification are at an early stage with a gestation period before scaling up. Pace of diversification and profitability remains key monitorable.

**Financial Risk Profile Assessment (Liquidity):**
*   **Assessment:** Strong / Comfortable.
*   **Free Cash Balances:** ₹385 crore.
*   **Liquid Investments:** ₹632 crore (in mutual funds).
*   **Total Fund-based Borrowings:** ₹269 crore (as on December 31, 2025).
*   **Additional Support:** Surplus margins maintained with exchanges provide further liquidity support in exigencies.
