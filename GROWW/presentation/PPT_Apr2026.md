**Glossary – Product Metrics Definitions (Groww Shareholders’ Letter – Q4 FY26)**

This page defines key business metrics used by Groww to measure platform activity and user engagement. These are critical for equity analysts evaluating growth, monetization potential, and product penetration in a fintech/discount broker model.

---

**KPIs & Definitions Table:**

| KPI                          | Definition                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
|------------------------------|------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **MF Active Users**          | Unique users who qualified as “New Transacting User” AND have either: <br> • Transacted in Mutual Funds in last 3 months of reporting period, OR<br> • Have MF holdings value > ₹0 on last day of reporting period.       |
| **MF SIP Inflows**           | Total value of Systematic Investment Plans (SIPs) purchased through Groww’s platform during the reported period.                                                                                    |
| **Stocks Active Users**      | Unique users who qualified as “New Transacting User” AND have either: <br> • Transacted in Stocks, IPO, or MTF products in last 3 months, OR<br> • Hold stocks with value > ₹0 in demat account on last day.         |
| **Average Daily Turnover**   | Cumulative turnover in Stocks during period ÷ total number of trading days in reporting period.                                                                                                          |
| **Equity Derivatives Active Users** | Unique users who qualified as “New Transacting User” AND transacted in Equity Derivatives on platform in last 3 months of reporting period.                                                     |
| **Average Daily Premium Turnover** | Cumulative turnover in Equity Derivatives (notional for futures + premium for options) ÷ total number of trading days in reporting period.                                                           |
| **Commodity Derivatives Active Users** | Unique users who qualified as “New Transacting User” AND transacted in Commodity Derivatives on platform in last 3 months of reporting period.                                                 |
| **MTF Book**                 | Refers to “Loan - Margin Trading Facility”; i.e., outstanding (funded) book as on last day of reporting period.                                                                                        |

---

**Analyst Notes:**

- **“New Transacting User”** is a recurring qualifier — implies Groww tracks acquisition vs. reactivation. Analysts should look for how this definition evolved over time if historical comparisons are needed.
- **Active user definitions vary by segment** (MF, Stocks, Derivatives), reflecting different behavioral thresholds (transaction frequency vs. asset holding).
- **Turnover metrics** (Average Daily Turnover / Premium Turnover) are essential for estimating revenue from brokerage fees, exchange charges, and STT — especially since discount brokers often rely on volume-based income.
- **MTF Book** indicates leverage exposure; important for assessing credit risk and capital adequacy under regulatory frameworks (e.g., SEBI margin norms).
- **SIP Inflows** reflect sticky, recurring AUM — key for long-term valuation multiples compared to one-off lumpsum inflows.

These definitions form the basis for interpreting growth trends, user stickiness, and monetization efficiency in subsequent slides.
