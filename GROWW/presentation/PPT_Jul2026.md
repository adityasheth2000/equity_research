**Page Title: Glossary (KPIs and Financial Metrics Definitions)**

This page provides definitions for key performance indicators (KPIs) and financial metrics used by the company. While it contains no actual numerical data, these definitions are critical for an equity analyst to correctly interpret future financial reports and operational updates from this issuer.

**1. Platform Metrics (Business Metrics)**

*   **Total Transacting Users:** Defined as unique new users who either deposited/invested ₹1,000 or more and transacted, OR took a loan on the platform for the first time.
    *   *Equity Note:* This metric appears to measure net-new user acquisition rather than total user base.
*   **Active Users:** Unique users who have transacted in the last three months OR hold total assets > ₹0. A subset of Total Transacting Users.
*   **Total Customer Assets:** Sum of (a) Stock value in demat accounts, (b) Mutual Fund holdings invested through Groww, and (c) Customer funds held on the platform.

**2. Financial Metrics**

The following table summarizes the company's specific definitions for its cost structures and profitability ratios, which deviate slightly from standard GAAP/IFRS interpretations:

| Metric | Definition / Composition |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Revenue from Operations** | As presented in financial statements. |
| **Cost to Serve** | Software, server & technology costs + Transaction & related charges. |
| **Cost to Grow** | Marketing & business promotion expense. |
| **Cost to Operate** | Total expenses excluding finance costs, D&A, marketing/promotion, software/server tech, and transaction charges. |
| **Contribution Margin** | Revenue from Ops minus Software/Server/Tech expenses and Transaction charges. <br>*(Excludes employee benefits and other expenses).* |
| **Contribution Margin (%)** | Contribution Margin as a percentage of Revenue from Operations. |
| **Total Income** | As presented in financial statements. |
| **EBITDA** | Profit for period/year + Tax + Finance Costs + D&A + Share of net loss of associate (net of tax) - Other income. |
| **EBITDA Margin** | EBITDA as a percentage of Revenue from Operations. |
| **Profit for the period / year** | As presented in financial statements. |
| **Profit for the period / year Margin** | Profit for the period/year as a percentage of Total Income. |

**Glossary - KPIs and Definitions (Q1 FY27)**

This page defines the key performance indicators (KPIs) used to measure business growth and operational scale. These metrics are critical for evaluating user engagement, asset accumulation, trading activity, and lending operations.

| Metric | Definition / Equity Research Relevance |
| :--- | :--- |
| **MF Assets held on Groww** | Value of Mutual Fund holdings as of the last day of the reporting period for all users. *Proxy for AUM accumulation.* |
| **MF SIP Inflows** | Total value of Systematic Investment Plans purchased through the platform during the reporting period. *Key metric for recurring revenue visibility and stickiness.* |
| **Stocks ADTO** | Average Daily Turnover in Stocks (Cumulative turnover / total number of trading days). *Indicator of equity trading volume and liquidity.* |
| **NSE Active Clients** | Customers who have executed at least one transaction on NSE within the last 12 months. *Standard definition for "Active" client base.* |
| **Equity Derivatives Premium ADTO** | Average Daily Turnover in Equity Derivatives (Notional turnover for futures + premium turnover for options). *Indicates sophisticated trader usage and potential fee income.* |
| **Commodity Derivatives Active Users** | Unique users qualified as "New Transacting User" transacting in Commodity Derivatives in the last 3 months. *Metric for new segment adoption.* |
| **Commodity Derivatives Notional ADTO** | Average Daily Notional Turnover in Commodity Derivatives. *Volume metric for commodity segment.* |
| **MTF Book** | Margin Trading Facility outstanding book as of the last day of the reporting period. *Direct proxy for interest-earning assets and credit risk exposure.* |
| **Disbursement by Groww CreditServ Technology** | Total amount of loans disbursed by NBFC subsidiary ("GCS") during the reporting period. *Growth metric for the lending arm.* |
