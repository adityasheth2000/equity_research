**Topic: Glossary of KPIs and Financial Metrics Definitions**

This page provides the specific definitions for key business and financial metrics used in Groww's reporting. Understanding these definitions is critical for accurate equity analysis, particularly regarding how "Cost to Serve" and "Adjusted EBITDA" are calculated versus standard accounting principles.

**Platform Metrics (KPIs)**
*   **Total Transacting Users:** Unique new users who deposited/invested ₹1,000+ and transacted, OR took a loan on the platform for the first time.
*   **Active Users:** Unique users with transactions in the last 3 months OR total assets > ₹0 as of the period end. (Subset of Total Transacting Users).
*   **NSE Active Clients:** Customers who executed at least one transaction on NSE within the last 12 months.
*   **Total Customer Assets:** Sum of (a) Stock value in demat accounts, (b) Mutual Fund holdings value, and (c) Cash funds held on the platform. Reported as of the last day of the period.

**Financial Metrics & Cost Definitions**
*   **Revenue from Operations:** As per Statement of Unaudited Consolidated Financial Results.
*   **Cost to Serve:** Software/Server/Tech costs + Transaction and related charges.
*   **Cost to Grow:** Marketing and business promotion expense.
*   **Cost to Operate:** Total expenses *excluding*: Finance costs, Depreciation/Amortization, Marketing, Tech costs, and Transaction charges.
*   **Adj. Cost to Operate:** Cost to Operate minus Share-based payments, One-time performance incentives, and Long-term incentives.
*   **EBITDA (excl. Other income):** Profit/(Loss) + Tax + Finance Costs + Depreciation/Amortization - Other Income.
*   **Adjusted EBITDA:** EBITDA + Exceptional items (taxes), Share-based payments, One-time performance incentive, Long-term incentive, and Share of net loss of associate (net of tax).
*   **Adjusted EBITDA Margin:** Adjusted EBITDA as a % of Revenue from operations.
*   **Profit / (loss) Margin:** Profit/(Loss) as a % of Total Income.

**Glossary - KPIs and Definitions**

This page defines key performance indicators (KPIs) used by Groww to measure business performance. These definitions are critical for interpreting financial results and operational metrics in equity analysis, particularly for a fintech platform with multiple revenue streams (Broking, Mutual Funds, MTF).

| Metric | Definition / Relevance to Equity Research |
| :--- | :--- |
| **MF Assets held on Groww** | Value of Mutual Fund holdings as of the last day of the reporting period. *Key metric for AUM growth and asset retention.* |
| **MF SIP Inflows** | Total value of Systematic Investment Plans purchased through the platform during the reported period. *Indicates recurring revenue potential and customer stickiness.* |
| **Broking Orders** | Total number of orders executed in Stocks and Derivatives during the reporting period. *Proxy for trading activity and transaction volume.* |
| **Stocks Active Users** | Unique users who qualify as "New Transacting User" and have either transacted in Stocks, IPO, or MTF products in the last three months OR hold >₹0 value in their demat account at Groww as of the last day. *Measures active user base and engagement.* |
| **Average Daily Turnover** | Cumulative turnover in Stocks divided by total trading days. *Standardized measure of trading liquidity and platform usage.* |
| **Derivatives Active Users** | Unique users who qualified as "New Transacting User" and have transacted in Derivatives in the last three months. *Tracks adoption of higher-margin derivative products.* |
| **Average Daily Premium Turnover** | Cumulative turnover in Derivatives (notional for futures + premium for options) divided by total trading days. *Specifically measures derivatives activity intensity.* |
| **Disbursement by Groww CreditServ Technology** | Total amount of loans disbursed by NBFC subsidiary (Groww Creditserv Technology Private Limited). *Critical for assessing credit book size and lending revenue.* |
| **MTF Active Users** | Unique users with an outstanding funded book as of the last day. *Measures users utilizing Margin Trading Facility — indicates leverage demand.* |
| **MTF Book** | Refers to “Loan - Margin Trading Facility” outstanding (funded) book per Unaudited Consolidated Financial Results. *Directly relates to balance sheet liability and interest income.* |
| **Cash Balance** | Includes Fixed Deposits, Investments in MFs, Securities including Government Securities and NCDs. *Reflects liquidity position and investment strategy of corporate cash.* |

These definitions provide clarity on how core business drivers are measured — essential for modeling future growth, assessing unit economics, and evaluating risk exposure in the margin financing and mutual fund distribution segments.
