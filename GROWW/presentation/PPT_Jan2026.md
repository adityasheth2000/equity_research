**Glossary – KPIs & Definitions (Groww Shareholders’ Letter – Q3 FY26)**

This page defines key performance indicators (KPIs) and financial metrics used in the report. While it contains no actual data, these definitions are critical for correctly interpreting subsequent financial and business metrics presented elsewhere in the deck. Extracted below are the definitions relevant to equity research analysis.

---

### Platform Metrics

- **Total Transacting Users**: Sum of unique new transacting users acquired as of end of reporting period on Groww platform. A ‘New Transacting User’ (“NTUs”) is defined as a user who has:
  - (i) deposited or invested ₹1,000 or more AND undertaken a transaction on our platform, OR
  - (ii) taken a loan on our platform for the first time.

- **Active Users**: Unique users that have transacted on Groww in the last three months OR have total assets greater than ₹0 as on the last day of the reporting period. Active Users are a subset of Total Transacting Users.

- **Total Customer Assets**: Sum of:
  - (a) value of Stocks held by users in demat accounts at Groww,
  - (b) value of Mutual Funds’ holdings of users invested through Groww,
  - (c) value of customer funds held by users on Groww platform.
  Reported as on the last day of the reporting period.

---

### Financial Metrics*

- **Revenue from Operations**: As presented in financials.

- **Cost to Serve**: Summation of:
  - (a) Software, server and technology costs
  - (b) Transaction and other related charges.

- **Cost to Grow**: Marketing and business promotion expense.

- **Cost to Operate**: Total expenses excluding:
  - (a) finance costs,
  - (b) depreciation and amortisation expense,
  - (c) marketing and business promotion expenses,
  - (d) software, server and technology expenses,
  - (e) transaction and other related charges.

- **Adj. Cost to Operate**: Cost to Operate minus share-based payments, one-time performance-based incentive, and long-term incentive.

- **Contribution Margin**: Revenue from Operations minus Software, server and technology expenses and Transaction and other related charges. Does not include attributable employee benefits expense and certain other expenses.

- **Contribution Margin (%)**: Contribution Margin as a percentage of Revenue from Operations.

- **Total Income**: As presented in the financials.

- **EBITDA (excluding Other income)**: Profit/(loss) for the period/year plus:
  - (i) Total tax expense,
  - (ii) Finance costs,
  - (iii) Depreciation and amortisation expense less Other income.

- **Adjusted EBITDA**: EBITDA plus:
  - (i) Exceptional item (taxes),
  - (ii) Share based payments,
  - (iii) One time performance based incentive,
  - (iv) Long term incentive,
  - (v) Share of net loss of associate accounted for using equity method (net of tax).

- **Adjusted EBITDA Margin**: Adjusted EBITDA as a percentage of Revenue from Operations.

- **Profit for the period / year**: As presented in the financials.

- **Profit for the period / year Margin**: Profit for the period / year as a percentage of Total Income.

---

**Glossary – KPI Definitions (Product Metrics)**

This page defines key business metrics used in the Groww Shareholders’ Letter for Q3 FY26. While it contains no actual performance data, these definitions are critical for equity analysts to interpret subsequent financial and operational disclosures accurately.

| KPI | Definition |
|-----|----------|
| **MF SIP Inflows** | Total value of Systematic Investment Plans (SIPs) purchased through the platform during the reported period. *Key indicator of retail investment traction and recurring revenue potential.* |
| **Average Daily Turnover** | Cumulative turnover in Stocks divided by total trading days in the reporting period. *Measures core brokerage activity volume.* |
| **Average Daily Premium Turnover** | Cumulative turnover in Equity Derivatives (notional for futures + premium for options) divided by total trading days. *Reflects derivatives engagement — higher-margin segment.* |
| **Commodity Derivatives Active Users** | Unique users qualified as “New Transacting User” who transacted in Commodity Derivatives on the platform in the last 3 months of the reporting period. *Tracks user acquisition/engagement in a high-growth vertical.* |
| **MTF Book** | Refers to “Loan - Margin Trading Facility”, i.e., MTF outstanding (funded) book as of the last day of the reporting period. *Critical balance sheet metric indicating leveraged lending exposure and interest income potential.* |

> Analyst Note: These metrics define how growth, monetization, and risk are measured. For example, “MTF Book” directly impacts capital adequacy and credit risk; “Premium Turnover” drives higher ASP per trade vs. cash equities. Tracking trends in these will be essential when reviewing future quarters.
