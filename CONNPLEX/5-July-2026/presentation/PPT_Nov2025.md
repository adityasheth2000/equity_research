## Page 9

**Slide Title: Transforming Cinema: Connplex's Scalable & Efficient Model**

**1. Lower Cost Structure & Higher Utilization (Lesser Seating Capacity)**
*   **Unit Economics:** The model focuses on high-grade viewing experiences rather than large seating capacity.
*   **Capex & Opex:** Lower capital expenditure (capex) and operational costs compared to larger multiplexes due to reduced space requirements.
*   **Break-even:** Easier to achieve a break-even point with a reduced audience size.
*   **Efficiency:** Higher utilization rates compared to larger multiplexes.
*   **Overheads:** Reduced maintenance and overhead expenses.

**2. Premium Product & F&B Strategy (Smart Seating Arrangement)**
*   **Seat Design:** Flexible layouts tailored to meet regional and urban audience-specific needs. Focus on comfort and premium experiences in smaller spaces.
*   **Food & Beverages (F&B):**
    *   Curated regional menus and premium gourmet selections.
    *   Efficient service design with customizable meal combos.
    *   Innovative beverage offerings to drive a superior dining experience.

**3. Growth Strategy & Geography (Strong Market Penetration)**
*   **Target Markets:** Strategic expansion into **Tier 2 and Tier 3 cities** where disposable income is growing.
*   **Localization:** Tapping into regional content trends and localized demand.
*   **Customer Acquisition:** Strategy focuses on attracting first-time movie-goers and building loyalty in niche markets.

**4. Business Model Structure (Asset Light Model)**
*   **Partnerships:** Utilizes franchise and vendor partnerships to expand operations efficiently.
*   **Capital Efficiency:** Optimized capital utilization reduces capital expenditure while ensuring scalability and profitability.

**5. Ancillary Revenue Streams (Group Booking & Advertising)**
*   **Event Monetization:** Offers customized event experiences including private shows, corporate events, and special gatherings.
*   **Advertising:**
    *   High-impact on-screen ads with immersive visuals and sound.
    *   Off-screen branding channels: Digital displays, standees, kiosks, F&B packaging, and online platforms.

**Slide Title:** Strategic Insights: Investment Rationale

**Business Model & Strategic Initiatives**
*   **Asset-Light Franchise Model:** The company utilizes an asset-light franchise model to minimize capital expenditure (Capex) while enabling rapid market penetration through ticket sales and diversified revenue streams.
*   **Scalability & Derisking:** The model features "smart seating capacity" and optimized operational costs, allowing for quicker break-even points and strong profitability to ensure sustainable growth.
*   **Premium Experience Strategy:** Strategic investments in cutting-edge technology, premium amenities, and sound/visual technologies are used to differentiate the product and drive high-quality financial performance.
*   **Market Expansion:** Expansion is driven by strategic entry into Tier 2, Tier 3, and Tier 4 cities (underpenetrated markets) via franchise partnerships.
*   **Unit Economics & Setup:** The cinema model is designed for rapid break-even through customized seating configurations and lean operating structures. A streamlined setup process and comprehensive support are provided to franchisees to enable quick launch and rapid ROI.
