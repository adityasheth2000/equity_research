## Page 17

**Slide Topic: Dual Franchise Models For Scalable Cinema Expansion**

**Strategic Business Model for Expansion**
The company employs a dual franchise structure to facilitate scalable growth.

*   **Franchise-Owned, Franchise-Operated (FOFO) Model**
    *   **Structure:** Franchisees own and manage the business operations.
    *   **Revenue Management:** All earnings (including box office, Food & Beverage, and ancillary services) are pooled into a centralized account.
    *   **Flexibility:** Provides local flexibility, allowing franchises to adapt operations to regional customer preferences.
    *   **Support:** Includes training, setup, and ongoing marketing assistance.

*   **Franchise-Owned, Company-Operated (FOCO) Model**
    *   **Structure:** Franchisees provide the investment capital, while the company handles operations to ensure consistency and efficiency. (Signifies reduced Capex burden on the company for this expansion model).
    *   **Revenue Management:** Centralized earnings with regular payouts to the franchisee after expenses are deducted.
    *   **Support:** Comprehensive support including training, setup, and continuous marketing assistance.
    *   **Marketing Access:** Integration of national campaigns with regional promotions.
