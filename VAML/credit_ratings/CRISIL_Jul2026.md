## Detailed Rationale

Crisil Ratings has upgraded VAML's long-term rating to **AA+** and assigned a **Stable** outlook, while removing it from 'Rating Watch with Developing Implications'. This decision reflects the successful resolution of the watch triggered by the demerger of Vedanta Ltd. The agency now has clarity on the support philosophy of the Vedanta group towards its entities post-demerger.

The credit profile is bolstered by VAML's dominant market position in the domestic aluminium industry, cost-efficient operations, and strong profitability. Financially, the company benefits from robust cash accruals and a net debt-to-Ebitda ratio expected to remain within rating thresholds (below 1.0–1.25 times) despite ongoing capital expenditure.

Crisil Ratings continues to fully consolidate Bharat Aluminium Company Ltd (BALCO) with VAML due to significant operational and strategic linkages. The group's financial flexibility has improved post-demerger, with the market value of VRL's shareholding covering ~5.6 times against net debt as of June 30, 2026. However, risks include sizeable planned capex nearing completion and susceptibility to commodity price volatility. Additionally, while the Enforcement Directorate (ED) searches regarding FEMA breaches have not yet resulted in material implications, adverse developments remain a key sensitivity factor.

---

## Key Rating Drivers — Strengths

*   **Dominant Market Position:** VAML is the largest domestic producer of aluminium (48% market share in FY26) and the third-largest globally (excluding China). Total smelting capacity stands at ~2.8 MTPA (including BALCO), more than double that of the second-largest producer, with plans to reach 3 MTPA by FY28 via debottlenecking.
*   **Low-Cost Operations:** The company operates in the first quartile of global cost curves. Cost of production decreased to **$1,848/tonne** in FY26 from $1,915/tonne in FY25, driven by increased captive alumina availability (Lanjigarh refinery expansion to 5 MTPA), improved linkage coal utilization, and lower coal prices.
*   **Strong Profitability & Growth:** EBITDA surged 43% year-on-year to **Rs. 25,208 crore** in FY26. Profitability is expected to improve further in FY27 driven by volume growth (capacity ramp-up to 2.8 MTPA) and higher value-added product (VAP) mix. EBITDA per tonne is expected to sustain above **$1,300/tonne** in FY27.
*   **Robust Financial Risk Profile:** Net debt-to-EBITDA stood at **1.47 times** in FY26 under the demerged structure. It is expected to decline to **<1.0–1.25 times** from FY27 onwards, supported by an interest coverage ratio expected to remain above 8 times.
*   **Group Synergies:** VAML benefits from the diversified Vedanta group portfolio (zinc, copper, oil & gas, etc.). The group's net debt-to-EBITDA is expected to reduce to below 2.0 times over the medium term.

---

## Key Rating Drivers — Weaknesses

*   **Sizeable Planned Capital Outlay:** While nearing completion, the company has committed to significant capex. Unspent capex was Rs. 8,252 crore as of FY26. Future capex requirements are estimated at **Rs. 20,000–25,000 crore** over the next three fiscals (FY27–FY29). Timely commissioning without cost/time overruns remains monitorable.
    *   *Note:* A greenfield project in Odisha (3 MTPA smelter) was announced but currently has no capital commitment or finalized timelines.
*   **Commodity Price Volatility:** The business remains exposed to fluctuations in aluminium prices and input costs (coal, coke, pitch). While coal linkage security is at 60–70%, reliance on Coal India Ltd exposes the company to potential price hikes. Captive bauxite and coal mines are expected to mitigate this risk over the medium term.
*   **Regulatory Scrutiny:** Recent ED searches regarding alleged FEMA breaches require monitoring. While management states there are no material implications yet, any adverse development impacting the business or financial profile is a key rating sensitivity.

---

## Liquidity

*   **Assessment:** **Strong**.
*   **Cash Accruals:** Expected annual cash accruals of **Rs. 20,000–22,000 crore** in FY27 and FY28 will sufficiently cover yearly capex (Rs. 7,000–10,000 crore) and term debt obligations (~Rs. 6,700 crore in FY27 and ~Rs. 4,000 crore in FY28).
*   **Liquid Surplus:** Consolidated liquid surplus was approximately **Rs. 4,339 crore** as of March 31, 2026.
*   **Refinancing Ability:** The company expects to refinance debt obligations timely if required, supported by healthy financial flexibility.

---

## Outlook & Rating Sensitivity

**Outlook:** **Stable**  
*Crisil expects VAML to maintain stable operating performance with volume ramp-ups and sustained leverage, relying on internal accruals for growth capex.*

### Upward Factors
*   Strengthening of the business risk profile via higher-than-expected capacity ramp-up or sustained cost improvements through backward integration.
*   Further deleveraging resulting in a net debt-to-EBITDA ratio remaining below **0.5 times**.
*   Strengthening of the overall Vedanta group credit risk profile.

### Downward Factors
*   Weakening of the business risk profile due to slower facility ramp-up, higher-than-expected production costs, or lower realizations impacting EBITDA per tonne.
*   Higher-than-expected, debt-funded capex or excessive dividend/cash outflows leading to a net debt-to-EBITDA ratio exceeding **1.5–2.0 times** on a sustained basis.
*   Weakening of the Vedanta group's credit profile.

## Company & Subsidiaries

### Company Overview
Post-demerger, VAML houses the aluminium business of the Vedanta group.
*   **Smelting Capacity:** 2.8 MTPA (Jharsuguda + BALCO).
*   **Alumina Refinery:** 5 MTPA (Lanjigarh).
*   **Subsidiary:** Bharat Aluminium Company Ltd (BALCO) is fully consolidated.

### Group Structure (Post-Demerger)
The Vedanta group is owned by Vedanta Resources Ltd (VRL). Following the demerger, VRL holds a majority stake in the demerged entities.
*   **Aluminium:** VAML (includes BALCO).
*   **Zinc:** Vedanta Ltd (includes Hindustan Zinc Ltd).
*   **Oil & Gas:** Vedanta Oil & Gas Ltd.
*   **Iron & Steel:** Vedanta Iron & Steel Ltd (subsidiaries: ESL Steel Ltd, Sesa Mining Corporation Ltd).
*   **Power:** Vedanta Power Ltd (VPL) – Aggregate capacity 4,180 MW (+600 MW under commissioning).
*   **Copper:** Mining and smelting operations across India, Zambia, and Australia.
*   **Other:** Stake in AvanStrate Inc (Glass substrate manufacturer).
