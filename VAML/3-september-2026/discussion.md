# Discussion — Aluminium, Alumina, and VAP (clarification)

**Date:** 3 September 2026 · **Ticker:** VAML

## The distinction

Aluminium, alumina, and VAP are **not three separate metals**. They are stages of one value chain:

```
Bauxite (ore)  →  Alumina (input)  →  Aluminium (the metal)  →  VAP (premium forms of aluminium)
```

### Alumina — an input, not a metal
- Alumina (aluminium oxide, Al₂O₃) is refined from bauxite at the **Lanjigarh** refinery.
- VAML produces alumina primarily to **feed its own smelters** (captive use), not to sell on the open market.
- Recent expansion: 3.5 → 5 MTPA. Q1 FY27 alumina output was 826 kt (+41% YoY).

### Aluminium — the metal (the actual product)
- Primary "hot metal" produced by smelting alumina with electricity.
- This is VAML's core salable output. Q1 FY27 total aluminium production: **632 kt** (Jharsuguda 464 kt + BALCO 168 kt).

### VAP — premium forms of the *same* aluminium
- VAP (value-added products) = the same aluminium, further cast/processed into billets, wire rods, flip coils, rolled products and foundry alloys.
- VAP earns a **premium** over commodity-grade metal, so a higher VAP mix raises realised price and EBITDA/t.
- VAP is a **subset** of aluminium volume, not a separate product — 389 kt of the 632 kt in Q1 FY27.
- Management target: VAP share **>90%** (vs ~61% currently).

## Takeaway

VAML sells **one metal — aluminium**. Alumina is an internal input, and VAP is aluminium sold in higher-value forms. The "volume of each metal" question therefore really has one metal: **aluminium at 632 kt/quarter** (with 389 kt of that in VAP form), plus 826 kt of alumina consumed internally.
