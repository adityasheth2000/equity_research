*   **Capitalization:** Described as "strong" and providing "sufficient room to grow."
*   **Leverage Sensitivity:** A sustained gearing (debt/equity) level **above 3x** is a negative factor that could lead to a downgrade.
*   **Asset Quality/Rentability:** A slowdown in asset quality leading to a Return on Total Assets (ROTA) **below 3%** on a sustained basis is a negative factor.

#### 4. Business Risk Profile Assessment
*   **Market Position:** Strong established track record in affordable housing finance.
*   **Borrower Risk:** High risk inherent in a borrower profile consisting mostly of the self-employed informal segment.
*   **Portfolio Risks:** Moderate seasoning and geographical concentration.

*   Significant improvement in the scale of operations while maintaining profitability and asset quality at comfortable levels.
*   Diversification in the funding profile to support growing scale of operations.

**Negative Factors (Downgrade):**
*   Weakening of asset quality parameters leading to ROTA < 3% on a sustained basis.
*   Weakening of capital adequacy levels with gearing > 3x on a sustained basis.
