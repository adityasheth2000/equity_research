## 1. Company Overview & Industry Positioning

Aptus Value Housing Finance India Ltd is an affordable housing finance company (HFC) focused on self-employed borrowers in Tier-2/3/4 cities of South India. Incorporated in 2010 by M. Anandan (ex-Cholamandalam), the company has built a ₹13,107 Cr AUM book over 16 years. It operates 339 branches across 7 states, serving 1.88 lakh customers.

**Key differentiators:**
- **Fully in-house model** (origination, credit, collections, legal/technical all internal) with 3,807 employees
- **Niche segment focus:** 80% self-employed, 74% low-income group (LIG), 85% rural — historically underserved by formal credit
- **Conservative underwriting:** ~40% average LTV, centralized credit decisions, 100+ data points per application
- **Dual-entity structure:** HFC (AVHFIL) for home loans + NBFC subsidiary (AFIPL) for SME/LAP loans

**Market position:** Among the top affordable housing NBFCs/HFCs in South India. Peer set includes Aadhar Housing (P/E 21), Aavas Financiers, Home First.

## 4. Strategy & Growth Outlook

### Management Strategy (from latest PPT)
1. **AUM growth target:** 22-24% in FY27 — driven by expansion in Maharashtra/Odisha + deeper penetration in existing markets
2. **Product mix shift:** Reducing small-ticket loans (<₹7 lakh); increasing focus on ₹8-20 lakh ticket size HL and SME loans
3. **Geographic expansion:** Contiguous strategy — Maharashtra & Odisha now contributing ₹143 Cr AUM (small base, 165% growth)
4. **Funding cost improvement:** Rating upgrade to AA (both CARE and ICRA) enabling lower CoB; active repricing of liabilities
5. **Branch expansion:** Front-loaded FY27 branch additions; new branches take ~3 years to reach ₹52 Cr/branch productivity

### Growth Headroom
- Andhra Pradesh still 44% of AUM, Tamil Nadu 28% — core markets still growing 14-23%
- Maharashtra/Odisha combined just 1.1% of AUM — massive runway
- Total addressable market in affordable housing is structurally underpenetrated

## 8. Verdict Summary

**Overall Assessment: BUY with medium conviction (6.5/10)**

**Bull case:**
- Best-in-class ROE (20%+) in affordable HFC space with strong capital position
- Proven track record of 26% AUM CAGR over 5 years
- Credit rating upgrade cycle (AA- → AA) lowers funding costs
- Q4 FY26 NPA stabilization suggests the cycle may have peaked
- PEG of ~0.6 is attractive for 20%+ growth company
- Huge geographic runway (Maharashtra/Odisha just starting)
- Strong institutional ownership (FIIs: 30%, DIIs: 29%) despite promoter selling

**Bear case:**
- NPA increase from 1.19% to 1.52% in FY26 — requires monitoring
- Geographic concentration risk (44% AP, 91% top 3 states)
- Promoter stake down from 62% to 24% — raises governance questions
- 48% non-housing exposure through NBFC subsidiary adds risk
- NBFC funding model means structurally negative operating cash flows
- Macro risk: if informal sector incomes face stress, the 80% self-employed base is vulnerable

**Bottom line:** Aptus offers 20%+ ROE growth at 15x P/E in a structurally underpenetrated affordable housing market. The key risk (asset quality) appears to be stabilizing. The 1-year stock return is -18%, presenting a potential entry point. Position sizing should account for NBFC volatility and promoter overhang. Q1 FY27 results (expected August 2026) will be the next major catalyst.
