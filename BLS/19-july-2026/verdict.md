## 1. What the Company Does (in plain language)

When a country's embassy or government needs to issue a visa, processing the application is repetitive operational work — collecting the form, checking documents, capturing fingerprints/biometrics, couriering the passport back, etc. The embassy itself only wants to do the part it cares about: **deciding whether to approve or reject**. BLS International does everything else.

BLS is a **visa/consular outsourcing company**. It is the **world's 2nd-largest player** (after VFS Global) and the **only publicly listed company** in this domain globally. Founded in 2005 with a single contract for the Portuguese Embassy in New Delhi, it now serves **46+ client governments across 70+ countries**, having processed 490 Mn+ applications cumulatively.

**How the model works:**
- BLS wins exclusive, multi-year (typically 5–10 year) contracts from governments/embassies via competitive tenders (QCBS — Quality + Cost Based Selection).
- BLS sets up Visa Application Centres (VACs) — **all leased, asset-light** — and runs the front-end: appointment booking, document verification, biometric capture, fee collection, courier, attestation.
- The applicant pays BLS a service fee **upfront, before service is rendered** → **negative working capital**, zero receivables from embassies.
- BLS does **not** decide visa approvals — only governments do — so BLS carries **no approval risk**.
- Beyond the per-application fee, BLS earns from **value-added services (VAS)**: courier, photo, form-filling, insurance tie-ups, premium lounge, mobile biometrics, translation, SMS — higher-margin upsells.
- All contracts are denominated in USD/EUR with local-currency collection → natural FX hedge.

**Two business segments (FY26 mix):**

1. **Visa & Consular Services (~61% of FY26 revenue; ~90% of EBITDA)** — Rs. 1,840 Cr revenue, 40.1% EBITDA margin. Processed 44.1 Lakh visa applications in FY26 (+17.6% YoY). Net revenue per application: Rs. 3,302 (+14% YoY). Includes the acquired businesses: **iDATA** (Turkey; Germany/Italy/Czech missions across 15+ countries, Rs. 720 Cr deal Jul 2024) and **Citizenship Invest** (Dubai; residency/citizenship-by-investment for HNWIs, Rs. 260 Cr deal Oct 2024).

2. **Digital Services (~39% of FY26 revenue; ~10% of EBITDA)** — Rs. 1,158 Cr revenue (+114% YoY), but EBITDA margin only 7.0%. India-only, run via 51.8%-held subsidiary **BLS E-Services (BLSe)**. Three pillars:
   - **Business Correspondent (BC):** Last-mile rural banking on behalf of 17+ banks — deposits, withdrawals, money transfer, doorstep services. 45,800+ CSPs, 1,55,000+ touchpoints, GTV Rs. 1.11 Lakh Cr in FY26.
   - **E-Governance:** 700+ citizen services (birth/death certs, PAN/Aadhaar enrolment, land records, Ayushman Bharat) across 5 states + Central Government.
   - **Loan Distribution & Assisted E-Services:** Via acquired **Aadifidelis Solutions** (57% for Rs. 123 Cr, Nov 2024) — Rs. 36,800 Cr+ loan leads in FY26. Also **Atyati Technologies** (100% for Rs. 157 Cr, completed Jul 2026; adds 25,900 CSPs).

## 7. Strategy, Growth Outlook, Management Quality

### Strategic Pillars
1. **Aggressive bidding** for new tenders — USD 1–2 Bn of contracts up for renewal in next 1–2 years (Europe, North America).
2. **Inorganic growth** — Rs. 2,000 Cr earmarked over 4–5 years; focus on synergistic Visa (outside India) and Digital (domestic) targets.
3. **Service diversification** — Aadhaar Seva Kendras (Rs. 2,500 Cr / 6-year UIDAI contract, 15–20% EBITDA), trade document attestation (IACCIA), AI-powered visa processing (Sypha AI partnership).
4. **Geographic expansion** — China VACs (MEA contract), Latin America, Africa, CIS.
5. **Technology moat** — AI/biometric/blockchain, OCR e-visa, AI voice bots (15 languages for Spain-Ireland), cybersecurity upgrades.
6. **Self-managed model** — fully transitioned; margin uplift largely captured.

### Growth Outlook (FY27)
- **Guidance: 20–25% revenue and EBITDA growth** (reaffirmed Q4 FY26 concall).
- Visa & Consular margins to **hold at ~40%**.
- Digital margins to **stabilize at 7–8%**, with scope for improvement as VAS layer on loan distribution.
- UIDAI Aadhaar ramp: 200–300 centres; Phase 3 completes in FY27; full revenue run-rate ~Rs. 350–400 Cr/yr at maturity.
- Q1 FY27 "going steady" — Middle East conflict impact not material yet.
- Effective tax rate 10–12% currently; could rise to ~13% if Dubai raises corporate tax to 12%.

### Management Quality
- **Promoter-led**, family-run (Diwakar Aggarwal — Chairman; Shikhar Aggarwal — JMD; Nikhil Gupta — MD; Gaurav Aggarwal — Director).
- Promoter holding 70.39% — high skin-in-the-game; free float only 29.6%.
- Conservative guidance pattern (guide 20–25%, deliver 30%+).
- Institutional shareholder base: LIC, Vanguard, ADIA, Axis Max Life, Sixteenth Street Capital, Maybank.
- **Dividend:** Interim Rs. 2.0 + Final Rs. 0.5 per share = ~Rs. 103 Cr payout for FY26 (~14% of PAT). Policy: up to 30% of surplus cash post funding needs.
- No buyback — board prefers dividend (no tax advantage to buyback per CFO).

## 8. Risks

### Business / Structural Risks
1. **Tender renewal concentration** — Many major contracts (Spain global, India MEA) up for renewal over FY27–28. While BLS wins ~90% of renewals historically, a single loss would materially impact revenue (USD 1–2 Bn pipeline = both opportunity and risk).
2. **Digital margin dilution** — Digital EBITDA margin compressed from 14.3% (FY24) → 7.0% (FY26) despite 3x revenue growth. As Digital scales faster than Visa, blended margin will continue to compress unless Digital margin recovers. Aadifidelis (4–5% margin) is the key drag.
3. **Geopolitical risk** — Middle East conflict has caused short-term travel disruption; management says no material impact due to 70+ country diversification, but flagged as a watch-item. India-China, Russia-Ukraine are reopening tailwinds if resolved.
4. **M&A integration** — Three acquisitions in 24 months (iDATA Jul 2024, Aadifidelis Nov 2024, Atyati Jul 2026) plus Citizenship Invest. Goodwill at Rs. 1,213 Cr (32% of total assets). **Impairment risk** if any acquisition underperforms.
5. **Regulatory / data security** — Handling sensitive biometric data for 46+ governments; any breach would be catastrophic for contract retention.
6. **MEA debarment episode** — 2-yr debarment in Oct 2025 (later quashed by Delhi HC in Dec 2025) showed regulatory susceptibility is real, not theoretical. New MEA China contract post-quash confirms capability intact.
7. **Tax tailwind dependency** — Effective tax rate 10–12% supported by Dubai tax-free status. If Dubai raises tax to 12%, ETR rises to ~13% — modest drag.
8. **Goodwill on books** — Rs. 1,213 Cr (32% of total assets) — tested annually, no impairment currently but watch-item.
9. **Working capital swung** from -42 days (FY25) to +2 days (FY26) — acquisitions are changing the negative-WC character of the business. Watch.
10. **Concentration** — Top 10 countries account for bulk of global visa applications; loss of any large country contract would be material.

## 11. Summary View

**BLS International is a high-quality, structurally-growing business trading at a deep discount to peers despite superior fundamentals.** The bear case rests on (a) tender/renewal risk, (b) Digital margin dilution, and (c) M&A integration risk. The bull case rests on (a) Visa margin sustainability at 40%, (b) USD 1–2 Bn renewal pipeline, (c) UIDAI Aadhaar ramp, (d) net cash of Rs. 1,434 Cr funding Rs. 2,000 Cr M&A without leverage, and (e) 20–25% growth guidance that management has consistently exceeded.

The FY22→FY26 journey (Revenue 37% CAGR, EBITDA 66% CAGR, PAT 60% CAGR; Visa margin 14.6% → 40.1%) is among the most impressive operational transformations in mid-cap India. The 1-year -38% stock price decline appears disconnected from fundamentals — likely a function of low free float, geopolitical overhang, and digital-mix-margin fears. If management executes on the FY27 20–25% growth guidance (which they have historically beaten), and Digital margins stabilize, the stock offers meaningful re-rating potential from a P/E of 14x.

**Key swing factors for the next 12 months:** (1) Win-rate on the USD 1–2 Bn renewal pipeline, (2) UIDAI Aadhaar revenue ramp, (3) Digital margin trajectory, (4) geopolitical stability.
