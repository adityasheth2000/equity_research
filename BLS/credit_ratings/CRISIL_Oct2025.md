## ## Detailed Rationale

Crisil Ratings has placed the long-term bank facilities of BLS International Services Limited on **'Rating Watch with Developing Implications'**. This decision follows an order issued by the Ministry of External Affairs (MEA) which debars BLS from participating in new tenders for Indian Missions for a period of two years. While all existing contracts remain intact, Crisil anticipates that the revenue contribution from Indian Missions is low (approximately 12% of Q1 revenue), expected to reduce further during fiscal 2026. Consequently, the immediate impact of the debarment remains minimal; however, potential business disruptions or stronger-than-expected impacted performance require monitoring.

The rating also acknowledges the company's established market position in the visa outsourcing sector and its robust financial risk profile, characterized by minimal debt and improving operating margins. These strengths are partially offset by regulatory susceptibility and risks associated with growth via acquisitions.

---

## ## Key Rating Drivers — Strengths

*   **Established Market Position:**
    *   Decades-long experience (30+) with strong brand recognition.
    *   Present in **70+ countries**, catering to **45-50 government sector clients** worldwide.
    *   Largest global player in visa, passport, and consular services.
*   **Revenue Growth:**
    *   Revenue projected at **Rs 2,200 - 2,300 crore** for Fiscal 2025 (from Rs 1,677 crore in FY24).
    *   Reflects on-year growth of nearly **35%**.
    *   Growth backed by tourism trends, digital business penetration, and acquisitions (Aadifidelis Solutions, Citizenship Invests DMCC).
*   **Robust Financial Risk Profile:**
    *   Adjusted Networth projected at **Rs 1,800-1,900 crore** as on March 31, 2025 (vs Rs 1,314 crore in FY24).
    *   Minimal debt capital structure with improving operating margins.
    *   Strong financial flexibility to raise external debt if required.

## ## Key Rating Drivers — Weaknesses

*   **Regulatory Susceptibility:**
    *   Contracts are tender-based and depend on government ministry decisions.
    *   Risk of non-renewal or inability to secure new contracts due to regulation changes.
*   **Acquisition Risk:**
    *   Growth strategy involves inorganic expansion.
    *   Credit profile may materially alter if acquired businesses face slower-than-expected ramp-up.

## ## Liquidity

*   **Assessment:** **Strong**
*   **Cash Accruals:** Expected annual cash accrual of **Rs 500-520 crore**, sufficient to cover incremental working capital, capex, acquisition plans, and minimal term debt.
*   **Bank Limit Utilisation:** **Nil**.
*   **Working Capital:** Prudent management; improving financial flexibility with sizeable cash reserves (Rs 950-1,000 crore as of September 2024).
*   **Debt Obligations:** Minimal term debt obligation of Rs 25-28 crore over the medium term.

---

## ## Outlook & Rating Sensitivity

### Outlook Statement
*   **Status:** Watch Developing
*   **Implication:** Crisil Ratings will monitor updates regarding the MEA debarment order and its actual impact on group performance to resolve the watch status.

### Upward Rating Factors
*   Sustained revenue growth over **Rs. 3000 crores** driven by new customer onboarding.
*   Regular timely renewal of contracts.
*   Steady operating margin of **30-31%**.
*   Efficient working capital management ensuring **low to nil reliance on external debt**.

### Downward Rating Factors
*   Weakening of capital structure due to large, **debt-funded capex or acquisitions**.
*   Cash accrual falling below **Rs. 250-300 crore** due to declining operating income/margins or unfavorable/unrelated business acquisitions.
