# Credit Rating Report: BLS International Services Limited

## Rating Action
*   **Agency Name:** CRISIL Ratings Limited
*   **Date:** April 03, 2024
*   **Company:** BLS International Services Limited
*   **Total Bank Loan Facilities Rated:** Rs. 300 Crore
*   **Long Term Rating:** CRISIL A / Positive (Removed from 'Rating Watch with Developing Implications'; Rating Reaffirmed)
*   **Short Term Rating:** CRISIL A1 (Removed from 'Rating Watch with Developing Implications'; Rating Reaffirmed)

*Note: The Board of Directors of CRISIL Ratings Limited does not participate in discussion or assignment of any ratings.*

## Detailed Rationale
CRISIL Ratings has removed its ratings on the bank facilities and debt instruments of BLS International Services Ltd (BLS) from 'Rating Watch with Developing Implications' and has reaffirmed its ratings at 'CRISIL A / CRISIL A1' while assigning a 'Positive' outlook to its long-term rating. The watch status was previously placed due to the corporate announcement on January 15, 2024, regarding the acquisition of a 100% stake in iData Danismanlik Ve Hizmet Dis Ticaret Anonim Sirketi (“iDATA”) and its subsidiaries for an enterprise value of Euro 50 million (~ Rs 450 crore).

The acquisition is expected to yield strong operational and financial synergies for the group, supported by iData's revenue of around Rs 212 crore and EBITDA margin of nearly 61% during Jan-Oct 2023. CRISIL has revised the outlook to 'Positive' anticipating an improvement in the business risk profile, backed by synergies from iData and regular growth following easing of travel restrictions. Operating margins improved to 20.69% during the first nine months of fiscal 2024. The company maintains a robust financial risk profile, aided by efficient working capital management and an absence of sizeable debt-funded capital expenditure. Liquidity remains comfortable with healthy net cash accrual and unencumbered cash reserves.

## Key Rating Drivers — Strengths
*   **Established Market Position:** The group has over three decades of experience, operating in more than 66 countries, catering to 45-50 clients in the government sector. It is one of the largest players globally in visa, passport, consular, and citizen services.
*   **Revenue Growth:** Revenue is expected to improve to Rs 1,650-1,700 crore during fiscal 2024 (from Rs 1,525 crore in fiscal 2023), reflecting year-on-year growth of nearly 11%. Revenue is projected to record a CAGR of 33-35% for the four fiscals till 2024.
*   **Robust Financial Risk Profile:**
    *   **Networth:** Projected to be in the range of Rs 1,100-1,200 crore as on March 31, 2024 (up from Rs 702 crore as on March 31, 2023).
    *   **Debt Structure:** Debt-free capital structure with debt protection indicators remaining strong.
    *   **Accretion:** Better accretion to reserves and prudent working capital management.
*   **Operational Efficiency:** Operating margin improved to 20.69% during the first nine months of fiscal 2024 (vs 15.26% in fiscal 2023) due to better contribution from value-added services and volumes.

## Key Rating Drivers — Weaknesses
*   **Regulatory Susceptibility:** Business is susceptible to changes in regulations by concerned ministries of various countries. Contracts with diplomatic missions are tender-based, and non-renewal or inability to bag new contracts could weaken the revenue profile.
*   **Moderate Scale:** The scale of operations is moderate, and sustenance of growth remains a key monitorable.
*   **Profitability Track Record:** Operating margins were in the range of 13-15% over fiscals 2022 and 2023. While improved recently, the track record of sustained improvement in operating profitability amidst steady business growth is limited.
*   **Acquisition Risks:** Fall in demand or any unfavorable acquisition could adversely impact the overall business risk profile. Sustainability of margin improvements from the iData acquisition is a key monitorable.

## Liquidity
*   **Assessment:** Strong.
*   **Cash Accrual:** Expected at Rs 250-270 crore per fiscal, which covers incremental working capital expenses and capex/acquisition plans in the absence of term debt obligations.
*   **Bank Limit Utilisation:** Nil.
*   **Cash Reserves:** Sizeable unencumbered cash reserves of Rs 950-1,000 crore as of February 2024.
*   **Current Ratio:** Estimated at around 89 times as of March 31, 2023.
*   **Financial Flexibility:** Enhanced by strong networth and cash accruals, allowing for future debt raising if required without significant risk.

## Outlook & Rating Sensitivity
*   **Outlook:** **Positive**

**Upward Factors:**
*   Sustained growth in revenue driven by onboarding new customers and timely contract renewal, with a steady operating margin of 20-22%.
*   Efficient working capital management ensuring low to nil reliance on external debt.
*   Timely completion of iData or any other acquisition with no cost overrun.
*   Higher-than-expected net cash accrual.

**Downward Factors:**
*   Any large, debt-funded capex or acquisition weakening the financial risk profile and liquidity.
*   Drop in cash accrual to Rs 80-90 crore, owing to a decline in operating income and/or margin, or any unfavorable/unrelated business acquisition.

**Company Information**
*   **Established:** 1983

*   **Operations:** Specialist provider of visa, passport, and attestation services to Indian missions globally. Present in over 62 countries through joint ventures or wholly owned subsidiaries.
