## Detailed Rationale
CRISIL Ratings has placed the bank facilities ratings of BLS International Services Limited (BLS) on **'Rating Watch with Developing Implications'** following a corporate announcement on January 15, 2024. BLS plans to acquire a 100% stake in **iData Danismanlik Ve Hizmet Dis Ticaret Anonim Sirketi ("iDATA")**, a Turkey-based company providing visa and consular services. The transaction has an enterprise value of approximately **Euro 50 Million (~Rs. 450 Crores)** plus milestone payments. The acquisition is expected to be funded mainly through internal accruals and is subject to regulatory approvals. The watch status will be resolved once detailed information regarding the post-acquisition business and financial risk profile is available. Currently, the ratings reflect BLS's established market position and robust financial risk profile, partially offset by its moderate scale of operations and regulatory susceptibility.

---

## Key Rating Drivers — Strengths
*   **Established Market Position:**
    *   Presence in over **66 countries** with around **45-50 government clients**.
    *   Operates around **30,000 application centers** worldwide.
    *   Strengthened by the acquisition of **Zero Mass** during Q1 FY23, providing diversification in digital business.
    *   Recognized as one of the largest global players in Visa, Passport, and Consular services.
*   **Robust Financial Risk Profile:**
    *   **Nil dependence on external debt**; strong networth provides financial flexibility.
    *   Networth estimated at **Rs. 940-950 Crore** as of March 31, 2024 (vs Rs. 702 Crore as of March 31, 2022).
    *   Strong debt protection indicators: Interest coverage of **90.44 times** (FY23).
    *   Prudent working capital management supports financial flexibility with no stretch in the working capital cycle.

## Key Rating Drivers — Weaknesses
*   **Moderate Scale & Regulatory Susceptibility:**
    *   Contracts with diplomatic missions are **tender-based**, creating revenue risk regarding contract non-renewal or inability to secure new contracts.
    *   Susceptible to changes in regulations by ministries of various countries.
    *   Sustained revenue growth depends on contract renewals and business acquisition synergies.

---

## Liquidity
*   **Assessment:** **Strong**
*   **Bank Limit Utilization:** Nil over the past few years due to prudent working capital management.
*   **Cash Accruals:** Expected to be **Rs. 250-260 Crores per annum** (medium term).
*   **Cash Reserves:** **Rs. 687 Crore** as of September 2023.
*   **Current Ratio:** Estimated at **5.62 times** as of March 31, 2023.
*   **Outflow:** No term debt repayments currently envisaged.

---

## Outlook & Rating Sensitivity
**Current Outlook:** **Watch Developing**
*   **Resolution Criteria:** The watch will be resolved once detailed information and management discussion regarding the business and financial risk profile of the post-acquisition consolidated entity are available.

**Upward Rating Factors:**
*   Sustained increase in revenue driven by onboarding new customers and timely contract renewal.
*   Steady operating profitability at **15-16%**, leading to net cash accruals exceeding expectations.
*   Efficient working capital management ensuring low/nil reliance on external debt.

**Downward Rating Factors:**
*   Large, debt-funded Capex or acquisition adversely impacting financial risk profile and liquidity.
*   Cash accrual declining below **Rs. 80-90 Crore** on account of decline in operating income, profitability, or unfavorable/unrelated business acquisitions.
