## Detailed Rationale

CRISIL Ratings has upgraded the long-term rating to 'CRISIL A+/Stable' and reaffirmed the short-term rating at 'CRISIL A1'. The upgrade reflects healthy quarterly revenue growth supported by an established market position in the visa services business, with revenue expected to reach Rs 2,200-2,300 crore in Fiscal 2025 from Rs 1,677 crore in Fiscal 2024. The business risk profile is supported by operating efficiencies, with operating margins expected between 27-30% in Fiscal 2025. The financial risk profile is robust due to efficient working capital management and low reliance on external debt. The ratings assume continued benefit from tourism growth and the integration of the recently acquired entity, iData (July 2024). Key risks include regulatory susceptibility and growth strategy via acquisitions.

---

## Key Rating Drivers — Strengths

*   **Established Market Position:** The group has over three decades of experience, a strong brand, and presence in more than 70 countries. It caters to 45-50 clients in the government sector and is a global leader in visa, passport, consular, and citizen services. Revenue growth is projected at nearly 35% on a year-on-year basis.
*   **Business Growth Catalysts:** Medium-term improvement is backed by steady tourism growth and digital business penetration. Incremental business is expected from the acquisition of **iData** (July 2024), **Aadifidelis Solutions Pvt. Ltd.**, and **Citizenship Invests DMCC**, whose benefits have already started flowing in from Q2 Fiscal 2025.
*   **Robust Financial Risk Profile:**
    *   **Networth:** Adjusted networth projected at Rs 1,800-1,900 crore as on March 31, 2025 (up from Rs 1,314 crore in March 2024).
    *   **Leverage:** Minimal debt capital structure.
    *   **Capex:** Absence of debt-funded capital expenditure or stretch in the working capital cycle.

## Key Rating Drivers — Weaknesses

*   **Regulatory Susceptibility:** Contracts with diplomatic missions are tender-based with pre-requisites for experience and IT infrastructure. Non-renewal of contracts or inability to bag new contracts could weaken the revenue profile. Sustenance of growth remains monitorable.
*   **Acquisition Risks:** While growth has been both organic and inorganic, reliance on acquisitions is a key rating sensitivity factor. Slower-than-expected ramp-up of acquired businesses could materially alter credit profiles.

## Liquidity

*   **Assessment:** **Strong**.
*   **Cash Accruals:** Expected annual cash accrual of **Rs 500-520 crore** is adequate to cover incremental working capital expenses, Capex or acquisitions, and minimal term debt obligations of Rs 25-28 crore.
*   **Bank Limit Utilisation:** Nil/Unutilized.
*   **Cash Reserves:** Sizeable unencumbered cash reserve of **Rs 950-1,000 crore** as of September 2024.
*   **Management:** Strong working capital management supports financial flexibility.
