### **Key Equity Research Observations:**

*   **Operating Cash Flow (OCF) Growth:** The company demonstrates strong cash generation capabilities, with OCF growing from **Rs. 350 Cr in FY24** to **Rs. 829 Cr in FY25**. Guidance for FY26 projects OCF reaching **Rs. 903 Cr**, implying consistent underlying business performance.
*   **Significant Strategic Investment (FY25):** FY25 saw a massive outflow of **Rs. 899 Cr** for "Payment made towards acquisition of Subsidiary," which reduced the Net Cash position and Closing Cash Balance (from 414 Cr to 283 Cr). This indicates an aggressive M&A strategy or completion of a major deal in this period.
*   **Capex Levels:** Capital expenditures (Purchase of PPE) are stable/moderate, ranging between **Rs. 64 Cr (FY24)** and **Rs. 161 Cr (FY25)**, with projected **Rs. 137 Cr for FY26**. This suggests the heavy CAPEX phase might be over or shifting to lower annual levels.
*   **Financing Strategy Shift (FY26 Guidance):** While FY24 and FY25 relied on debt inflows (**Rs. 205 Cr** proceeds from current borrowings), FY26 guidance shows a shift towards **deleveraging and capital returns**.
    *   Net Financing Cash Flow is projected to be negative **(Rs. 246 Cr)**.
    *   Significant dividend payout is planned for FY26 (**Rs. 130 Cr**).
    *   Borrowings are being repaid (**Rs. 39 Cr** outflow) and lease liabilities reduced (**Rs. 55 Cr** outflow).
*   **Cash Position Recovery:** Despite the M&A outflow in FY25, the strong OCF in FY26 is expected to recover the cash balance, projecting a year-end cash equivalent of **Rs. 494 Cr** by March FY26.
*   **Investment Outflows:** There is planned significant parking of idle cash in Term Deposits in FY26 (**Rs. 187 Cr outflow**), which is a use of cash not reflected in operating/investing PPE lines but impacts liquidity management.
