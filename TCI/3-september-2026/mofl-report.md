# MOFL Report — Transport Corporation of India (1QFY27 Results Update)

**Source:** Motilal Oswal Financial Services (MOFSL), dated 3 Aug 2026
**Rating:** **BUY** | **CMP (at report):** ₹915 | **Target Price:** ₹1,150 (+26%) | **Valuation:** 17x FY28E EPS
**Ticker in report:** TRPC (NSE: TCI)

> **Unit note:** Report figures are in **INR million (₹ Mn)**. Converted to ₹ Cr where noted (1 Cr = 10 Mn). "E" = estimate.

---

## 1. Headline — 1QFY27 (quarter ended June 2026)

| Metric | Actual | YoY | vs MOFSL est. |
|---|---|---|---|
| Revenue | ₹1,250 Cr (₹12,485 Mn) | +9.6% | in line (₹12,526 Mn) |
| EBITDA | ₹135 Cr (₹1,352 Mn) | +11.7% | **+5% beat** |
| EBITDA margin | 10.8% | +20 bps | vs 10.3% est |
| Adj. PAT | ₹106 Cr (₹1,057 Mn) | **-0.8% (flat)** | in line |

**Read:** Revenue in line, EBITDA beat, but **PAT flat** — the drag came from a **higher effective tax rate (14.0% vs 10.8% YoY)** and a lower share of JV profit (₹184 Mn vs ₹197 Mn).

| Segment | Revenue (₹ Mn) | YoY | EBIT margin | YoY change |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Freight | 5,870 | +8.9% | 2.2% | -20 bps |
| Supply Chain (SCS) | 5,523 | +10.0% | 5.5% | -10 bps |
| Seaways | 1,672 | +6.2% | **34.7%** | **-220 bps** |
| Energy (wind) | 13 | -7.1% | 46.2% | — |
| **Net segment revenue** | **13,078** | +9.0% | 7.8% | — |

**Revenue share (1QFY27):** Freight 45% | SCS 42% | Seaways 13% | Energy ~0%

**Key segment observations from the report:**
- **Freight EBIT margin has been grinding down for 8 straight quarters** — 3.0% (1QFY25) → 2.2% (1QFY27). Revenue grew but margin compressed on elevated diesel/bunker costs (mitigated by contractual pass-through, with a short-term lag).
- **SCS growth has materially decelerated** — 22-31% YoY during FY26 → just **10%** in 1QFY27 (off a high base; auto inventory cleared post-GST cut).
- **Seaways margin is the one that is visibly rolling over** — EBIT 40.6% (3QFY26) → 39.1% (4QFY26) → **34.7% (1QFY27)**, hit by volatile bunker prices.

## 3. Management commentary & guidance (from the report)

**Freight:**
- +9% YoY driven by **healthy volume growth + LTL traction**.
- **LTL:FTL mix = 63:37** currently (note: this reverses the usual convention — i.e., ~63% is the value-added LTL share per MOFSL); targeting **60:40 by FY27** (i.e., shifting further toward LTL).
- Opened **10 new LTL branches** in the quarter; plan to add **30 more**.
- Cost pressure from elevated operating costs continues.

**Supply Chain:**
- Growth guidance: **10-15%**.
- **Auto = ~80% of SCS revenue.**
- Fresh inventory replenishment post-GST cut expected to support demand; seasonal fluctuations to continue.
- Margins/ROCE "subdued" due to continued **warehouse + truck investments**.

**Seaways:**
- Revenue +6% YoY (pricing-led; volumes flat).
- **Management guides Seaways EBITDA margin at 38-40%** (vs 46.9% in FY26 — a clear step-down).
- One vessel dry-docking in **Mar'27**.

**Consolidated:**
- **FY27 revenue & profit growth guidance maintained at 10-12%.**
- **Capex:** FY26 ~₹370 Cr (₹3.7 Bn); **FY27 ~₹600 Cr (₹6 Bn), of which ~₹240 Cr (₹2.4 Bn) on ships.**

**JVs (1QFY27):**
- TCI-Concor: **+8% YoY** (38k TEUs vs 40k YoY).
- Transystem (Mitsui): **+11.5% YoY**.
- Cold Chain: **~+49% YoY** (customer additions in quick-commerce & retail); capital employed crossed ₹110 Cr (₹1.1 Bn).

**Macro tailwind cited:** Govt target to **double waterways share in modal mix 6% → 12% by 2030** (USD 120bn+ planned investment; Sagarmala + Coastal Shipping Bill 2024).

## 5. Financial projections (MOFSL, consolidated)

### Returns & efficiency — the uncomfortable trend

| Metric | FY25 | FY26 | FY27E | FY28E |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| ROE | 19.5% | 19.0% | 17.2% | 16.6% |
| ROCE | 18.7% | 18.2% | 16.3% | 15.9% |
| RoIC | 20.2% | 19.8% | 17.7% | 16.3% |

→ MOFSL forecasts **a steady multi-year decline in every return metric** even as revenue grows 13-14%/yr — the classic "growth without returns" pattern, driven by capex outrunning earnings.

### Working capital — a rising risk flag

| Metric | FY25 | FY26 | FY27E | FY28E |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Debtor days | 59 | 65 | **70** | **70** |
| Creditor days | 9 | 20 | 8 | 8 |
| **WC days** | 51 | 46 | **63** | **63** |

→ **Working-capital days spike to 63 in FY27E-28E** (debtor days stretch to 70). This is the report's most notable *negative* forward flag — and it sits right at the edge of CRISIL's stated downgrade trigger (>90 days). The report attributes it to a longer receivable cycle despite short creditor terms.

### Cash flow — FCF turns negative

| Metric (₹ Mn) | FY25 | FY26 | FY27E | FY28E |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| CFO | 3,592 | 4,444 | 3,177 | 4,671 |
| Capex | -3,545 | -4,122 | -4,000 | -5,500 |
| **FCF** | 47 | 322 | **-823** | **-829** |
| Closing cash | 510 | 932 | 472 | 196 |

→ **Free cash flow goes negative in FY27E and FY28E** as capex (ships, warehouses, trucks) stays heavy (~₹400-550 Cr/yr). Net Debt:Equity stays ~0 (net cash), so this is a *returns/valuation* risk, not solvency.

### Valuation

| Multiple | FY26 | FY27E | FY28E |
|---|---|---|---|
| P/E | 15.5x | 14.6x | 13.0x |
| EV/EBITDA | 12.9x | 11.4x | 9.9x |
| P/BV | 2.7x | 2.4x | 2.0x |
| Dividend yield | 0.9% | 0.9% | 0.9% |

**Per-share:** BV ₹333 (FY26) → ₹450 (FY28E); DPS ₹8 flat; payout 13.5% → 11.3%.

## 6. Key takeaways — what this report adds/changes vs my verdict

1. **Confirms the de-risking story quantitatively.** Freight drops from 45% → 42% of revenue by FY28E while SCS rises to 47%. The diversification thesis is playing out on schedule.

2. **The Seaways margin rollover is now *visible*, not just guided.** EBIT margin already fell 40.6% → 34.7% in one quarter. MOFSL still models EBITDA 38-40% (management guidance), but the trajectory is down.

3. **A NEW risk I should weight more heavily: working capital.** MOFSL forecasts debtor days 65 → 70 and WC days 46 → 63 by FY27-28. This is the clearest sign yet that the freight/SCS receivable cycle is stretching — exactly the metric CRISIL flags as a downgrade trigger.

4. **FCF negative in FY27-28** — the balance sheet stays net-cash, but the company is "buying" its growth (₹400-550 Cr capex/yr) with no free cash for two years. This caps any near-term re-rating on cash-flow grounds.

5. **Returns keep grinding lower.** MOFSL has ROE 19% → 16.6% and ROCE 18.2% → 15.9% by FY28E. If this materializes, the current ~14.7x P/E is *fair* rather than *cheap* — the "discount" to peers is partly earned.

6. **Growth re-accelerates on paper (13.5% FY27E, 14.1% FY28E)** — notably *above* management's own 10-12% guidance. This is the single most aggressive assumption in the report and the key thing to watch: if MOFSL's 13-14% growth doesn't land, the "Buy" thesis (and ₹1,150 TP on 17x FY28E EPS) looks stretched.

7. **TP mechanics:** ₹1,150 = 17x FY28E EPS of ₹70.5. That multiple is ~30% above today's ~13-14x forward P/E — the report is effectively betting on a **re-rating**, which only happens if the return-on-capital slide reverses.
