**Slide Title:** Why Recombinant Human Albumin ?

**Topic: Product Differentiation, Competitive Moat, and Operational Advantages**

*   **Supply Chain Security & Production Method:**
    *   The product is manufactured via fermentation in yeast (*Pichia pastoris*).
    *   This approach ensures a "Predictable & assured supply," mitigating supply volatility risks often associated with donor-dependent sources (like human plasma).

*   **Safety & Regulatory Moat (Competitive Advantage):**
    *   The product offers inherent freedom from potential contamination sources.
    *   Specifically free from major viral/biological risks: HIV, HBV, HCV, and Prion contamination.
    *   All raw materials used in production are free from animal sources (xeno-free).

*   **Cost Competitiveness & Unit Economics:**
    *   The process achieves "Best in class productivity."
    *   This efficiency makes the product cost-competitive when compared directly to the incumbent: Plasma Albumin.

*   **Market Positioning:**
    *   Product features "High quality/Purity" which "Meets Global standards," implying readiness for international market entry or export.
