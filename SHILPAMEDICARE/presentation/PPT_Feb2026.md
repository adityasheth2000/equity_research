**Slide Topic: Why Recombinant Human Albumin?**

*   **Manufacturing Technology:** The product is produced via yeast fermentation (*Pichia pastoris*), ensuring a "predictable & assured supply."
*   **Safety & Regulatory Profile:**
    *   Inherently free from major blood-borne contamination sources, specifically HIV, HBV, and HCV.

*   **Competitive Positioning:**
    *   **Productivity:** Claims "best in class productivity," which drives the product's cost competitiveness compared to plasma-based albumin.
    *   **Quality:** Claims high quality and purity that meets "Global standards."
