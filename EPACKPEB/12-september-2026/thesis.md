**Rationale for the small starter:** EPACK is an execution-dependent, thin-margin, working-capital-heavy small-cap with a weak moat. At ~25x trailing, it is fairly valued rather than cheap. A ~0.4% starter lets the investor track the story closely without meaningful capital at risk, and add only when the evidence improves.

**Sizing discipline:** raise the maximum ceiling only if margins stabilise above 10.5%, sandwich-panel utilisation inflects, and free cash flow turns sustainably positive. Otherwise, stop at the ceiling.

## 1. Top 5 Reasons to Buy

### Reason 1 — Underpenetrated, structurally growing industry where EPACK is the fastest-growing organised player

- PEB is only **~13–15% of India's industrial construction** and **~3–5% of overall construction activity**, versus **~60–98% of low-rise industrial in the US/UK** — a decades-long runway, not a cyclical fad.
- The market grows **~10% CAGR (₹21,000 cr FY25 → ₹34,500 cr FY30)** vs overall construction ~6–7%; organised share rising **42–47% → 47–52%**.
- EPACK has compounded revenue **~36% CAGR (FY22–26)** and grown share from **~1% to ~5%** — roughly 4–4.5x the industry pace.
- Demand drivers are broad and durable: private industrial capex, PLI (₹1.97 lakh cr across 14 sectors), data centres (~30% CAGR), Semicon 2.0, logistics/warehousing, renewables.
- **Must be true:** industrial capex holds up and EPACK keeps converting pipeline at/above industry growth.

### Reason 2 — Visible order book plus a funded capacity runway = multi-year growth with operating leverage

- Pending order book **₹1,376 cr (+40% YoY)**, ~3.8x quarterly revenue (7–8 months visibility); FY27 order-inflow target **₹2,000 cr+**; Q1 FY27 inflow **₹578 cr (+142%)**.
- Capacity going **134k → 220k MTPA by end-FY27 (+64%)** and panels **1.31m → 2.11m sqm**, largely IPO-funded.
- PEB utilisation already **50% → 75%+** — the bottleneck is capacity, not demand.
- Stated peak revenue potential of **₹2,700–2,900 cr vs ₹1,525 cr today** — ~1.8–1.9x growth already funded by assets under construction.
- **Must be true:** Ghiloth/Gujarat commission on time and demand absorbs the new capacity.

### Reason 3 — Deleveraged, net-cash balance sheet with strong cash conversion — low downside risk and self-funding growth

- Gross borrowings cut **₹210 cr → ₹108 cr**; net cash **₹177–201 cr**; D/E **0.15x** — rare for a small-cap capex growth story.
- FY26 operating cash flow **₹136 cr (~85% of EBITDA)**; **ICRA upgraded from A/A2+ to A+/A1**.
- FY27 capex (~₹150 cr) is guided to be funded **without incremental debt**, and the company carries ~₹219 cr in >3-month deposits.
- **Must be true:** cash conversion holds without further stretching payables/supplier finance (the key quality caveat).

### Reason 4 — Management has demonstrated execution and is candid about misses

- FY26 delivered: revenue **₹1,525 cr vs guided ₹1,500–1,550 cr**, **PAT +56%**, debt repaid ahead of plan (~₹107 cr), FOCF ~₹135 cr, Mambattu commissioned.
- Management openly discussed panel underutilisation (~25%), **losing two data-centre bids**, and incomplete steel-cost pass-through — evidence of honest communication rather than spin.
- **Promoter holds ~65% with zero pledge**, no default history, unmodified audit opinion, and an independent director bought ~₹1.86 cr of stock in the past year.
- **Must be true:** guidance continues to be met and first-year governance missteps (audit-committee breach, penalty) do not recur.

### Reason 5 — Valuation is reasonable relative to growth, with optionality not yet priced in

- Trades at **~25x TTM / ~18–21x FY27E** — broadly in line with Interarch (~21x) despite roughly double the growth rate.
- Free optionality: **sandwich-panel utilisation 44% → 70%+** (high-margin, automated line); **data-centre subsidiary (₹75 cr)**; **exports** (18–26% EBITDA margins vs 10–11% domestic); long-dated LGSF/residential.
- All post-IPO broker coverage is BUY (ICICI ₹240, DAM Capital ₹230).
- **Must be true:** EBITDA margin normalises to **≥10.5% in FY27** and the multiple does not compress if growth moderates.

## 2. Triggers to Add More

Add only on **evidence**, not on price momentum or headlines. Each add should be justified by at least one of the following:

| # | Add Trigger | Threshold | Why it matters |
|---|---|---:|---|
| 1 | EBITDA margin recovery | **≥10.5% in Q2 FY27** and sustained | The #1 near-term test; proves steel-cost pass-through works |
| 2 | Sandwich-panel utilisation | **>60%** (toward the 70–75% target) | Unlocks the high-margin, automated operating-leverage story |
| 3 | Revenue / order trajectory | FY27 revenue **≥₹1,900 cr**; order inflow **≥₹2,000 cr** | Validates the capacity build-out |
| 4 | Cash-conversion quality | CFO/EBITDA **≥80% without further payable expansion** | Proves cash generation is real, not vendor-stretch |
| 5 | Capacity execution | Ghiloth commissioned (Sep/Oct 2026) and Gujarat on track (Apr 2027) | Execution credibility |
| 6 | Data-centre proof | First **complete turnkey DC order won** (not just panels) | Converts narrative into earnings |
| 7 | Return ratios | ROCE/ROE recovering toward **20%+** as IPO capital deploys | Confirms capital allocation is value-accretive |
| 8 | Price discipline | Material weakness toward **₹180–200** (without thesis damage) | Improves entry risk-reward |

**Mechanics:** accumulate in tranches tied to the triggers above, not a fixed calendar. Avoid adding immediately after a run-up on data-centre or order-headline news, since that part is currently narrative (DC ~4–5% of order book; two bids lost).

## 3. Triggers to Avoid / Trim / Exit

### No-add (pause accumulation) signals

- Another quarter of **sub-10% EBITDA margin**, or evidence steel pass-through has structurally failed.
- Sandwich-panel utilisation **stagnating or falling** while more panel capex is committed.
- **Working-capital deterioration:** receivables past-due >6 months rising, ECL allowance increasing, or the lean cycle maintained only by further payables/supplier-finance stretching.
- **Guidance cut** or another Q3-FY26-style execution surprise.
- **Governance missteps:** further listing-compliance breaches, KMP pay/disclosure conflicts, or related-party concerns.

### Trim / Exit signals (thesis-breakers)

- **Two consecutive quarters** of margin below the guided range with no credible pass-through, plus **guidance cut** → the growth-with-margin thesis breaks.
- **Free cash flow stays negative** through FY27 despite the capex being funded and order book full → the self-funding story fails.
- **Data-centre / panel optionality written down** or the ₹75 cr subsidiary deployed into a low-return project without disclosure.
- **Promoter or PE selling** after lock-in expiry (~42% of capital now unlocked; South Asia Growth Fund III ~10.2%) — especially large, sustained sales.
- **Balance-sheet reversal:** net debt returning, supplier finance withdrawn, or a receivables write-off large relative to PAT.
- **Valuation excess:** multiple re-rating to **30x+** without corresponding margin/FCF proof → reduce risk.

## 4. Ongoing Monitoring Checklist

**Quarterly (every results call):**
1. EBITDA margin vs 10.5% guidance (the single most important datapoint).
2. Panel utilisation and any segment-margin disclosure.
3. Order inflow vs the ₹2,000 cr FY27 target; order-book conversion.
4. CFO/EBITDA conversion and the source of cash flow (payables vs profit).
5. Trade receivables ageing, ECL provision, supplier-finance balance.
6. Revenue vs the ₹1,900–1,950 cr FY27 guidance.
7. Data-centre order wins/losses; first full turnkey DC order.
8. Any related-party, KMP or governance disclosure.

**Annual / event-driven:**
1. Ghiloth and Gujarat commissioning and ramp.
2. ROCE/ROE trajectory as IPO capital is deployed.
3. Promoter shareholding and pledge status; post-lock-in selling.
4. ICRA rating actions.
5. Capital-allocation announcements (new capex, acquisitions, DC subsidiary deployment).

## 5. One-Line Thesis Statement

> **EPACK is a fast-growing, net-cash, organised #3 in an underpenetrated PEB industry with a funded capacity runway and genuine industrial/data-centre tailwinds — but it is a thin-margin, working-capital-heavy project business with a weak moat, so it earns only a small starter position that grows with proof of margin recovery, panel utilisation and real cash conversion, and is sized with a pre-committed ceiling.**
