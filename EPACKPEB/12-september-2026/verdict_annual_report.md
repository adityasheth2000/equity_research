# EPACK Prefab Technologies Ltd (NSE: EPACKPEB) — FY2026 Annual Report verdict

> Source: `EPACKPEB/annual_reports/AnnualReport_2026.pdf` (308 pages), read via the text-extraction + selected-page-image workflow. All page references are to the Annual Report (AR p.X).
> Market data (12 Sep 2026): price ₹240 | mkt cap ₹2,420 cr | P/E ~25.5x | P/B ~3.3x | promoter 65.0% (zero pledge).

## 1. Bottom line

**Verdict: a genuinely fast-growing, structurally-supported industrial — but not a high-conviction multibagger at today's price. WATCHLIST / accumulate on weakness.**

EPACK has compounded revenue ~36% CAGR (FY22–26), is the self-described #3 PEB player, grew its core Prefab segment 45% in FY26, and now runs a net-cash balance sheet (net cash ≈ ₹198–201 cr; D/E 0.15x) after its Oct-2025 IPO and a ₹107 cr debt repayment (AR pp.5, 60, 67). The demand backdrop — private industrial capex, solar/renewable module factories, data centres, logistics, cold chain — is real and the order book grew 21.5% to ₹1,113 cr (AR pp.61, 84).

The problems are equally clear, and they are structural rather than temporary:

1. **Thin, steel-linked margins.** Consolidated EBITDA margin is only ~10.5% and PAT margin ~6.1% (AR pp.59–60). Steel is the dominant input, contracts are largely fixed-price, and there is no contractual pass-through. Margin expansion is the whole thesis, not a given.
2. **No durable moat.** The group itself frames the advantage as execution speed, design capability and pan-India footprint (AR pp.26, 82–84) — replicable advantages in a competitive, capital-light contract market.
3. **Capital-hungry growth.** Inventory (₹265.8 cr) and receivables (₹308.8 cr) build every year; the "32-day" net-working-capital figure is flattered by ₹397.8 cr of trade payables (AR pp.160, 231). True free cash flow after capex was only ~₹6 cr in FY26 (OCF ₹135.7 cr − capex ₹129.3 cr); the headline "Free Operating Cash Flow +143% to ₹136 cr" (AR p.60) is simply operating cash flow, not post-capex.
4. **Governance / related-party intensity.** Sales to group company Epack Durable jumped to ₹86.9 cr (≈5.7% of revenue) from ₹47.2 cr, purchases from East India Technologies ₹37.1 cr and Epack Petrochem ₹49.3 cr, plus a web of expense reimbursements to promoter family members (AR pp.205, 207).
5. **Valuation already reflects success.** At ~25x trailing earnings the market is paying for the growth and the margin recovery at the same time.

**The realistic path is 25%+ earnings compounding delivering perhaps 2–3x over 3–5 years if execution holds — a good outcome, but a "prove-it" story, not a table-pounding multibagger at ₹240.**

## 2. What the business does

Two segments (AR pp.4, 13):

| Segment | FY26 revenue | % | FY25 revenue | Growth |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Prefab / Buildings (PEB, prefab structures, sandwich panels, LGSF) | ₹1,382.3 cr | 90.6% | ₹953.2 cr | **+45.0%** |
| EPS Packaging (expanded polystyrene beads/shapes) | ₹143.0 cr | 9.4% | ₹180.7 cr | **−20.9%** |
| **Total** | **₹1,525.3 cr** | 100% | **₹1,133.9 cr** | **+34.5%** |

- **Prefab is now effectively the company.** Turnkey design → fabrication → logistics → erection of pre-engineered steel buildings, plus insulated sandwich panels (PUF/PIR/EPS/Rockwool/Glasswool), clean rooms, cold storage and modular structures (AR pp.13, 78, 80). Sells on speed/cost vs RCC.
- **EPS is the legacy anchor and is shrinking** — FY26 revenue fell ~21% to ₹143 cr. No order book (roll-out basis); historically concentrated on large consumer-electronics customers (AR p.13, 84). Decline partly reflects softer packaging demand.
- **Revenue is entirely domestic** — exports were nil in FY26 vs ₹2.5 cr in FY25 (AR p.267).
- **Regional mix (Prefab):** North+Central 40% (₹553 cr), South 37% (₹512 cr), West 17% (₹235 cr, down from 32%), East 6% (₹83 cr) — a deliberate de-risking from the West into the South (AR p.85).

**Assets & capacity (AR pp.5, 61, 62):** 4 operational plants — two at Greater Noida (UP), Ghiloth (Rajasthan), Mambattu (AP, with an adjoining expansion + continuous sandwich-panel line). PEB capacity 133,922 MTPA, ~147,122 MTPA after the new Mambattu line; panel capacity expanded to 1.3 mn sqm. 3 design centres; 3,515+ employees including contract labour.

| ROE | 17.0% | 22.7% | diluted by IPO cash |

**Positives**
- Revenue and profit growth are genuine and broad-based, driven by Prefab, not accounting.
- Balance sheet transformed: gross debt down from ~₹218 cr to ~₹115 cr, D/E 0.15x, ICRA upgraded to A+/A1 (AR pp.67, 102). Corporate guarantees to Epack Petrochem (₹140.3 cr) fully extinguished (AR p.276).
- Operating cash flow and cash conversion improved materially; the FY25 negative true-FCF position turned positive in FY26.
- Auditor (Talati & Talati LLP) issued a clean opinion; the single Key Audit Matter is tax-litigation assessment, and internal-financial-controls opinion is unqualified (AR pp.150, 159). No auditor qualification.

**Watch-points**
- **Return ratios fell** (ROCE 22.9%→19.4%, ROE 22.7%→17.0%). Management calls this temporary IPO-equity dilution (AR p.59) — fair, but it also means the fresh ₹285 cr of equity must earn its keep; until the Gujarat/Ghiloth assets ramp, the company is earning <20% on a larger capital base.
- **Working capital deepening.** Inventories ₹265.8 cr (from ₹151.5 cr), receivables ₹308.8 cr (from ₹205.3 cr). The 32-day NWC metric survives because payables are ₹397.8 cr (from ₹223.3 cr). Receivables ageing worsened: ₹22.7 cr in the 1–2-year bucket, ₹10.6 cr 2–3 years, ₹4.0 cr >3 years, and ₹12.3 cr flagged as significant-increase-in-credit-risk; ECL provision only ₹3.5 cr (AR pp.182, 231).
- **"Free Operating Cash Flow" is mislabelled.** It equals OCF, not OCF-minus-capex (AR p.60). Investors should use the cash-flow statement.
- **Contingent liabilities are material vs profits:** bank guarantees ₹212.4 cr (normal for a contractor), disputed income-tax demands ~₹154 cr plus numerous GST penalty appeals, none provided (AR pp.203, 276). Capital commitments jumped to ₹92.3 cr from ₹2.3 cr (AR p.276) — consistent with the FY27 capex plan, but a large step-up.

## 4. Strategic quality & the growth engine

**Industry (AR pp.73–77):** India's prefabricated market is estimated at ₹465–485 bn (FY25) and projected to reach ₹730–800 bn by FY30 — a 9–11% CAGR. PEB was ~₹130 bn in FY19 → ₹210 bn FY25 (~8.3% CAGR). Growth drivers: urbanisation, government housing/infra policy, labour scarcity, sustainability, and new end-markets.

**EPACK is out-growing the industry by ~4x**, which is the strongest part of the case. It is riding exactly the right end-markets:
- **Renewable-energy / solar module manufacturing** (Premier Energies, Waaree, Avaada, Inox cited), **data centres**, **aviation/airports** (Hindon), **steel** (Tata Steel, JSPL), FMCG/warehousing and cold chain (AR pp.13, 80–82).

**Capacity plan (AR pp.62, 67):**
- PEB capacity 147,122 → **220,000 MTPA by end-FY27** (Gujarat Phase 1 & 2).
- Sandwich-panel utilisation **44.1% → 70%+ by FY27-end**; Ghiloth continuous panel line commissioning by Oct-2026.
- FY27 capex **~₹150 cr, fully funded from internal accruals + IPO proceeds, no new debt**; payback 18–24 months; net cash ₹201 cr at FY26-end.
- Capital-allocation principles explicitly stated: "demand before capacity", "internal funding first", "returns with accountability", "transparent reporting" (AR p.67).

**Operating leverage evidence:** PEB utilisation rose 50.3% → 71.7% (AR p.61) while Prefab EBITDA margin held ~10.3%. The panel line is the clearest margin lever — running at 44% while capacity was just doubled; filling it is a high-incremental-margin opportunity.

**Concerns:** the entire plan is volume-led in a commodity-margin business. If steel spikes or demand slips, fixed-price contracts and high operating leverage cut both ways. FY26 EPS margin was helped by a benign cost environment; the order book (₹1,113 cr ≈ 0.73x annual revenue) is not especially long, so visibility is modest.

## 5. Management, governance & incentives

- **Promoters hold 65.0%, zero pledge** (Screener; AR pp.124, 186). Promoter family (Bothra/Singhania) controls the board: Bajrang Bothra (non-exec Chairman), Sanjay Singhania (MD & CEO), Nikhil Bothra (Whole-Time Director), Ajay DD Singhania (Director) (AR pp.124, 135).
- **Remuneration is modest at the top** — MD ₹30.99 lakh, WTD ₹21.50 lakh, CFO ₹21.42 lakh (AR p.118). No stock options granted to directors in FY26.
- **Board attendance is mixed:** Dharam Chand Jain attended 6/9, Manuj Agarwal 6/9; Krishnan Ganesan and Dharam Chand Jain did not attend the last AGM (AR p.124).
- **Related-party transactions are the main governance flag.** Sales to Epack Durable Ltd ₹86.9 cr (FY25 ₹47.2 cr); purchases from East India Technologies ₹37.1 cr; purchases from Epack Petrochem ₹49.3 cr; sales/purchases with Epavo, Decent Softech, Mool Chand Eatables; and substantial "reimbursement of expenses" to promoter relatives (Sanjay Singhania ₹126.7 cr received by the company, Laxmi Pat Bothra ₹175.7 cr) (AR pp.205, 207). These are disclosed and at arm's-length claims, but the group ecosystem is large and growing.
- **No dividend** for FY26 (conserving capital) (AR p.92).
- **Attrition rose to 65.4% from 54.4%** despite the "caring employer" narrative (AR p.61) — a cultural/execution red flag in a services-heavy model.

## 6. Risks / what would invalidate the thesis

1. **Steel-price volatility** in fixed-price contracts — the single biggest earnings risk (AR p.88).
2. **Panel ramp failure** — if utilisation stalls near 44% rather than reaching 70%+, the main margin-expansion lever fails.
3. **Working-capital blow-out** — receivables >2 years of ₹14.6 cr and rising inventory; a large customer default would hit thin PAT.
4. **Client concentration** in the shrinking EPS segment.
5. **Execution risk** on the Gujarat/Ghiloth build-out and the 220,000 MTPA target.
6. **Governance / related-party leakage** — group sales and expense reimbursements could mask value transfer, even if disclosed.
7. **Capital cycle** — PEB is attracting capacity; if the industry over-builds, pricing and utilisation compress together.

## 7. Multibagger math (why it is not one *yet*)

- FY26 PAT ₹92.6 cr, market cap ₹2,420 cr → ~26x earnings.
- Assume PAT compounds 25% for 5 years → ~₹283 cr. At 25x, market cap ~₹7,075 cr = **~2.9x**; at 30x ~3.5x. To reach 5x+ you need 30%+ compounding **and** multiple expansion — i.e. both volume growth and margin expansion must land, with no equity dilution and disciplined working capital.
- The balance sheet (net cash, no-debt FY27 capex) genuinely removes financing risk and makes the plan credible. But the starting multiple already capitalises a lot of that success.

## 8. Open questions / contradictions

- **"Free Operating Cash Flow +143%" (AR p.60) vs true FCF ~₹6 cr** — marketing framing of a cash-hungry model.
- **ROCE/ROE falling** — described as temporary dilution, but unproven until the new assets ramp.
- **Panel capacity doubled while utilisation halved** (71.7%→44.1%) — was the expansion premature or will demand catch up by FY27?
- **EPS segment reported as a strength ("one of the largest in India") but revenue fell 21%.** Management gives no clear turnaround path.
- **Net cash quoted as ₹201 cr (p.60) vs net debt (₹177 cr) on p.87** — definitional inconsistency.
- **No broker/consensus view is contained in the AR**; the valuation judgement above uses Screener market data only.
