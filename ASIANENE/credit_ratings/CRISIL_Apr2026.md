## Detailed Rationale

Crisil Ratings has continued its ratings on the bank facilities of Asian Energy Services Limited (AESL; part of the Asian group) on 'Rating Watch with Developing Implications'. The watch was initially placed on September 09, 2025, following the announcement regarding the proposed scheme of merger of Oilmax Energy Private Limited (OEPL, the parent of AESL) into AESL. While AESL has received board and stock exchange approval, the National Company Law Tribunal (NCLT) has ordered a meeting of shareholders, and the final order is pending. Crisil Ratings will continue to monitor the progress of the merger and resolve the watch with appropriate rating action post-completion upon gaining clarity on OEPL's performance.

The ratings reflect AESL’s long-standing presence, technical capabilities, diversified revenue stream, and comfortable financial risk profile. These strengths are partially offset by the working capital-intensive nature of operations and susceptibility of AESL’s performance to changes in government regulations.

    *   Compounded annual growth (CAGR) of nearly **62%** for the past three fiscal years ending Fiscal 2025.
    *   Reported revenue of **Rs 453 Cr** for the nine months ended December 31, 2025.
    *   Healthy unexecuted order book of **Rs 1874 Cr** as on December 31, 2025.
*   **Diversified Revenue Base:**
    *   Diversification beyond the seismic segment includes EPC operations (from Fiscal 2021) and Operations and Maintenance (O&M) segments.
    *   Acquisition of Kuiper group in H1 of Fiscal 2026 for manpower services in the Middle East.
    *   Year-to-date growth of **81%** in topline for the nine months ended December 31, 2025, compared to the same period last year.
*   **Comfortable Capital Structure:**
    *   Net worth of **Rs 443 Cr** as on September 30, 2025.
    *   Gearing of **0.24 times** as on September 30, 2025, despite debt-funded capex and acquisitions.
    *   Interest Coverage Ratio (ICR) at **7.57 times** for H1 of Fiscal Year 2026.
