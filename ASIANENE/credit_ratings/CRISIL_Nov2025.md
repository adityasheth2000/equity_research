## Detailed Rationale

Crisil Ratings has continued its rating on the long-term bank facilities of Asian Energy Services Ltd (AESL), part of the Asian group, on **'Rating Watch with Developing Implications'**. Additionally, the agency has assigned its **'Crisil A2'** rating to the short-term bank facilities, also placed on the watch.

The watch was initially placed on September 09, 2025, following AESL's announcement regarding the proposed scheme of merger of Oilmax Energy Private Limited (OEPL, the parent) into AESL. While board approval has been received, approvals from shareholders, the Bombay Stock Exchange, and the National Company Law Tribunal are still pending. Crisil Ratings will monitor the merger progress and resolve the watch with appropriate rating action post-completion and upon gaining clarity on OEPL's performance.

The ratings reflect AESL's long-standing presence, strong technical capabilities, established clientele, and healthy financial profile. These strengths are partially offset by exposure to intense competition, government regulations and policies, and the working capital-intensive nature of operations.

---

## Key Rating Drivers — Strengths

*   **Longstanding presence, strong technical capabilities, and established clientele:**
    *   AESL is promoted by Dr. Kapil Garg (Asian Group) with over three decades of experience in upstream oil and gas. Management comprises industry veterans with over two decades of experience.
    *   Customers include major players such as GAIL India Ltd, ONGC Ltd, Oil India Ltd, Vedanta Ltd, Heavy Engineering Corporation, Singareni Coalfields Ltd, and Eastern Coalfield Ltd.
    *   **Order Book:** Healthy order book of **Rs 1687 Cr** provides revenue visibility over the medium term.
*   **Diversified revenue stream:**
    *   Previously heavily dependent on the seismic segment, AESL has diversified into operations & maintenance (O&M) and EPC segments for oil & gas and mining.
    *   Revenue contribution from seismic decreased from **41% (FY24)** to **15% (FY25)**. This diversification provides insulation against sector-specific downturns.
*   **Healthy financial risk profile:**
    *   Net worth exceeds **Rs 398 Cr** with low reliance on external debt.
    *   **Gearing:** Remains below **0.10 times** consecutively for the past three fiscal years.
    *   **Debt Protection Metrics (FY25):** Interest Coverage Ratio at **20.28 times**; Net cash accruals to adjusted debt at **2.52 times**.

---

## Key Rating Drivers — Weaknesses

*   **Government Regulations-Policy:**
    *   The geophysical onshore market is subject to various regulations related to environmental issues, noise pollution, land use, and indigenous rights.
    *   Taxation policies require royalty and tax payments on revenues.
    *   Increased regulatory scrutiny from the Ministry of Petroleum and Natural Gas is expected to raise compliance costs.
*   **Working Capital Intensive Nature of Operations:**
    *   **Gross Current Asset (GCA) Days:** Increased from **229 days (Mar 31, 2024)** to **292 days (Mar 31, 2025)**.
    *   **Debtor Days:** Increased from **163 days (Mar 31, 2024)** to **177 days (Mar 31, 2025)**, driven by higher revenue earned in Q4 FY25.
    *   **Inventory Days:** Doubled to **79 days (Mar 31, 2025)** from **36 days (Mar 31, 2024)** due to significant unbilled work-in-progress.
    *   These requirements are financed by internal accruals, creditors, and bank lines. Sustained improvement in the working capital cycle is a key rating sensitivity factor.

---

## Liquidity

*   **Assessment:** Adequate.
*   **Cash Accruals vs Obligations:** Net cash accruals expected over **Rs 78-80 Cr**, sufficient against repayment obligations of **Rs 1.5-1.75 Cr**.
*   **Cash Position:** Cash equivalent balance of **Rs 105 Cr** (as on May 31, 2025), of which **Rs 69 Cr** is unencumbered.
*   **Bank Limit Utilisation:** Averaged low at **17%** for the past 12 months ended May 2025.
*   **Future Inflows:** Issued 47,00,000 fully convertible equity warrants in FY25. 25% money received; balance expected in FY26 and FY27 upon conversion, leading to a sizeable liquidity surplus.
*   **Post-Acquisition Stabilization:** Stability of operations post-acquisition of Kuiper group and merger remains a monitorable factor.

## Outlook & Rating Sensitivity

**Outlook:** **Rating Watch Developing Implications**

### Upward Factors
*   Sustained increase in the scale of operations leading to net cash accruals over Rs 80 Cr on a sustained basis.
*   Sustained improvement in the working capital cycle, particularly driven by improvement in debtor days.
*   Sustenance of the financial risk profile.

### Downward Factors
*   Moderation in scale or operating margins leading to net cash accruals below Rs 50 Cr on a sustained basis.
*   Further stretch in receivable days leading to higher-than-expected gross current asset days.
*   Higher than expected debt-funded CapEx leading to moderation in the financial risk profile.
