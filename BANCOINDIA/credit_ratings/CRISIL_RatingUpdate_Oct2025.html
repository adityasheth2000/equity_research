Non-cooperation by issuer

Crisil Ratings has been consistently following up with Banco Products India Limited (BPIL) for obtaining information through letters and emails dated September 19, 2025 and September 30, 2025 among others, apart from telephonic communication. However, the issuer has remained non-cooperative.

 

The investors, lenders and all other market participants should exercise due caution with reference to the rating assigned/reviewed with the suffix 'ISSUER NOT COOPERATING' as the rating is arrived at without any management interaction and is based on best available or limited or dated information on the company. Such non-cooperation by a rated entity may be a result of deterioration in its credit risk profile. These ratings with 'ISSUER NOT COOPERATING' suffix lack a forward-looking component.

Detailed rationale

Despite repeated attempts to engage with the management, Crisil Ratings failed to receive any information on either the financial performance or strategic intent of BPIL, which restricts the ability of Crisil Ratings to take a forward-looking view on the entity's credit quality. Crisil Ratings believes that the rating action on BPIL is consistent with ‘Assessing Information Adequacy Risk’. Therefore, on account of inadequate information and lack of management cooperation, Crisil Ratings has migrated the rating on the long-term bank facilities of BPIL to 'Crisil BB+/Stable; Issuer not cooperating' from ‘Crisil AA/Stable’.

Analytical Approach
Crisil Ratings has consolidated the business and financial risk profiles of BPIL with its 100% wholly owned subsidiaries -- BGIL, NRF and Banco New Energy Cooling System Ltd (BNECSL). The consolidated entity is referred to herein as the Banco group.

Please refer Annexure - List of Entities Consolidated, which captures the list of entities considered and their analytical treatment of consolidation.

About the group

Incorporated in 1961, BPIL manufactures engine cooling systems including radiators, change air coolers, oil coolers – transmission and hydraulic, fuel coolers, battery coolers, inverter coolers, in aluminium and copper/brass configurations and accessories expansion tanks, fan motor assembly, fan shroud and guard and miscellaneous mounting and plumbing accessories. Mr Mehul K Patel is the group’s chairman; he is supported by a qualified team. BPIL is listed on Bombay Stock Exchange and National Stock Exchange.

 

Banco Gaskets India Ltd (BGIL) was hived off from BPIL in fiscal 2012 for better operational efficiency. BGIL is a wholly owned subsidiary of BPIL and manufactures metal and rubber gaskets, which have application as a sealing material in automobile and industrial sectors.

 

Nederlandse Radiateuren Fabriek B.V. (NRF) was acquired by the Banco group in fiscal 2010 and is a leading European manufacturer and trader of automotive, industrial, railway and marine, automobile air conditioning and heat exchanger products. Operations and distribution facilities of NRF are located in major western and eastern European countries.

 

Incorporated in May 2021, Banco New Energy Cooling Systems (India) Ltd (BNECSL) is a wholly owned subsidiary of BPIL and manufactures heat exchangers for railway and automotive industries.
