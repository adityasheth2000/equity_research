**Credit Risk Management & Underwriting Policies**
The right-side section details controls on disbursement to borrowers with poor repayment history.

*   **High-Risk Disbursements:** The metric "Disbursement To Borrower With Internal Peak DPD Of 30+ In Preceding 6 Months" remains negligible throughout the period (Q4FY25-Q4FY26), fluctuating strictly between **0.11% and 0.15%**.
*   **Underwriting Rules:**
    *   **Policy:** Customers with 1 Day Past Due (DPD) are precluded from receiving a new loan at the time of disbursement.
    *   **Monitoring:** A Business Rule Engine and Independent Credit team ensure adherence to these rules.
