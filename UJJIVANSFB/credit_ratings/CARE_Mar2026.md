### Key Financial Metrics & Business Profile

*   **Loan Book Growth:** Grew modestly in FY25; rebounded to **15%** in 9MFY26.
*   **Asset Composition (Secured vs Unsecured):**
    *   **Micro-banking Share:** 52% (Targeting reduction).
    *   **Secured Loans:** Rose to **48%** of the loan book as of December 31, 2025 (compared to 44% as of March 31, 2025). Targeted to increase to 60–65% over the medium term.
*   **Asset Quality:**
    *   **Slippages:** Increased to **4.21%** in FY25 and **3.60%** in 9MFY26 (FY24: 2.25%).
    *   **GNPA:** Maintained below **3%**.
    *   **NNPA:** Maintained below **1%**.
    *   *Note:* Asset quality weighed down by stress in micro-banking segment.
*   **Profitability (ROTA - Return on Total Assets):**
    *   FY24: **3.50%**
    *   FY25: Declined to **1.66%**
    *   9MFY26: Further declined to **1.10%**
    *   *Drivers of decline:* Rising secured loan mix, elevated credit costs, interest reversals linked to micro-banking stress, higher operating expenses.
*   **Capitalization:** Described as "comfortable."
*   **Resource Base:** Rated as "moderate, though improving," with a relatively low Current Account Saving Account (CASA) ratio.

### Rationale for Rating Decision

*   **Positive Factors:** Comfortable capitalization, improving scale of loan book, increasing diversification (shift toward secured lending).
*   **Challenges:** Margin compression due to product mix shift (secured loans yield lower returns than unsecured), elevated credit costs.
*   **Constraint:** Ratings remain constrained by the moderate/improving resource profile and low CASA ratio.

### Rating Sensitivities (Factors likely to lead to rating actions)

**Positive Factors (Potential Upgrade):**
*   Significant scale up of business accompanied by meaningful diversification into secured asset class.
*   Sustained improvement in CASA proportion resulting in lower cost of funds.

**Negative Factors (Potential Downgrade):**
*   Significant deterioration in profitability with ROTA remaining below **1%** on a sustained basis.
*   Material deterioration in asset quality.
*   Capital Adequacy Ratio (CAR) remaining below **18%** on a sustained basis.

### Debt Maturity & Structural Details
*   **Total Facilities Mentioned:** ₹11,000 Crore combined across bank facilities, NCDs, and FDs.
*   **Specific Terms/Covenants:** Not detailed in this summary press release.
*   **Analytical Approach:** Standalone (Subsidiary/JV details not highlighted for consolidation purposes).

### Detailed description of key rating drivers

**Key strengths**
*   **Comfortable capitalisation levels:**
    *   **Current Capital Adequacy Ratio (CAR):** 21.62% (Overall) and 20.13% (Tier-I) as of December 31, 2025.
    *   **Supporting Factors:** Internal accruals and periodic equity infusions (including ₹475 crore raised in FY23).
    *   **Guidance:** Management guidance suggests CAR remaining healthy at 17–20% over the medium term.

*   **Scaling up of loanbook with increasing diversification:**
    *   **Loan Growth Trajectory:**
        *   FY23: 33%
        *   FY24: 24%
        *   FY25: 8% (Moderated due to deliberate reduction in unsecured micro-banking).
        *   9MFY26: 15% year-to-date.
    *   **Portfolio Shift (Micro vs. Secured):**
        *   **Dec 31, 2025:** Micro-banking loans at 52%; Secured advances at 48%.
        *   **Mar 31, 2024:** Micro-banking loans at 70%; Secured advances at 30%.
    *   **Future Targets:** Intends to increase secured mix to 65–70%; Compounded Annual Growth Rate (CAGR) target of 20–25% for the loan book.

*   **Portfolio & Geographic Composition (As on Dec 31, 2025):**
    *   **Non-Micro Banking Portfolio Breakup:**
        | Segment | Share |
        | :--- | :--- |
        | Housing Loans | 22% |
        | MSME Loans | 8% |
        | Loans to Financial Institutions | 7% |
        | Micro Mortgage | 4% |
        | Other Segments (Vehicle, Gold, Personal, Staff) | 7% |
    *   **Geographic Presence:** Operations across 24 states and two Union Territories.
    *   **Concentration:** Top three states account for 37% of advances; no single state contributes over 14% (Moderate geographic concentration).

**Key weaknesses**
*   **Significant exposure to microfinance sector (Muted profitability):**
    *   Asset quality moderated in FY25 and 9MFY26 due to stress in the micro-banking portfolio.
    *   **Risks Identified:** Borrower vulnerability, regulatory uncertainties, and socio-political factors associated with unsecured lending.
    *   **Slippage Rates:**
        | Period | Slippage % |
        | :--- | :--- |
        | FY24 | 2.25% |
        | FY25 | 4.21% |
        | 9MFY26 (Annualised) | 3.60% |
    *   **Gross/NPA Metrics:** GNPA and NNPA maintained below 3% and 1% respectively.
        *   **GNPA Trend:** Q3FY25 (2.68%) → Q4FY25 (2.18%) → Q1FY26 (2.52%) → Q2FY26 (2.45%) → Q3FY26 (2.38%).
        *   **Support Mechanisms:** Write-offs, sale of NPAs to asset reconstruction companies, accelerated provisioning.
    *   **Net Stressed Assets Ratio:** 3.5% as of December 31, 2025.
    *   **Profitability Metrics:**
        | Metric | FY25 | 9MFY26 | FY24 |
        | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
        | Return on Total Assets (ROTA) | 1.66% | 1.10% | 3.50% |
    *   **Drivers of Profitability Pressure:** Elevated credit costs, interest reversals, NIM compression (due to secured loan mix shift), higher operating expenses.

*   **Relatively lower CASA proportion:**
    *   **Deposit Base:** Deposits grew 22% year-on-year to **₹42,223 crore** as of December 31, 2025.
    *   **Liability Profile:** Deposits account for 92% of total outside liabilities (TOL).
    *   **CASA Ratio:** Remained modest at **27.32%** (lower than peers).
    *   **Deposit Composition:** CASA and retail term deposits (up to ₹3 crore) constitute 70% of total deposits. Gradual reduction in reliance on wholesale term deposits.

**Liquidity Risk & Financial Metrics**
*   **Liquidity Status:** Adequate. No negative cumulative mismatches in time buckets up to one year per ALM statement as on December 31, 2025.
*   **Regulatory Ratios (as on Quarter-ended Dec 31, 2025):**
    *   **Average Liquidity Coverage Ratio (LCR):** 160% (Regulatory Requirement: 100%).
    *   **Net Stable Funding Ratio (NSFR):** 124% (Regulatory Requirement: 100%).
*   **Statutory Liquidity Ratio (SLR):** Maintained excess SLR of **₹2,515 crore** (representing **6.3%** of Net Demand and Time Liabilities). Can be used for repo or liquidated in secondary markets.
*   **High-Quality Non-SLR Instruments:** Held assets worth **₹1,999 crore**.
*   **Contingent Sources:** Access to Rupee borrowing (CDs, term money, portfolio securitisation, re-finance), Liquidity Adjustment Facility (LAF), Marginal Standing Facility (MSF), and call money market.

**Environment, Social, and Governance (ESG) Assessment**
*   **Environmental Risk:** Direct exposure remains low due to service-oriented nature; however, implicit exposure exists through asset portfolio.
*   **Social Risk:** Key vulnerability identified in **Customer Data Privacy and Security** (breaches could attract regulatory attention/reputation damage). Risk of high costs associated with poor IT strategy execution or failure to meet customer needs.
*   **Positive Factors:** Enhancing financial inclusion through products for marginalized sections.
*   **Governance Structure:** Board comprises **eight Directors**, of whom **six are Independent Directors** and **four are Women Directors**.

**Corporate History & Ownership**
*   **Entity Name:** USFBL (Ujjivan Small Finance Bank Ltd).
*   **Market Position:** Third-largest small finance bank in India.
*   **Incorporation:** July 04, 2016. Originally a subsidiary of Ujjivan Financial Services Limited (UFSL), an NBFC-MFI registered since 2005.
*   **Bank Licensing:** Granted license by RBI on November 11, 2016. Operations commenced February 01, 2017. Assets/liabilities transferred from UFSL via Business Transfer Agreement (BTA).
*   **IPO:** Listed on NSE and BSE on December 12, 2019. Post-IPO shareholding by parent company (UFSL) stood at **83.32%**.

**Recent Strategic Developments & Corporate Actions**
*   **Reverse Merger:** Completed in **May 2024** with parent company UFSL.
    *   **Exchange Ratio:** 116 equity shares of USFBL for every **10 equity shares of UFSL**.
*   **Management Change:** **Sanjeev Nautiyal** appointed as MD and CEO effective **July 01, 2024** (Three-year term), succeeding Ittira Davis.
*   **Universal Banking License Application:** Submitted formal application to RBI on **February 04, 2025** under guidelines for 'on tap' licensing of universal banks in the private sector.
