#### Outlook
**Stable**
Reflects the likely continuation of comfortable capitalisation despite near-term pressures on profitability and asset quality.

#### Key Financial Metrics & Operational Data Cited
*   **Asset Mix:** Microfinance dominated product portfolio at **61%** share. Secured loans increased share to **39%** as on December 31, 2024 (from 30% as on March 31, 2024).
*   **Capitalization:** Supported by regular capital infusions, including Qualified Institutional Placements (QIP) of **₹475 crore** in FY23.
*   **Asset Quality (Slippage):** Increased to **3.6%** in 9MFY25 from **2.3%** in FY24.
*   **Asset Quality (GNPA):** Increased to **2.68%** in December 2024 from **2.23%** in March 2024.
*   **Growth:** Advances growth moderated in 9MFY25.
*   **Resource Profile:** Relatively low Current Account-Saving Account (CASA) ratio.
*   **Profitability Threshold:** Return on Total Assets (ROTA) remains a key metric, with a trigger point set below **1%** for downgrades.
*   **Capital Adequacy Trigger:** Rating downgraded if Capital Adequacy Ratio (CAR) falls below **18%**.

#### Rationale for Rating Action
*   **Strengths:** Established track record in lending; active diversification away from microfinance into secured loans; comfortable capitalisation levels driven by QIP and internal accruals post-COVID.
*   **Weaknesses/Risks:**
    *   Ongoing stress in the microfinance sector impacting asset quality.
    *   Significant write-offs and sale of Non-Performing Assets (NPAs).
    *   Moderated advances growth due to stricter regulatory guardrails and sector stress.
    *   Deteriorated profitability in 9MFY25 due to elevated credit costs and interest reversals linked to asset quality issues.
    *   Expected margin pressure going forward due to a change in product mix favoring lower-margin secured loans.

#### Business & Financial Risk Assessment
*   **Business Risk:** Exposure to microfinance sector stress; reliance on regulatory compliance which may curb disbursement growth; product mix shift toward secured loans which may lower overall yields.
*   **Financial Risk:** Rising slippages and GNPA indicate asset quality deterioration; profit margins under pressure; moderate resource profile with low CASA ratio limits low-cost funding sources.

#### Rating Sensitivities (Triggers)
*   **Positive (Upgrade Triggers):**
    *   Significant scale-up of business and diversification into secured asset class comprising **50%** of the portfolio.
    *   Continuous improvement in CASA proportion on a sustained basis.
*   **Negative (Downgrade Triggers):**
    *   Significant deterioration in profitability with Return on Total Assets (ROTA) remaining below **1%** on a sustained basis.
    *   Material deterioration in asset quality.
    *   Decline in total Capital Adequacy Requirement (CAR) below **18%**.

### Business Risk Profile Assessment & Rating Drivers

**Market Position & Track Record:**
-   USFB has a strong presence in the microfinance sector since 2005 (via erstwhile parent Ujjivan Financial Services Limited).
-   Active diversification strategy: Secured loans share increased from 30% (Mar 31, 2024) to 39% (Dec 31, 2024).
-   Loan Book Composition (as of December 31, 2024):

| Segment | Share of Total Loan Portfolio |
| :--- | :--- |
| Microfinance Loans | 61% |
| Housing Loan Segment | 21% |
| MSE (Micro, Small, Medium Enterprises) Segment | 6% |
| Financial Institution Group (FIG) Segments | 7% |
| Others (Vehicle, Gold, Personal, Staff) | 5% |

**Geographic & Customer Diversification:**
-   **Gross Loan Portfolio:** ₹30,466 crore.
-   **Coverage:** 26 states and union territories.
-   **Customer Base:** 50 lakh active customers.
-   **Concentration:** No single state accounts for over 15% of total; Tamil Nadu is highest at 14% (Dec 31, 2024).
-   **Growth:** Post-reporting strong advances growth of 24% in FY24; growth moderated to 3% (annualised) in 9MFY25 due to regulatory guardrails and sector stress.

**Specific Risks Flagged:**
-   **Sectoral Risk:** Large exposure (61%) to the microfinance sector exposes the bank to inherent socio-economic and geo-political risks, including socio-political intervention and regulatory uncertainties.
-   **Borrower Vulnerability:** Marginal borrower profiles remain vulnerable to economic downturns; cash-based transactions introduce operational risks.
-   **Asset Quality Stress:** Ongoing stress in the microfinance segment leads to elevated slippages and pressure on asset quality/profitability.

### Financial Risk Profile Assessment

**Capitalisation Levels:**
-   **Overall CAR (Capital Adequacy Ratio):** 23.90% as of December 31, 2024.
-   **Tier-I CAR:** 22.10% as of December 31, 2024.
-   **Support:** Driven by internal accruals and periodic equity infusions (specifically ₹475 crore raised in FY23).
-   **Outlook:** Anticipated to remain at healthy levels to absorb potential credit risks.

**Asset Quality Trends:**
-   **GNPA (Gross Non-Performing Assets):** Increased to 2.68% (Dec 31, 2024) from 2.23% (March 31, 2024).
-   **Write-offs / ARC Sales:** Significant write-offs and sale of NPA to ARCs totaled ₹448 crore in 9MFY25 (vs ₹274 crore for FY24).
    -   Specific sales: ₹270 crore in Dec 2024 quarter and ₹365 crore in March 2025 quarter.
-   **Slippages:** Increased to 3.6% in 9MFY25 (majority due to microfinance).
-   **Stressed Assets:**
    -   Gross Stressed Assets (Advances): 2.84% (Dec 31, 2024) vs 2.15% (Mar 31, 2024).
    -   Net Stressed Assets (Net Worth): 4.07% (Dec 31, 2024) vs 1.87% (Mar 31, 2024).

**Operational & Liquidity Analysis**

*   **Liability Mix & Deposits:**
    *   **Deposit Franchise:** Consistently strengthened post-becoming a small finance bank.
    *   **Total Outside Liabilities (TOL):** Deposits accounted for **87.93%** of TOL as on December 31, 2024.
    *   **Deposit Growth:** Deposits grew by **16% year on year** to **₹34,494 crore**.
    *   **CASA Proportion:** Remained relatively low at **25.11%** as on December 31, 2024.
    *   **Deposit Composition:**
        *   CASA plus retail term deposits (up to ₹3 crore) constituted **73%** of the deposit book.
        *   Dependence on wholesale term deposits has been reducing.
    *   **Analyst Expectation:** CARE Ratings expects the bank to focus on raising CASA deposits to provide a stable depositor base and reduce the cost of funding in the medium term.

*   **Liquidity Profile: Adequate**
    *   **Mismatches:** No negative cumulative mismatches in the time bucket up to one year per Structural Liquidity statement as on December 31, 2024.
    *   **Liquidity Coverage Ratio (LCR):** **132%** as on December 31, 2024 (Minimum regulatory requirement: **100%**).
    *   **Statutory Liquidity Ratio (SLR) Investments:** **₹1,639 crore** as on December 31, 2024.
    *   **SLR %:** **23.01%** of Net Demand Time Liabilities (NDTL) (Minimum requirement: **18%**).
    *   **Access to Funding:**
        *   RBI’s Liquidity Adjustment Facility (LAF) and Marginal Standing Facility (MSF).
        *   Refinance from SIDBI, NHB, and NABARD.
        *   Access to call money markets.

**Risk Assessment**

*   **Environmental, Social, and Governance (ESG) Risks:**
    *   **Environmental:** Direct exposure remains low. Bank targeted a **5% reduction in energy consumption** from the previous year and follows e-waste disposal through authorised recyclers.
    *   **Social:** Key vulnerabilities include customer data privacy and security; breaches could attract regulatory attention and damage reputation. Poor execution of IT strategies or failure to meet customer needs could lead to high costs.
    *   **Governance/Prudence:** Enhancing financial inclusion by offering products aimed at marginalised sections. Prudent lending to underserved segments could create growth opportunities but must be weighed against asset quality risks (borrower creditworthiness, economic vulnerabilities, regulatory uncertainties).

    *   **Target Customers:** Salaried and self-employed women.
*   **Regulatory Licensing:**
    *   One of the 10 entities granted "in-principle" approval by Reserve Bank of India (RBI) on September 16, 2015, to set up a bank under the "Guidelines for Licensing of Small Finance Banks in the private sector".
    *   License granted to USFB by RBI on November 11, 2016.
*   **Operations Commencement:** Formally commenced operations on February 01, 2017.
*   **Transition:** Entered terms with Business Transfer Agreement (BTA) effective from February 01, 2017, between UFSL and USFB, wherein entire assets/liabilities of UFSL were transferred to USFB.
