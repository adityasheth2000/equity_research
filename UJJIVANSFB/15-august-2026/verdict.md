# Ujjivan Small Finance Bank (UJJIVANSFB) — Investment Verdict

**Date:** 15 August 2026 · **Price:** ₹72.47 (NSE, 14 Aug 2026) · **52-wk range:** ₹41.32 – 73.29
**Market cap:** ~₹14,000 Cr · **BVPS (Jun'26):** ~₹36.8 · **P/B:** ~2.0x · **P/E (TTM):** ~15x

---

## 1. What the company does (in plain language)

Ujjivan Small Finance Bank is a **bank for India's "working poor" and lower-middle-income customers** — small shopkeepers, weavers, artisans, small farmers, and salaried workers at the bottom of the income pyramid who mainstream banks historically ignored.

It started in 2005 as Ujjivan Financial Services, a microfinance NBFC, giving **small group loans** (average ~₹66,000) to low-income women who borrow together in groups and repay weekly. In 2017 it converted into a **Small Finance Bank** (SFB), which let it also collect **deposits** (savings accounts, fixed deposits) and expand lending.

Today it makes money in two ways, like any bank:
1. **Lending** — it borrows money (from depositors and the market) at ~7% and lends it out at ~17.5% blended yield. The gap (NIM ~8.5%) is its core profit engine.
2. **Fees** — processing fees, insurance distribution, and other income (~₹1,100 Cr in FY26).

Its loan book (₹42,900 Cr, roughly 50/50 split):
- **Micro-banking (~50%)** — its historical core: unsecured microfinance group loans + individual loans at ~22% yield. This is the high-yield but high-risk engine.
- **Secured lending (~50%, growing fast)** — affordable housing loans (₹9,400 Cr), loans against property/MSME (₹3,470 Cr), micro mortgages, gold loans, two-wheeler finance, agri loans, and lending to other financial institutions (FIG). Lower yield (~12%) but much lower risk.

**Customers:** ~1.02 crore customers (Jun'26), of whom ~54 lakh are deposit-only, ~44 lakh have both deposits and loans. It operates **814 branches across 26 states**, with a heavy south/east presence (Karnataka, Tamil Nadu, West Bengal).

**The strategic story in one line:** Ujjivan is deliberately **de-risking** itself — shrinking its dependence on volatile microfinance and building a mainstream, secured, deposit-funded small bank. This is the entire investment thesis.

## 3. Key metrics to track

1. **NIM (~8.5%)** — the engine. Microfinance earns 22%, secured ~12%; as secured mix rises, NIM structurally compresses. Watch whether cost-of-funds declines offset it. Guidance: ~8.5% held.
2. **Secured mix %** — the de-risking barometer. 43.5% (FY25) → 49.4% (FY26) → 50.4% (Q1FY27). Targets: 56% by Mar'27, 65–70% by FY30.
3. **GNPA / NNPA / PCR** — asset-quality health. GNPA 2.17%, NNPA 0.34%, PCR 85% (Q1FY27) — improving.
4. **Credit cost** — the swing factor for profits. 2.4% (FY25) → 2.2% (FY26) → guided 0.9–1.0% of avg assets for FY27.
5. **RoA / RoE** — the output. FY26 RoA 1.4% / RoE 10.9%; Q1FY27 annualized 2.2% / 18.2%; FY27 guided 1.8–2.0%.
6. **CASA ratio & cost of funds** — 28.6% CASA, cost of funds 7.0% (down from 7.5%). Deposit franchise quality determines long-term NIM.
7. **CRAR / capital** — 20.4% (Jun'26). ~₹2,000 Cr equity raise planned; the 20% CAR floor is board-mandated.
8. **MFI collection efficiency (Bucket-X)** — the early-warning signal. 99.68% and stable — the single most watched number during stress.

## 4. Management track record (walked the talk?)

**Hits:**
- **Executed the SFB transition** (2017) and built a ₹45,700 Cr deposit franchise from near-zero, largely funded by retail/CASA.
- **Delivered on the de-risking plan** — secured mix from 35% (FY23) to 50.4% (Q1FY27), ahead of most peers.
- **Navigated COVID + MFI over-indebtedness cycles** and restored asset quality twice (GNPA from 7.1% in FY21/FY22 to 2.2%).
- **Beat FY26 guidance** — delivered RoA 1.4% (top of 1.2–1.4% range) and Q4FY26 PAT +238% YoY.
- **Raised FY27 guidance within one quarter** (RoA 1.6% → 1.8–2.0%) after a strong Q1 — showing responsiveness to momentum.

**Misses:**
- **Profitability collapsed from the FY24 peak**: PAT ₹1,281 Cr (FY24) → ₹726 Cr (FY25, −43%) → ₹693 Cr (FY26, −4.6%). The MFI stress cycle (FY25–FY26) cut PAT nearly in half from peak.
- **FY22 was a ₹415 Cr loss** (COVID-era microfinance stress) — a reminder of the franchise's cyclicality.
- **Universal banking licence rejected** (RBI returned the application in Apr 2026, asking to reapply after further diversification). Management had been "hopeful" of approval — this is a clear miss on a stated goal.
- **FY30 vision requires big execution**: ₹1 lakh Cr advances, 65–70% secured, 35% CASA, RoA 1.8–2% — ambitious, and current RoA (1.4% FY26) is still well short of the target.

**Verdict:** Credible operators with a strong record on execution and asset-quality repair, but the franchise remains hostage to the microfinance cycle. They under-promise and over-deliver on near-term guidance; the long-dated FY30 targets remain unproven.

## 7. Strategy, growth outlook & management quality

**Strategy (de-risking + scale):**
1. **Pivot to secured** — affordable housing, MSME, gold, two-wheeler, agri now 50.4% of book (target 56% by Mar'27, 65–70% by FY30). Secured grew +43% YoY in Q1FY27.
2. **Restrain microfinance deliberately** — MFI growth capped at high single digits (~9–10%), both for portfolio balance and because the borrower pool is still recovering.
3. **Build the liability franchise** — CASA to ~35% by FY30; cost of funds down to 6.9% from 7.5%.
4. **Reapply for universal banking licence** — the endgame that would lower regulatory drag and funding costs, but timing is now "not immediate."

**FY27 guidance (raised in Jul 2026):** 25% advances growth; credit cost 0.9–1.0% of avg assets; **RoA 1.8–2.0%**; NIM ~8.5%; ~140 new branches; secured 56%.

**Management quality:** Experienced, conservative-communicative team (Nautiyal has steered the bank since the Ujjivan group era). They flag risks candidly, guide conservatively and beat, and have proven execution on the diversification pivot. Governance is strong (78% independent board, ICRA ESG 80 "Outstanding"). The one concern is bench depth after a couple of senior exits (Business Head-MSME resigned Jan 2026; Head of Strategy appointed Aug 2025).

## 8. Risks

1. **Microfinance cyclicality** — ~50% of the book is still unsecured microfinance. A fresh over-indebtedness or economic shock (El Niño-driven rural distress, election disruptions) could re-trigger a credit-cost spike, as seen in FY22 and FY25.
2. **NIM compression** — the very de-risking strategy dilutes yields (secured ~12% vs MFI ~22%). If funding-cost relief stalls, NIM could break below 8%.
3. **Universal bank licence uncertainty** — RBI returned the application; reapplication timing and terms are unknown. This caps the re-rating story until resolved.
4. **Capital raise dilution** — ~₹2,000 Cr equity raise (QIP/preferential) is planned for H2FY27, ~14% of current market cap. Accretive if deployed at 25% growth, but dilutive near-term.
5. **Credit rating trajectory** — both agencies hold steady (CARE AA-/Stable, CRISIL A1+), but CARE explicitly flagged that **ROTA at 1.10% breached its 1% sustainability threshold** (Mar'26 report) and listed ROTA <1% or CAR <18% as downgrade triggers. Ratings are stable but "watch the profitability floor."
6. **Deposit competition & cost of funds** — the bank acknowledged tight liquidity; bulk deposits are ~30% of the base and rising.
7. **MSME asset quality** — MSME GNPA at ~3.7% (though new-book delinquency is negligible; the legacy book is the drag).
8. **Execution risk on FY30 vision** — ₹1 lakh Cr advances, 65–70% secured, 35% CASA, RoA 1.8–2% all require flawless multi-year execution.

- **P/E (TTM) ~15x** on a recovering earnings base; **forward P/E ~11x** if FY27 PAT lands near ₹1,200–1,300 Cr (management's 1.8–2.0% RoA implies this). This is the bull case — earnings are inflecting while the multiple is yet to fully reflect it.
- **P/B ~2.0x** is fair-to-rich for a bank earning ~15% RoE; it re-rates to justify the multiple only if RoE sustains at 18%+.
- **Consensus:** average target ₹82 (~13% upside), bull targets ₹90 (Investec/Nuvama/ICICI, ~24–31% upside), low ₹60.
- **Why the re-rating could continue:** the market is paying for the *transition* from cyclical microfinance lender to diversified SFB. Every quarter of improving asset quality + rising secured mix de-risks the franchise and supports a higher multiple. **Why it might not:** the core business still cyclically produces 7% RoE in bad years, and the universal-bank catalyst has been pushed out.

## 10. Key monitorables (what would change the view)

1. **MFI Bucket-X collection efficiency** staying ≥99.5% — any slip below 99% is an early red flag.
2. **Credit cost trajectory** — delivered 0.9% in Q1FY27 vs guided 0.9–1.0%; sustaining this is the key to the RoA upgrade.
3. **NIM holding ~8.5%** as secured mix rises toward 56%.
4. **Secured book GNPA** (housing 1.2%, MSME new book ~0.5%, gold 0.2%) — proving the new engine is clean as it scales.
5. **Capital raise terms & timing** (QIP size, price, dilution) in H2FY27.
6. **Universal-bank reapplication** and any RBI signal.
7. **Micro-mortgage seasoning** — PAR has ticked up (0.47% → 0.55% GNPA) as the young book matures; worth watching.
8. **CASA & cost-of-funds** progress toward the 35% CASA / sub-7% funding-cost targets.

## Bottom line

Ujjivan SFB is a **high-quality operator in the middle of a successful de-risking transformation**, now showing five consecutive quarters of accelerating earnings (Q1FY27 PAT ₹317 Cr, RoE 18.2%) on the back of repaired asset quality and a fast-growing secured book. The near-term momentum and management's conservative-then-raised guidance are encouraging.

The stock at ~15x TTM / ~11x forward earnings and ~2x book is **reasonably valued for the current inflecting earnings, but not obviously cheap**. The bull case rests on FY27 RoA delivering at the top of the 1.8–2.0% guide and the secured pivot compounding; the bear case is that a fresh microfinance downturn (the franchise's recurring Achilles heel) or NIM compression from the mix shift erodes the earnings recovery — and the universal-bank re-rating catalyst has been deferred by RBI.

**Net: a quality, improving franchise — attractive on earnings momentum and asset-quality repair, but with the structural caveat that ~half the book is still cyclical unsecured microfinance and the balance of the re-rating story (universal licence, 18%+ sustainable RoE) remains to be proven.**
