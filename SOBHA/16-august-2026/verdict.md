## What Sobha Does

Sobha is a **luxury residential real estate developer** based in Bengaluru. Think of them as a company that buys land, designs apartment complexes (from 2BHK to 4BHK and villas), builds them from scratch, and sells individual homes to buyers. What makes Sobha unusual is their **backward-integrated model**: they don't just contract out construction — they own factories that make the concrete blocks, the window glass, the metal door frames, the interior woodwork, even the mattresses. This means they control quality from start to finish, similar to a vertically-integrated manufacturer.

Their customers are primarily **end-users** (families buying homes to live in), not speculators. They sell apartments priced mostly between ₹2–5 crore (~USD $240K–600K), putting them in the premium-to-luxury segment. About **81% of revenue** comes from selling residential apartments; the rest is from their captive factories doing outsourced work (glazing, interiors, concrete) and commercial rental income.

They operate in **13 cities across 8 states**, but are heavily concentrated in **Bengaluru (~55% of sales)** and the **NCR region of Gurgaon/Greater Noida (~30%)**. They've built over 154 million sq ft of completed area across 595 developments. Their average delivery rate has been roughly 7 million sq ft per year over the last decade. They also have a small commercial rental portfolio (1.3M sq ft leasable) generating ~₹52 Cr in NOI.

**Revenue trend:** Revenue has grown at a 19.7% 5-year CAGR from ₹2,110 Cr in FY21 to ₹5,190 Cr in FY26. But this is an accounting reflection of projects sold 2–4 years ago. Pre-sales (the real business momentum) grew 30% in FY26 to ₹8,136 Cr, and revenue recognizes with a lag.

**The margin story is the crux.** OPM has collapsed from 32% in FY21 to 6% in FY26. This is because:
1. Projects completing now were sold in FY22–FY23 when the market was weaker and Sobha was clearing inventory at lower margins.
2. The interest cost on that older debt (₹3,052 Cr in FY21 at ~9%) depressed the P&L.
3. Revenue recognition delays (OC approvals) mean fixed costs get spread over less recognized revenue.

The unrecognized revenue book of ₹18,647 Cr (as of Mar 2026), later ₹20,553 Cr (as of Jun 2026), carries blended project-level EBITDA margins of ~30%. As these projects complete and revenue recognizes, consolidated EBITDA margins should expand. Management guides 24–26% EBITDA on projects completing in the next 12 months and 17–20% consolidated EBITDA by Q4 FY27 exit (vs 9.3% in FY26).

## Strategy, Growth Outlook & Management Quality

### Strategy

1. **Supply-led growth:** Launch more to sell more. Sobha targets ~10 Mn sft of new launches annually (GDV ~₹15,000 Cr at current realizations). The pipeline is ~20.77 Mn sft forthcoming.

2. **Geographic diversification with Bengaluru core:** Bengaluru remains 55% of sales, but NCR has grown to 30%. New markets: Mumbai (entry via Inizio + a new 1.3-acre land buy), Hyderabad, and deeper NCR (Greater Noida).

3. **Backward-integrated execution:** Own manufacturing keeps quality high and delivery predictable. This is the moat.

4. **Capital discipline:** Net cash position, AA- credit rating, low D/E. Management emphasizes "prudent capital allocation" and opportunistic land buys.

5. **Product mix shift to mid-premium:** ₹2–5 Cr ticket sizes are now 73% of sales (up from ~60% in FY22). The sub-₹2 Cr segment has shrunk from 72% to 16% over 5 years.

### Growth Outlook

- **FY27 pre-sales:** ₹10,600 Cr (+30%). Q1 delivered ₹3,656 Cr — well on track if launches execute.
- **FY27 launches:** ~10 Mn sft across 12 projects (9 identified for next 9 months = 8.2 Mn sft + 1-2 from Mumbai pipeline).
- **FY27 EBITDA margin:** 17–20% by Q4 exit (vs 9.3% in FY26). This is the single most important number to track.
- **FY27 operating cash flow:** ~₹2,000 Cr target.
- **Medium term:** Unrecognized revenue of ₹20,553 Cr provides 3+ years of P&L visibility.

### Management Quality

- Professional management (MD Jagadish Nangineni is a career real estate executive, not family). Promoter PNC Menon holds 52.88% but day-to-day operations are professional.
- Transparent communication: detailed cash flow statements, project-level margin disclosures, candid about launch delays.
- Conservative balance sheet: net cash, no promoter pledge, low leverage.
- **Weakness:** Pattern of over-promising on launch timelines and margin improvement timing. The "margins will improve next year" narrative has been running since FY24. Q3/Q4 FY27 will be a credibility test.

**What would change my mind (Bear):** If Q2 FY27 pre-sales drop below ₹1,800 Cr (indicating Hoskote/Crescent were one-off launch boosts, not sustained demand), the growth thesis breaks and the stock derates to book-value multiples like other mid-cap developers.

## Risks

### 1. Execution Risk (Launch Delays)
FY26 launches came in at 6.04 Mn sft against a ~10 Mn sft ambition. If RERA, TDR, or design-change delays persist, FY27 launches could miss again, directly capping pre-sales growth. Management acknowledged this pattern.

### 2. Margin Recovery Timing
The entire bull case requires EBITDA margins to nearly double (9.3% → 17–20%). The gap between "unrecognized book margins" (30%) and "recognized margins" (12% in 9M FY26) has persisted for multiple quarters. If OC delays or cost inflation push the inflection further into FY28, the stock's expensive valuation becomes harder to justify.

### 3. Geographic Concentration
Bengaluru = 55% of sales, 50% of land bank. Any Bengaluru-specific shock (IT layoffs, policy changes, FSI rules) hits disproportionately. The NCR expansion is a hedge but still in early stages.

### 4. Commodity Cost & Labor Inflation
Steel, cement, and labor are key inputs. Karnataka's proposed 60% minimum-wage hike could pressure project margins. Management says it's "absorbable within project budgets," but unquantified.

### 5. Credit Rating Trajectory
- **ICRA AA- (Stable):** Assigned Dec 2025. No detailed rationale available (report gated behind subscriber login). The "Stable" outlook suggests ICRA sees no imminent deterioration.
- **Ind-RA AA- (Positive Outlook):** Outlook upgraded from Stable — reflects improving leverage and cash flows.
- **Implication:** Credit profile is strong and improving. Debt is only ₹1,002 Cr vs ₹1,802 Cr cash. No refinancing risk. NCD issuance of ₹1,000 Cr is approved but would be for growth, not distress. The rating trajectory is a tailwind — if Ind-RA upgrades to AA, cost of debt could decline further from 7.62%.

### 6. Regulatory & Tax
- PMLA attachment of ₹201.6 Cr (announced Jan 2026) — nature unclear, needs monitoring.
- GST orders with penalties (various dates) — company is contesting.
- IT demand of ₹67.2 Cr (Q3 FY26).
- Potential change in Karnataka FSI rules (in various forums, no clarity yet).

### 7. Demand Cycle Risk
The Indian residential cycle has been strong since FY21. If interest rates rise or economic growth slows, luxury/premium housing (discretionary purchases) is the first segment to soften. Management noted "pockets of concern" in Gurgaon and "investor demand down."

## Key Monitorables (Next 2–4 Quarters)

1. **Q2 FY27 pre-sales** (Oct 2026 result): Needs to show ₹2,000+ Cr in a non-mega-launch quarter to confirm demand sustainability beyond One World/Crescent.

2. **Q3 FY27 EBITDA margin** (Jan 2026 result): Management said "Q3/Q4 FY27 to look far better." If Q3 EBITDA margin doesn't cross 12–13% (from 8–9% currently), the inflection is delayed.

3. **FY27 launch pipeline execution:** 9 projects / 8.2 Mn sft over next 9 months. Track RERA registration dates vs guidance. Any slippage here directly impacts pre-sales.

4. **Net cash / net debt trajectory:** Ended Q1 FY27 at ₹659 Cr net cash. Watch for Q2/Q3 land spends — if crossing ₹1,500 Cr annual run-rate, net cash erodes faster than expected.

5. **Karnataka minimum wage impact:** Quantified cost impact expected in Q2/Q3. If >2% of project cost, it's a margin headwind.

6. **Mumbai expansion:** The 1.3-acre land buy (₹180 Cr) is expensive per-acre. Track launch timeline and pre-sales response. A Mumbai success opens a large TAM; a flop is a distraction.

7. **S&M spend:** Already up 84% YoY in FY26. If it keeps rising faster than pre-sales, it signals demand-push rather than demand-pull.
