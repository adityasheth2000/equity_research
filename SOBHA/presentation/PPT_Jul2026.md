**A Journey of Excellence (Corporate History & Strategic Milestones)**

**Foundational Growth & Vertical Integration**
*   **1995:** Company incorporated on 7 August 1995.
*   **1997:** Launched first residential project (*SOBHA Sapphire*, Bengaluru); expanded presence to Coimbatore.
*   **1998:** First Indian real estate company to obtain ISO 9001 certification.
*   **2000:** Established vertical integration with the creation of **SOBHA Glazing and Metal Works Division** and **SOBHA Interiors Division**; completed first corporate contractual project for Infosys (Bengaluru).
*   **2005:** **SOBHA Concrete Products Division** commenced operations.

**Public Listing & Product Diversification**
*   **2006:** Went public with an IPO oversubscribed by **126x**; established CSR arm.
*   **2007:** Expanded to Pune with *SOBHA Carnation*; established SOBHA Restoplus (suggests development of commercial/retail amenities within projects).
*   **2008:** Entered Thrissur with *SOBHA City* (Kerala’s first township project); established SOBHA Academy.
*   **2009:** Achieved ISO certifications for environmental and safety standards.

**Geographic Expansion**
*   **2011:** Expanded to **Delhi-NCR** and Mysuru.
*   **2012:** Entered **Chennai**.
*   **2014:** Expanded to Kozhikode.
*   **2016:** Expanded to Kochi with *Marina One*.
*   **2019:** Entered **Gujarat**.
*   **2022:** Expanded to Thiruvananthapuram.
*   **2023:** Entered **Hyderabad** with *SOBHA Waterfront*.
*   **2026 (Projected):** Plans to enter **Greater Noida & Mumbai** with *SOBHA Aurum* & *SOBHA Inizio*.

**Financial & Capital Events**
*   **2024:** Successfully raised **Rs 20 billion** via a rights issue.

**Competitive Positioning & ESG**
*   **2014:** CEO Mr. Ravi PNC Menon received the ‘EY Entrepreneur of the Year’ Award.
*   **2019:** Ranked as India’s ‘Top National Realty Brand’ for the fifth consecutive year (Track2Realty’s BrandXReport 2018–19).
*   **2022:** *SOBHA City, Thrissur* recognized as India’s First **‘Net Water Positive (Platinum)’ Operational Project**, highlighting strong ESG credentials.
