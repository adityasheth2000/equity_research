*   **Product Mix Strategy:** In FY26, the mix is shifting ("gyrating") towards the "sweet spot" of **₹2 Cr. to ₹5 Cr.** driven by new launches like **SOBHA Aurum, SOBHA Magnus,** and inventory clearance in **SOBHA Townpark**.
