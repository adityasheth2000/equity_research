## Detailed Rationale

Crisil Ratings has revised the outlook on the long-term bank facility of OSEL Devices Ltd (OSEL) to 'Positive' from 'Stable' while reaffirming the rating at 'Crisil BBB'. This adjustment reflects the company's expected improvement in business risk profile, driven by strong revenue growth and a healthy financial position following capital infusion.

Revenue in Fiscal 2025 grew by 41% year-on-year to Rs 186 crore, driven by volume growth and a diversified clientele. OSEL has achieved Rs 147 crore in the first six months of Fiscal 2026 alone, with full-year expectations ranging between Rs 300-310 crore. This growth is supported by a diversified product mix (hearing aids and LED display systems) and a strategic partnership with Philips to introduce feature phones.

The financial risk profile has strengthened significantly following an IPO in September 2024 (raising Rs 63 crore) and a preferential issue in September 2025 (raising Rs 54 crore). These funds have facilitated debt reduction and supported working capital requirements, improving networth to Rs 108 crore as of March 31, 2025. Operating margins have remained stable at 17-18%.

Key risks acknowledged include exposure to technological obsolescence, raw material price fluctuations (specifically imports from China), and a large working capital requirement. Networth is expected to reach Rs 190-195 crore by March 31, 2026.

---

## Key Rating Drivers — Strengths

*   **Extensive Promoter Experience:** Promoters possess approximately two decades of experience in the healthcare equipment industry, fostering strong relationships and market understanding.
*   **Revenue Growth:** Operating income has grown at a Compound Annual Growth Rate (CAGR) of 42% over the last three fiscals, with projected FY2026 revenue of Rs 300-310 crore.
*   **Product Diversification:** A balanced mix of products contributes to revenue: LED display systems (~46%) and Hearing aids (~54%) in FY2025.
*   **Strategic Partnerships:** Introduction of feature phones via a partnership with Philips is expected to strengthen the business risk profile and support near-to-medium-term revenue growth.
*   **Healthy Financial Profile:**
    *   **Networth:** Expected to rise to Rs 190-195 crore by March 31, 2026 (vs Rs 108 crore as of March 31, 2025).
    *   **Gearing:** Expected to remain below 0.5 times as of March 31, 2026 (0.46 times as of March 31, 2025).
    *   **Interest Coverage:** Projected at 7-8 times in FY26 (9.6 times in FY2025).
*   **Strong Clientele:** Access to both private and government customers supports revenue stability.

## Key Rating Drivers — Weaknesses

*   **Technology Obsolescence:** Intense competition and rapid innovation lead to bargaining power constraints and margin fluctuations based on tenders.
*   **Raw Material Exposure:** Key raw materials are procured from China, exposing the company to volatility in prices and supply chain issues.
*   **Working Capital Intensity:**
    *   **Gross Current Assets (GCA):** 350 days as of March 31, 2025 (vs 219 days a year earlier).
    *   **Inventory:** 97 days (85 days a year earlier).
    *   **Receivables:** 128 days as of March 31, 2025 (73 days a year earlier).
    *   **Payables:** 60-90 days as of March 31, 2025.
    *   GCA is expected to remain high (370-380 days) in FY26 due to large cash/bank balances and inventory requirements for customizations.

## Liquidity

*   **Assessment:** Adequate.
*   **Cash Accrual:** Expected net cash accrual of Rs 30-35 crore per annum is sufficient to cover working capital requirements.
*   **Debt Obligation:** Yearly term debt obligation is Rs 0.5-1.0 crore.
*   **Cash Balance:** Rs 32 crore as of September 30, 2025.
*   **Bank Limit Utilisation:** Averaged 77% for the 12 months through September 2025.
*   **Current Ratio:** Expected at 2.1 times as of March 31, 2026.
