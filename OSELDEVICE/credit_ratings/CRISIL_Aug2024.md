# Credit Rating Report: OSEL Devices Limited

## Rating Action
*   **Rating Agency:** CRISIL Ratings Limited
*   **Date of Report:** August 31, 2024
*   **Credit Rating:** `CRISIL BBB/Stable`
*   **Facility Type:** Long-term Bank Debt
*   **Total Facilities Rated:** Rs. 45 Crore
*   **Entity:** OSEL Devices Limited (ODL)

---

## Detailed Rationale
CRISIL Ratings has assigned its 'CRISIL BBB/Stable' rating to the long-term bank facilities of OSEL Devices Ltd (ODL). The rating reflects the extensive experience of the promoters in the healthcare equipment industry, the improving scale of operations of the company, its sound operating efficiency, and a healthy financial risk profile. These strengths are partially offset by exposure to risks related to technological obsolescence and raw material price fluctuations, and moderate working capital requirements.

---

## Key Rating Drivers — Strengths

*   **Extensive Experience of Promoters:** The promoters possess approximately two decades of experience in the healthcare equipment industry. The company transitioned from trading (until 2019) to manufacturing LED display systems and hearing aids in 2019, leveraging market dynamics knowledge to build supplier and customer relationships.
*   **Improving Scale of Operations:**
    *   Operating income grew at a healthy CAGR of 32% for the past three fiscal years through 2024.
    *   Estimated operating income for fiscal 2024 is Rs 132.25 crore, expected to improve by 30-35% in fiscal 2025 due to increased capacity utilization.
*   **Strong Clientele:** Maintains relationships with prominent private and government entities, including Artificial Limbs Manufacturing Corporation of India, PVR Ltd, Lulu Mall, and the Indian Space Research Organisation (ISRO).
*   **Healthy Financial Risk Profile:**
    *   **Gearing:** ~1.02 times (as on March 31, 2024), expected to improve to 0.8 times by March 31, 2025.
    *   **TOLANW:** Total outside liabilities to adjusted networth ratio was 2.04 times (March 31, 2024), expected to decline to 1.5 times.
    *   **Debt Coverage:** Interest coverage ratio of 6.5 times and Net cash accrual to adjusted debt ratio of 0.5 times (fiscal 2024).

## Key Rating Drivers — Weaknesses

*   **Technology Obsolescence & Raw Material Prices:**
    *   Intense competition leads to rapid product evolution, impacting bargaining power and causing operating margin fluctuations based on tenders.
    *   Key raw materials are procured from China; volatility in prices or supply chain issues could impact operations.
*   **Moderate Working Capital Requirement:**
    *   **GCA Days:** 157 days as on March 31, 2024 (187 days in fiscal 2023).
    *   **Inventory:** Held for 82 days (mostly raw material imported from China to handle orders).
    *   **Receivables:** 70 days as on March 31, 2024 (57 days in fiscal 2023).
    *   Supported partially by payables of 60-90 days.

---

## Liquidity
*   **Assessment:** Adequate
*   **Cash Accruals:** Generated Rs 13.1 crore in fiscal 2024. Expected to generate Rs 18-21 crore over the medium term.
*   **Debt Obligation:** Yearly debt obligation is around Rs 1.6 crore.
*   **Bank Limit Utilization:** Moderate utilization at 78.5% for the 12 months through June 2024.
*   **Enhancement:** Company availed enhancement in bank lines in August 2024 to cushion liquidity.
*   **Current Ratio:** Adequate at 1.4 times (as on March 31, 2024).

---

## Outlook & Rating Sensitivity
*   **Outlook:** Stable
*   **Upward Factors:**
    *   Increase in revenue by more than 30% aided by higher capacity utilization.
    *   Stable operating margin of 15-16% leading to higher cash accrual.
    *   Sustenance of financial risk profile.
*   **Downward Factors:**
    *   Decline in revenue by more than 30% and fall in operating margin below 10% leading to lower net cash accrual.
    *   Further stretch in the working capital cycle or large, debt-funded capital expenditure weakening the liquidity and financial risk profile.
