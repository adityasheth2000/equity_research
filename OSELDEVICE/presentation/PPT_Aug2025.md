**Slide Topic: Hearing Aid Demand / India’s Stark Reality / Global Landscape**

**India Market Metrics & Infrastructure**
*   **Total Population:** 1.3 billion people
*   **Hearing Loss Prevalence:** Estimated 7% of the population
*   **Market Volume (Volume):** India sells 700,000 hearing aids annually
*   **Treatment Penetration:** < 3% (Treatment remains under 3%); Shockingly, <0.5% have taken steps to address hearing loss
*   **Bilateral Penetration:** Of those who treat, only 10% use two hearing aids
*   **Medical Infrastructure:** India has only one audiologist per 500,000 people

**Global Market Context**
*   **Total Addressable Market (TAM):** 488 million people suffer from hearing loss worldwide
*   **Global Adoption Rate:** Hearing aid adoption remains under 10%

**Market Barriers & Risks**
*   **Primary Barriers:** Access, affordability, and stigma
*   **Perceptual Risks:** Hearing aids are wrongly perceived as outdated or only suitable for the elderly
*   **Operational Gap:** Massive gap in hearing care awareness and action exists (India)

**Source Citations**
*   *The Economic Times Health / Audientes* (indicates reliance on external industry reports for these estimates)
