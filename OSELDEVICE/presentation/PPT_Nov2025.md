## Page 16

**Topic: Hearing Aid Market Opportunity & Landscape Analysis (India & Global)**

**India Market Fundamentals**
*   **Population Base:** 1.3 billion people.
*   **Addressable Market (Hearing Loss Prevalence):** Estimated 7% of the population affected.
*   **Current Treatment Penetration:** Treatment remains under 3% despite demand.
*   **Annual Unit Volume:** India sells 700,000 hearing aids annually.
*   **Actionable Demand Gap:** Shockingly, <0.5% of the affected population have taken steps to address hearing loss.
*   **Utilization Gap:** Of those seeking help, only 10% utilize two hearing aids (suggests potential for bilateral fitting up-selling).

**Supply-Side Constraints**
*   **Doctor/Audiologist Density:** India faces a massive shortage with only **one audiologist per 500,000 people**. This suggests a potential market opportunity for automated or digital hearing solutions that rely less on clinical intervention.

**Global Landscape**
*   **Global Prevalence:** 488 million people suffer from hearing loss worldwide.
*   **Global Adoption Rate:** Hearing aid adoption remains under 10% globally.
*   **Market Barriers to Entry:** Primary barriers identified as access, affordability, and stigma.
*   **Consumer Perception Risk:** Hearing aids are often wrongly perceived as outdated or exclusively for the elderly, representing a branding/education challenge the company must overcome.

**Strategic Implications**
*   **Growth Visibility:** The slide highlights a "massive gap in hearing care awareness," implying significant growth potential if education and stigma reduction strategies are successful.
*   **Market Efficiency:** The low audiologist density combined with high hearing loss prevalence suggests a structural inefficiency that a scalable business model could disrupt.
