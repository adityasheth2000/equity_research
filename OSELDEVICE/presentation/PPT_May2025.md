## Page 12

**Market Landscape and Penetration Metrics (India & Global)**

**India Market Opportunity & Reality**
*   **Total Addressable Market (TAM) - Demographics:** India has a population of 1.3 billion with an estimated 7% affected by hearing loss.
*   **Market Penetration & Adoption:**
    *   Treatment penetration remains under 3% of the total population.
    *   Less than 0.5% of the population has taken steps to address hearing loss.
    *   Of those who seek treatment, only 10% use two hearing aids (bilateral use).
*   **Sales Volume:** India sells approximately 700,000 hearing aids annually.
*   **Supply Chain Constraints:** There is a scarcity of healthcare providers, with only one audiologist per 500,000 people.
*   **Awareness Gap:** There is a noted massive gap between hearing care awareness and actual action.

**Global Industry Context**
*   **Global TAM:** 488 million people worldwide suffer from hearing loss.
*   **Global Adoption:** Hearing aid adoption remains under 10%.

**Key Market Barriers & Risks**
*   **Primary Barriers:** Access, affordability, and stigma are identified as key impediments to growth.
*   **Perception:** Hearing aids are currently perceived by the market as "outdated" or only intended for the elderly, representing a psychographic barrier.

*   **Strategy:** Rigorous testing protocols exceeding industry standards.
*   **Impact:** Ensures high product reliability and reduces post-installation service requirements.

**5. Strategic Moat Summary**
*   **Vertical Integration:** The company controls critical value chain elements from procurement through maintenance.
*   **Margin Profile:** This integrated approach allows the company to capture higher margins than competitors who outsource these functions while delivering superior customer value.
