**CERTIFICATIONS & REGULATORY MOAT**

This slide outlines the company's regulatory "moat," detailing specific certifications that grant access to key markets (domestic and international) and enable participation in government subsidy schemes.

*   **Export Capability (Hearing Aids & Medical Devices):**
    *   **USFDA Registration:** Described as the "key to the world's largest hearing-aid market." This credential allows access to the high-value US market, suggesting significant export revenue potential for this segment.

*   **Domestic Market Access & Government Benefits:**
    *   **ZED Gold (Zero Defect Zero Effect):** A top-tier manufacturing credential that opens eligibility for **PLI (Production Linked Incentive)** schemes and "Make-in-India" benefits. This is material for financial modeling regarding potential government subsidies.
    *   **BIS (Bureau of Indian Standards) – Hearing Aids:** Identified as a "legal prerequisite" for selling hearing aids in India, effectively gating market entry.
    *   **BIS – Visual Display Units:** Mandatory for "government and institutional LED procurement," ensuring market access to large institutional buyers.

*   **General Quality Standards:**
    *   **ISO 9001:** Quality management mandated by many export and institutional buyers.
