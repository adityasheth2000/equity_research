# CPPLUS (Aditya Infotech Ltd) — Investment Analysis

**Date:** 10 July 2026 | **CMP:** ₹3,644 | **Mkt Cap:** ₹42,918 Cr | **P/E:** 116x

---

## 1. Company Overview & Industry Positioning

Aditya Infotech Ltd (NSE: CPPLUS) is India's largest video surveillance company, operating under the flagship **CP PLUS** brand. The company designs, manufactures, and distributes CCTV cameras, network video recorders (NVRs), access control systems, and AI-powered surveillance solutions. It also serves as the exclusive Indian distributor for **Dahua Technology**, a leading global player.

**Market Position (As of Q3 FY26):**
- ~45.4% market share in Indian video surveillance (up from ~30% pre-STQC)
- 3rd largest surveillance manufacturer globally (by units)
- 800+ distributors across 500+ cities; 141 Galaxy brand stores
- 2,900+ SKUs across varied price points

**Regulatory Moat:** The Indian government's STQC certification mandate and Public Procurement Order (PPO) favouring domestic manufacturers have driven market consolidation away from Chinese players (Hikvision, etc.), creating a structural advantage. CP PLUS claims the "largest range of STQC-certified products" in India.

## 3. Strategic Priorities & Growth Outlook

### Guidance (FY27)

| Metric | FY26 Actual | FY27 Guidance (Latest) |
|:---|:---|:---|
| Revenue | ₹4,221 Cr | ₹6,000–6,500 Cr |
| Revenue Growth | 35.6% | 40–55% |
| EBITDA Margin | 13.7% | 14–15% |
| PAT Margin | 8.7% | 8.5–9.5% |

Management highlights 25% growth from ASP hikes (component price pass-through) and 25% from volume scaling. This implies meaningful pricing power but also inflationary risk.

### Key Drivers

1. **STQC Consolidation:** Non-STQC Chinese brands continue exiting the market. CP PLUS is the primary beneficiary.
2. **AI & Technology:** Qualcomm partnership for AI-enabled edge surveillance. L&T Semiconductor deal for 9M indigenous SoC-powered cameras over 3 years.
3. **Manufacturing Scale:** Capacity at 2.5M units/month, targeting 2x by FY28. Housing plant Phase 1 by Q2 FY27. Lens assembly line with 500K units/month initial capacity.
4. **Backward Integration:** JV with Orient Cables for cable manufacturing. In-house housing/enclosure plant. Lens auto-assembly. Localization of IR LEDs, SMD boards, WiFi modules.
5. **Multi-Brand Strategy:** NEXIVUE (mass market post-STQC vacuum) and EYRA brands launched alongside premium CP PLUS.
6. **R&D Expansion:** Taiwan R&D centre operational. Bangalore centre upcoming. DSIR-certified labs in Noida and Ahmedabad.

### Risks

1. **Sustainable Growth Questions:** FY27 guidance implies 40–55% revenue growth, with ~half from price hikes. If component prices stabilize or competition intensifies, price-led growth may moderate.
2. **Working Capital:** Operating cash flow remains weak (₹13 Cr in FY26). High debtor/inventory days are structural. Without improvement, the business will continue consuming cash as it scales.
3. **Supplier Concentration:** ~40–50% of products imported from Dahua. Any change in this relationship is a material risk.
4. **Credit Rating Trajectory:** CARE downgraded from A-/A2+ to BBB+/A2 between 2024-2026. CRISIL assigned A/Stable (a notch higher), but the CARE trajectory warrants attention.
5. **Forex & Component Volatility:** Majority of raw materials imported. Ongoing DDR3 discontinuation and semiconductor shortages are pushing costs higher.
6. **Competition:** Honeywell, Syrma SGS, Kaynes Tech and other EMS/electronics players are investing in similar capabilities. Barriers to entry in assembly are moderate.
7. **Execution Risk:** The company is attempting simultaneous expansion across manufacturing, R&D, branding, channel, and new product categories. Management bandwidth dilution is possible.

## 6. Valuation Context

- **TTM P/E:** 116x on adjusted PAT of ₹368 Cr (EPS ~₹31.2)
- **FY27E P/E:** 69–84x on guided PAT ₹510–618 Cr
- **Price/Book:** ~23x on net worth ₹1,877 Cr
- **EV/EBITDA:** ~74x on FY26 EBITDA ₹579 Cr
- **Market Cap/Sales:** ~10x on FY26 revenue

The stock IPO'd in late 2025 (BSE: 544466) and has had limited price history. At ₹3,644, it's up significantly from the listing price (~₹1,015 low). The valuation embeds extremely optimistic growth assumptions.

**Why the premium might be justified:**
- 45% market share in a regulatory-protected industry
- 3rd largest globally by units manufactured
- Structural tailwinds from STQC + PPO policies
- AI/edge surveillance is an early-stage opportunity
- Indian security market expected to grow at 15–17% for years

**Why the premium is risky:**
- TTM FCF is negative (-₹120 Cr)
- Operating cash flow conversion is poor (CFO/OP = 22%)
- Working capital heavy business model
- Supply chain vulnerabilities (semiconductors, forex, Dahua dependency)
- Revenue quality is partly price-inflation driven (25% ASP growth in guidance)

## 7. Verdict

**STANCE: Positive on business quality; cautious on valuation.**

CPPLUS is a high-quality business with:
- Dominant market share in a structurally growing industry
- Regulatory moat (STQC) creating barriers for foreign competition
- Strong management execution (beat FY26 guidance, upgraded FY27)
- Healthy balance sheet post-IPO (D/E 0.06, ROE 25%, ROCE 31%)
- Clear growth roadmap: backward integration, AI, capacity doubling

**However**, at 116x TTM earnings and 69–84x forward earnings, virtually all of this is priced in. The business model is working capital-intensive, operating cash flows are poor, and there is meaningful execution risk in scaling multiple initiatives simultaneously. The credit rating downgrade by CARE (2024→2026) and historical suspension by CRISIL (2015–2026) suggest governance and information-sharing concerns worth noting.

**For fresh entry**, valuations at 50–60x FY28 earnings would offer a more reasonable risk-reward. At current levels, the stock is priced for perfection.

## 8. Key Monitorables

1. **Quarterly Revenue Growth:** Sustainability of 40%+ growth; watch for deceleration
2. **Operating Cash Flow:** Must improve vs. FY26's ₹13 Cr — strong revenue growth with negative cash flow is a red flag
3. **Gross Margin Trajectory:** Q4 FY26 at 31.8% was exceptional — reversal would hurt sentiment
4. **Dahua Relationship:** Any change in exclusive distribution status
5. **Price Hike Absorption:** Can the market absorb sustained 25% ASP increases?
6. **Credit Rating:** Next CRISIL review; any development with CARE/Brickwork
7. **AIL Dixon Subsidiary:** Progress since fire loss and full acquisition
8. **NEXIVUE/Eyra Traction:** Success of multi-brand strategy in expanding TAM
