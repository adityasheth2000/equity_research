    *   **Short-Term (Fund Based):** N/A (Rs.0.00 Crs)

## Detailed Rationale
Brickwork Ratings (BWR) has downgraded the long-term ratings and reaffirmed the short-term ratings while simultaneously withdrawing the ratings for the Bank Loan Facilities of Aditya Infotech Ltd.
*   **Rating Downgrade/W Withdrawal:** This action accounts for the company's non-achievement of projected revenue in the past period. Furthermore, the withdrawal factors in the non-receipt of specific information, preventing the access of future expected performance data required to assign a rating.
*   **Withdrawal Mechanism:** The withdrawal of the outstanding bank loan ratings (Rs.362.00 Crs) was executed at the specific request of the company via a letter dated 31-May-2022, supported by a No Objection Certificate (NOC) from the lenders covering the outstanding exposure. This complies with BWR's internal policy for withdrawal.

---

## Key Rating Drivers — Strengths
*   **Strong Market Position:** The company's flagship brand, **CP Plus** (CCTV camera products), commands a significant market share in the electronic surveillance industry.
*   **Brand Contribution:** The CP Plus brand contributes approximately **60% - 70%** of the company's total sales volume.
*   **Specialized Expertise:** Aditya Infotech Ltd. possesses special expertise in technology-driven security and safety solutions, particularly Electronic Video Surveillance Products and Solutions.

## Key Rating Drivers — Weaknesses
*   **Revenue Non-Achievement:** The rating downgrade reflects the failure to achieve previously established revenue projections.
*   **Information Non-Cooperation:** The agency cited the non-receipt of key financial information and an inability to assess future expected performance as critical factors leading to the rating withdrawal.
*   **Rating Withdrawal:** The withdrawal of the Credit Rating itself indicates a lack of transparency or an adverse shift in business operations that prevents the current rating from being maintained.
