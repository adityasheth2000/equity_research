### 3. Rationale for Rating Action
*   **Action Context:** CARE has reaffirmed and withdrawn outstanding ratings ('CARE BBB+; Stable/ CARE A2') at the request of the company, supported by a 'No Objection Certificate' received from the banks.
*   **Supporting Factors:** The ratings reflect the company’s strong market presence under the brand name 'CP Plus' in surveillance equipment distribution. Key drivers include an extensive distribution network across India, long-standing partnerships with reputed suppliers, a diverse customer base, and experienced promoters.
*   **Growth Drivers:** Sustained operational growth driven by consistent demand for surveillance equipment.
*   **Constraints/Risks:** Ratings are constrained by a moderate financial risk profile, working capital intensive nature of operations, industry competition leading to modest operating margins, supplier concentration risk, and exposure to foreign exchange fluctuations.

### 4. Business Risk Profile Assessment
*   **Market Position:** Strong market position in India within the electronic security segment (video surveillance).
*   **Brand:** Majority of turnover contributed by indigenous brand 'CP Plus', a leading flagship security and surveillance brand.
*   **Distribution:** Deep penetration in India with more than 500 channel partners spread across 500+ towns and a network of local service and support at 52 locations.
*   **Product Concentration:** Product profile is concentrated towards cameras and Digital Video Recorders (DVRs).
    *   **FY24:** Contributed ~82% to revenue.
    *   **9MFY25:** Contributed ~67% to revenue.
*   **Supplier Concentration:** Long-standing relationship (14 years) with Dahua Technology (under brand 'Dahua') as the exclusive distributor in India. The company acts as the exclusive distributor for Dahua Technology, one of the leading global players in advanced video surveillance solutions.

### 5. Financial Risk Profile Assessment
*   **Profile Summary:** Moderate financial risk profile.
*   **Operational Nature:** Working capital intensive.
*   **Margins:** Modest operating margins due to industry competition.
*   **Exposures:**
    *   **Foreign Exchange:** Exposure to foreign exchange fluctuations.
    *   **Imports:** The company imports ~40%-50% of its products from Dahua.

### 6. Subsidiary / Joint Venture Details
*   **Acquisition/Consolidation:** The analytical approach accounts for the acquisition of **AIL Dixon Technologies Private Limited** on September 10, 2024.
*   **JV History:** This entity was erstwhile a 50:50 Joint Venture between AIL & Dixon Technologies Private Limited.
*   **JV Operations:** AIL Dixon Technologies Private Limited commenced manufacturing CCTVs and DVRs in Jan’18. AIL essentially purchases the products manufactured by the JV and sells them to customers.

### 7. Financial Metrics & Comparative Data
*   **Revenue Mix (Cameras & DVRs):**
    *   FY24: ~82% of Revenue.
    *   9MFY25: ~67% of Revenue.
*   **Import Dependency:** ~40%-50% of products imported from principal supplier (Dahua).
*   **Product Expansion:** Efforts made to add product portfolio (surveillance software, video door phone, time & attendance recording device, digital door lock), though camera and DVR remain key.

### 8. Specific Risks Flagged
*   Supplier concentration risk.
*   Foreign exchange fluctuations.
*   Industry competition leading to modest operating margins.
*   Working capital intensive nature of operations.

### Business Risk Profile Assessment
*   **Market Position:** Operates in a competitive electronic surveillance equipment industry in India. Top 3 brands (CP Plus, Dahua, Hikvision) command ~70-75% market share. AIL (via CP Plus brand) is a leader with a wide distribution network.
*   **Manufacturing Capacity:**
    *   Kadapa plant operated at full capacity, adding 6 million units.
    *   New plant under construction to produce 30 million housing/enclosure units.
    *   Acquired 50% balance stake in **AIL Dixon Technologies Private Limited** (completed Sep 10, 2024) to expand manufacturing capacities. Aims to establish CP Plus campus (2nd largest outside China, potentially largest in India).
*   **Management:** Led by Mr. Aditya Khemka (MD) with 20+ years of experience in Indian Security and Surveillance industry. Promoters have 26 years of industry experience.

### Financial Risk Profile Assessment
*   **Risk Rating:** Moderate financial risk profile.
*   **Leverage:** Overall gearing ratio elevated above 1x over the past 3 fiscals.
*   **Working Capital Dependence:** Business is highly working-capital-intensive. Distributor business allows 2.5–3 month credit period to customers while availing 120 days credit from Dahua. This necessitates high dependence on working capital borrowing.
*   **Interest Coverage:** Indicators stood slightly moderate with interest coverage falling from 7.34x (PY) to 6.05x due to higher interest expenses from elevated utilization levels.

### Specific Risks Flagged
*   **Foreign Exchange Risk:** Subsidiary imports 85% of requirement for CCTV cameras/materials from China, exposing it to indirect forex risk.
    *   *Mitigation:* Enters hedging contracts covering largely currency fluctuation risks; policy of considering extra buffer in 'product costing'.
    *   *Supply Chain Risk:* High dependence on Chinese supplies (though no major disruptions reported).
*   **Competitiveness:** Lack of differentiation in product offerings forces competitive pricing strategies.
*   **Credit Policy Risk:** High dependence on working capital borrowing due to credit period mismatches with customers/suppliers.

### Other Credit-Relevant Details
*   **Subsidiary/Joint Venture:** Acquisition of balance 50% in **AIL Dixon Technologies Private Limited** via share swap transaction (grants Dixon Technologies 6.65% stake in AIL).
*   **Debt Maturity/Repayment:** Scheduled term loan repayment of Rs. 16.34 crore is anticipated against expected cash accruals > Rs. 200 crore.
*   **Margin Improvement:** PBILDT margin improved by 406 bps in 9MFY26 due to change in product mix, higher localization, and backward integration/economies of scale.
