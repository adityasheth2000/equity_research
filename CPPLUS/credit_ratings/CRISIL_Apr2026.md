# Credit Rating Report: Aditya Infotech Limited

## Rating Action

*   **Rating Agency:** Crisil Ratings Limited
*   **Corporate Name:** Aditya Infotech Limited (AIL)
*   **Date of Report:** April 10, 2026
*   **Rating Assigned:** **Crisil A / Stable**
*   **Rating Status:** Assigned (Suspension Revoked)
*   **Instruments Rated:** Long Term Bank Debt
*   **Total Facilities Rated:** **Rs. 555 Crore**

> **Note:** None of the Directors on Crisil Ratings Limited’s Board are members of the rating committee and do not participate in the discussion or assignment of ratings.

---

## Detailed Rationale

Crisil Ratings has revoked the suspension of its rating on the long-term bank facilities of Aditya Infotech Limited (AIL) and assigned a 'Crisil A/Stable' rating. The rating was previously suspended on June 18, 2015, due to the company's failure to provide necessary information required to maintain a valid rating. AIL has now shared the requisite information, enabling the reassignment of the rating.

The rating reflects the group's strong brand supported by an established domestic presence, strong geographical diversification and distribution network, and a healthy financial risk profile. These strengths are partially offset by susceptibility to competition, volatility in raw material prices and foreign exchange (forex) rates, and high working capital requirements.

---

## Key Rating Drivers — Strengths

*   **Established Brand and Domestic Presence:** AIL holds a strong market position in the electronics security and surveillance industry (CCTV cameras, recorders, etc.) under the "CP Plus" brand. It also earns revenue from service business and acts as a distributor for the Dahua Technologies group. Promoters possess 30+ years of experience with strong supplier/customer relationships, allowing the company to command premiums in the branded communication equipment industry. New brands (EYRA and NEXIVUE) are targeting the mass market.
    *   *Supporting Data:* Revenue of **Rs. 2,798 crore** achieved in the first nine months of FY2026 (vs. Rs. 2,134 crore a year ago).
*   **Strong Geographical Diversification and Distribution Network:** The group maintains a strong marketing network across India with over 800 distributors in more than 500 cities (Tier I, II, and III) and 10 warehouses located strategically across North, West, East, and South India.
    *   *Supporting Data:* Expansion into video door phones, time & attendance recorders, and digital door locks alongside cameras/recorders.
*   **Strong Financial Risk Profile:** Supported by steady accretion to reserves and fund raising through IPO.
    *   *Supporting Data:* Adjusted networth of **Rs. 1,150 crore** as on March 31, 2026 (vs. Rs. 458 crore on March 31, 2025).
    *   *Supporting Data:* Estimated gearing (0.2–0.3x) and TOLANW (1.4–1.5x) ratios as on March 31, 2026.
    *   *Supporting Data:* Healthy debt protection metrics with estimated Interest Coverage Ratio (**10+ times**) and Net cash accrual to adjusted debt ratio (**1.0–1.1x**) in FY2026.

---

## Key Rating Drivers — Weaknesses

*   **Exposure to Competition and Raw Material Price Fluctuation:** The electronics security industry is competitive and fragmented, leading to high technology obsolescence risk. Raw materials (chip sets, PCBs, copper, aluminum) are sourced from volatile domestic and foreign suppliers.
*   **Vulnerability to Forex Rate Fluctuations:** A majority of raw materials are procured from international markets (China and Taiwan). Sharp forex fluctuations affect procurement costs.
    *   *Mitigation:* The company enters hedging contracts and increases product costs to minimize this risk.
*   **Working Capital-Intensive Operations:** The business model involves large receivables and inventory, stretching the working capital cycle.
    *   *Supporting Data:* Gross Current Assets (GCA) were between **190–246 days** over the three fiscals through 2025.
    *   *Supporting Data:* Receivables and inventory days as on Sep 30, 2025, were 115 and 116 days, respectively.
    *   *Supporting Data:* Credit period provided to customers is around **90–100 days**.

---

## Liquidity

*   **Liquidity Assessment:** Liquidity is assessed as strong. Net cash accruals are expected to sufficiently cover yearly debt obligations.
*   **Cash Accruals:** Expected net cash accruals of **Rs. 275–400 crore per annum**.
*   **Bank Limit Utilisation:**
    *   Bank lines and internal cash accruals are adequate to meet debt obligations and working capital requirements over the medium term.
    *   Current ratio estimated at a comfortable **>1.5 times** as on March 31, 2026.
    *   Cash and bank balance of around **Rs. 25 crore** as on September 30, 2025.

---

## Outlook & Rating Sensitivity

*   **Outlook:** **Stable**
    *   Crisil Ratings believes the group will continue to benefit from its established brand presence and distribution network.

*   **Upward Rating Factors:**
    *   Improvement in scale of operations and sustenance of operating margins leading to higher cash accruals.
    *   Sustenance of healthy financial risk profile and improvement in the working capital cycle (GCAs at less than 180 days and reduction in receivables overdue by more than six months).

*   **Downward Rating Factors:**
    *   Decline in net cash accruals due to fall in revenues or operating margins.
    *   Further stretch in the working capital cycle or large, debt-funded capex weakening the liquidity and financial risk profile.
    *   TOLANW ratio increasing above **2 times**.
