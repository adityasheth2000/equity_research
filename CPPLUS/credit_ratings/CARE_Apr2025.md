### Outlook
**Stable**
CARE Ratings believes that the entity shall be able to sustain its growth in scale of operations over the medium to long-term considering the increasing demand of surveillance equipment.

### Key Financial Metrics & Ratios
*   **Losses reported (Subsidiary):** Rs. 177 crore (due to fire in government bonded warehouse, Jan 2024).
*   **Gearing (Capital Structure):** 0.75x.
*   **Profitability Margins (Target/Sensitivity):**
    *   Positive factor requires maintenance of profitability margins over **8%**.
    *   Negative trigger is PBILD T margin below **7%** on a sustained basis.
*   **Operating Cycle Days:** Sensitivity trigger is stretch beyond **120 days**.

### Rationale for Rating Action (Downgrade)
*   **Primary Driver:** Anticipated moderation in financial risk profile for FY25 (April 01, 2024 to March 31, 2025).
*   **Subsidiary Impacts:** Losses reported by subsidiary AIL Dixon Technologies Private Limited (ADTPL) during 9MFY25 (April 1, 2024, to December 31, 2024). The losses (Rs. 177 crore) were due to a fire at a government bonded warehouse in January 2024. These losses were underinsured and not fully recovered from insurance, leading to a weakening of the liquidity position.
*   **Operational Constraints:** The ratings remain constrained by:
    *   Working capital-intensive nature of operations.
    *   Competition in the industry leading to modest operating margins.
    *   Supplier concentration risk.
    *   Foreign exchange fluctuation risk.

### Strengths (Supporting the Rating)
*   **Brand Equity:** Established brand name ‘CP Plus’ in the surveillance equipment distribution business.
*   **Distribution:** Country-wide distribution network across India.
*   **Supplier/Customer Relationships:** Long-standing relationship with reputed suppliers and wide customer base.
*   **Promoters:** Experienced promoters.
*   **Market Demand:** Sustained growth in the company’s scale of operations driven by continuous uptick in demand of surveillance equipment.

### Business Risk Profile Assessment
*   **Market Position:** Strong market position in India within the electronic security segment (video surveillance).
*   **Revenue Contribution:** Majority of turnover contributed by its indigenous brand, ‘CP Plus’ (leading flagship security and surveillance brand).
*   **Diversification:** Geographical and product diversification are cited as positive factors.

### Financial Risk Profile Assessment
*   **Liquidity:** Weakening of liquidity position due to uninsured subsidiary losses. However, efficient management of working capital is cited as an improvement factor.
*   **Leverage:** Overall gearing of 0.75x.
*   **Working Capital:** Working capital-intensive operations pose a risk, though management is efficient.

### Specific Risks Flagged
1.  **Supplier Concentration/Relationship:** Any change in its relationship with supplier (**Dahua**) posing risk for AIL to maintain its market share in India.
2.  **Operational Performance:** Stretch in operating cycle days beyond 120 days leading to further pressure on liquidity.
3.  **Profitability:** Any decline in scale of operations with PBILD T margin below 7%.
4.  **Regulatory/External:** Fire at a government bonded warehouse (past event driving the loss).
