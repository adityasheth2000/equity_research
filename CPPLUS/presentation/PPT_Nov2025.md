**Management Commentary: Q2 and H1 FY26 Financial Results**

**Mr. Aditya Khemka, Managing Director, highlighted the following performance metrics:**

| Metric | Q2 FY26 (Current) | H1 FY26 (Current) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Revenue** | INR 919.6 crore (Up 37.5% YoY) | INR 1,659.7 crores (Up 27.2% YoY) |
| **EBITDA** | INR 111.1 crore (Up 157.6% YoY) | INR 176.0 crores (Up 102.0% YoY) |
| **EBITDA Margins** | 12.0% (560 bps accretion YoY) | 10.6% (390 bps expansion YoY) |
| **Adjusted PAT** | INR 70.0 crores (Up 239.1% YoY) | INR 102.8 crores (Up 138.4% YoY) |

**Key Drivers of Performance:**
*   **Q2 Drivers:** Robust demand for CP PLUS-branded IP cameras and extensive STQC-certified portfolio. Margin improvement driven by favorable product mix, higher localization, and operating leverage.
*   **H1 Performance:** Strong growth across revenue and profitability lines.

**Balance Sheet & Capital Allocation:**
*   **Debt Reduction:** Strategic use of IPO proceeds for debt repayment reduced the debt position from ~INR 466 crores (June 2025) to ~INR 68 crores (Sept 2025).
*   **Impact:** Substantial interest cost savings, improved overall profitability, and financial flexibility for future growth initiatives.

**Market Position and Product Mix:**
*   **Market Leadership:** Continues to strengthen leadership in the Indian video surveillance market.
*   **Brand Contribution:** CP PLUS brand contributes **86%** of revenue.
*   **Product Mix:** IP products form nearly **70%** of the portfolio.

**Strategic Initiatives & Expansion:**
*   **Capacity Expansion:** Targeting capacity expansion to **2 million units per month**.
*   **New Brands:** Launch of new brands **EYRA** and **NEXIVIEW**.
*   **Strategic Partnership:** Partnership with **L&T Semiconductor Technologies** for Vision AI SoC-powered cameras.

**FY26 Guidance:**
Management remains confident of achieving:
*   **Revenue Growth:** 25-30%
*   **EBITDA Margins:** 10-11%
