**Topic: Global Semiconductor & Memory Supply Disruption**

**Supply Chain & Strategic Mitigation**
*   **Market Constraint:** All major memory vendors have stopped DDR3 production, causing global supply chain shortages.
*   **Product Strategy:** Adoption of a **Multi SoC (System on Chip) Product Strategy**.
*   **Vendor Alignment:** Multi-Supply Chain Procurement Strategy aligned with **ESMT, Macronix & Winbond**.
*   **Planning:** Implementation of Forward Procurement & Long-Term Planning to mitigate disruption.

**Pricing Outlook & Guidance**
*   **Price Trend:** Management states price escalation is inevitable.
*   **Forecast Duration:** Price rises are projected to keep going up until **2027**.
*   **Pass-Through Strategy:** The company plans to pass these cost increases on to customers "in Gradual Manner" (noted as "AIL Passing it").
