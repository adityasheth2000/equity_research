# Management Commentary
**Speaker:** Mr. Aditya Khemka, Managing Director

### Financial Performance (Q1 FY26)
*   **Revenue:** Rose by **16.4% YoY** to **INR 740.0 crores**.
    *   *Note:* Growth observed despite limited availability of STQC-certified IP cameras (available from end of June 2025).
*   **EBITDA:** Increased by **47.5% YoY** to **INR 64.9 crores**.
*   **EBITDA Margins:** Expanded by **180 bps YoY** to **8.7%**, driven mainly by margin expansion in the CP Plus product line.
*   **Profit After Tax (PAT):** Grew by **46.1% YoY** to **INR 32.9 crores**.

### Strategic Initiatives & Operations
*   **Market Positioning:** CP Plus holds the largest portfolio of STQC and BIS-certified products in the market. As non-STQC stocks exhaust in the channel, a demand-supply gap is emerging, positioning the company to gain market share across Government, Enterprise, SMB, and Home segments.
*   **R&D & Localization:**
    *   Opened a new R&D center in Ahmedabad; a center in Taiwan is under process.
    *   Progressing on complete backward integration of components (housings, cables, connectors, power electronics, and CCTV lenses) to enhance supply chain resilience and cost competitiveness.
*   **AIL Dixon Integration:** Integration is progressing well, delivering synergies in manufacturing, supply chain, and localization of Bill of Materials (BOM).
*   **Balance Sheet Management:** **INR 375 crores** in debt repayment from IPO proceeds has strengthened the balance sheet. Benefits of lower finance costs are expected to be visible from Q2 FY26 onwards.
*   **Leadership:** Strengthened with key senior appointments during the quarter.
*   **Branding:** Launched OOH (Out-of-Home) campaigns across major airports and buses emphasizing the STQC certified product range and "CP PLUS Trusted Core (CTC)" technology.

### Guidance & Outlook (FY26)
The company is confident of outpacing industry growth in FY26 based on sector tailwinds, engagement in large government/enterprise projects, and improved funnel visibility.
*   **Revenue Growth:** Expected to be in the range of **25–30%**.
*   **EBITDA Margins:** Expected to be in the range of **10–11%**.

## Page 27

**CP PLUS Trusted Core: The Heart of Reliable Surveillance**

The company is launching "ctc TECHNOLOGY" (CP PLUS Trusted Core), a proprietary technology stack designed to enhance product security and reliability. This represents a strategic initiative to differentiate its surveillance hardware through robust security features.

**Key Technical Features & Value Proposition:**
*   **Secure Boot:** Ensures that only authorized firmware runs during device startup, preventing unauthorized access at the system level.
*   **Secure Transmission:** Data sent online is protected with strong security protocols.
*   **Strong Cryptography Algorithms:** Implements advanced measures to defend against side-channel attacks.
*   **Tamper Proof:** Utilization of specialized hardware to prevent physical tampering with the device.
*   **Data Security:** Protection of sensitive information through strong encryption.

**Strategic Implication:**
These features position the company's hardware as "More Trusted • More Secure • More Reliable," which is a critical differentiator in the security surveillance market, potentially appealing to government and enterprise clients requiring high-grade data integrity and physical security.
