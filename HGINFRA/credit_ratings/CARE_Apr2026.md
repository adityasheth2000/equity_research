*   **Outlook:** Stable
*   **Previous Facility Amount:** ₹200.00 Cr (Reduced to ₹140.00 Cr).

#### 2. Key Financial Metrics
*   **Total Operating Income (TOI) FY25:** ₹6,052 crore (18% growth).
*   **TOI 9MFY26:** ₹4,312 crore.
*   **PBILDT Margin (FY25):** 15.81%.
*   **Expected PBILDT Margin (Medium Term):** ~14%.
*   **Total Debt / PBILDT (FY25):** 1.55x.
*   **TOL/TNW (Debt Sensitivity):** Targeted below 0.5x on a sustained basis (Positive Factor).

#### 3. Rationale for Rating Action
*   **Strengths:** Established track record (>2 decades), experienced promoters, strong execution capabilities across geographies.
*   **Order Book:** Segmentally diversified outstanding order book as of December 31, 2025, providing adequate revenue visibility.
*   **Monetization:** Share Purchase Agreement (SPA) signed with Neo Infra Income Opportunities Fund for monetization of five hybrid annuallity model (HAM) projects (Aggregate consideration: ₹1,384 crore).
    *   *Proceeds Utilization:* ~₹235 Cr received till March 2026; balance ~₹1,149 Cr expected in FY27. These funds cover equity commitments for ongoing HAM projects.
*   **Profitability:** Healthy scale of operations and ability to sustain strong operating profitability.
*   **Diversification:** Order book supported by execution of road projects and diversification into railways, solar, and Battery Energy Storage System (BESS) segments.

#### 4. Business Risk Profile
*   **Working Capital Intensity:** Nature of operations is working-capital-intensive due to Public Private Partnership (PPP) projects.
    *   *Trend:* Working capital intensity increased in FY25 and 9MFY26 due to higher receivables and unbilled revenue (largely from HAM projects) and delays in debt disbursement in solar projects.
    *   *Outlook:* Expected to rationalize due to closure of solar projects and planned debt disbursement in ongoing HAM projects.
*   **New Segment Risks:** Ability to deliver projects in solar and BESS segments within stipulated timelines remains to be established.
    *   *Specific Risks:* Land acquisition, right-of-way (RoW), battery sourcing, foreign exchange volatility, technology obsolescence.
*   **Competition:** Heightened competition in the construction sector.

#### 5. Financial Risk Profile
*   **Leverage:** Indicators remain comfortable (Total debt/PBILDT of 1.55x in FY25) but expected to remain marginally higher in the near term.
*   **Margins:** Expected to moderate to ~14% in the medium term.
*   **Liquidity/Cash Flow:** Cash inflow from HAM project monetization is expected to sufficiently take care of equity commitments.

#### 6. Specific Risks Flagged by Agency
*   **Legal/Regulatory:** Investigation initiated by the Central Bureau of Investigation (CBI) and Anti-Corruption Bureau (ACB), Patna, in January 2026.
    *   *Context:* Involves certain railway project-related matters; search operations conducted at select company locations.
    *   *Management Stance:* Fully cooperating; operations and banking arrangements continue without disruption; no quantified financial or tax liability crystallized as on date.
*   **Execution:** Execution challenges in HAM projects and delays in debt disbursement for solar projects.

#### 7. Rating Sensitivities
*   **Positive Factors:**
    *   Significant growth in scale of operations while diversifying business segments.
    *   Total outside liabilities to tangible net worth (TOL/TNW) remaining below 0.5x on a sustained basis.
*   **Negative Factors (Implied/General Monitorable):**
    *   Rationalization of working capital intensity (key rating monitorable).
    *   Potential impact of CBI/ACB investigation on operational/financial profile.

#### 8. Debt Maturity / Commitments
*   **Upcoming Inflows:** ₹1,149 Cr expected from HAM project monetization in FY27.
*   **Equity Commitments:** Ongoing commitments for HAM projects to be covered by the above inflows.
*   **Debt Disbursement:** Planned disbursement for ongoing HAM projects expected to help rationalize working capital.

#### 9. Comparative Metrics (vs Previous Cycle)
*   **Facility Size:** Reduced from ₹200.00 Cr to ₹140.00 Cr.
*   **Growth:** TOI grew by 18% in FY25; 9MFY26 shows moderate year-on-year growth despite appointed date delays.

#### 10. Corporate Structure / Subsidiaries
*   **Parent:** H.G. Infra Engineering Limited (HGIEL).
*   **JV/Strategic Partnership:** Monetization partnership with Neo Infra Income Opportunities Fund regarding five HAM projects.
