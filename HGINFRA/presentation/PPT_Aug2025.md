**Company:** H.G Infra Engineering Ltd
**Reporting Period:** Q1 FY25 vs. Q1 FY26

**Financial Performance Overview**
The company reported revenue growth of 13.5% year-over-year, though profitability margins contracted across the board, leading to a decline in absolute PBT and PAT.

| Metric | Q1 FY25 (Previous Year) | Q1 FY26 (Current Year) | YoY Change / Trend |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Revenue from Operations (Rs. Mn)** | 15,059 | 17,092 | +13.5% |
| **EBITDA (Rs. Mn)** | 2,433 | 2,357 | -3.1% decline |
| **EBITDA Margin (%)** | 16.16% | 13.79% | -237 bps compression |
| **Profit Before Tax (Rs. Mn)** | 1,897 | 1,674 | -11.7% decline |
| **PBT Margin (%)** | 12.59% | 9.79% | -280 bps compression |
| **Profit After Tax (Rs. Mn)** | 1,395 | 1,255 | -10.1% decline |
| **PAT Margin (%)** | 9.27% | 7.34% | -193 bps compression |

**Key Financial Takeaways:**
*   **Top-line Growth:** Revenue grew to Rs. 17,092 Mn from Rs. 15,059 Mn.
*   **Margin Compression:** Despite higher revenue, EBITDA margins fell from 16.16% to 13.79%. Consequently, PBT and PAT margins also saw significant contraction, driving a double-digit decline in absolute profit numbers despite sales growth.
