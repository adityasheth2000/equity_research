**Key Definitions & Methodology Notes:**
*   **Debtor Days:** Average Trade Receivables (excl. retention money) / Revenue From Operations
*   **Inventory Days:** Average Inventory / Cost of Materials
*   **Creditors Days:** Average Trade Payables / (Cost of Materials + Contract & Site Expenses)
*   **Net Working Capital Days:** Debtor days + Inventory days – Creditor days
*   **Debt to Equity:** Total debt / Shareholders Equity
