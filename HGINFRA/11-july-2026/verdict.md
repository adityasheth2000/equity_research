# H.G. Infra Engineering Ltd — Verdict
**Date:** 11 July 2026 | **CMP:** Rs 564 | **Mcap:** Rs 3,673 Cr | **P/E:** 12.4x

---

## 1. Company Overview & Industry Positioning

H.G. Infra Engineering Ltd (HGIEL) is a mid-cap EPC/infrastructure player with 23+ years of execution history, operating PAN-India across 13+ states with a 2,880+ unit equipment fleet and 4,200+ employees. The company has diversified from its traditional highways core into five segments: Roads & Highways, Railways & Metro, Solar, Battery Energy Storage Systems (BESS), and Transmission & Distribution.

HGIEL occupies a niche as a high-return EPC player with a demonstrated track record of industry-leading EBITDA margins (15-16% standalone historically) and ROE (10Y avg: 21%). Among the 120-company construction peer set on screener.in, the stock trades at P/E of 12.4x vs median 17.9x — a valuation discount driven by recent earnings compression.

## 5. Credit Rating Trajectory & Risks

### Rating Summary

| Rating Agency | Date | Rating | Outlook | Key Highlights |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| ICRA | May 2025 | [ICRA]AA- | Positive | Long-term bank facilities reaffirmed |
| CARE | Jan 2026 | CARE AA | Stable (Credit Update) | CBI investigation flagged |
| ICRA | Feb 2026 | [ICRA]AA- | Positive | Reaffirmed with enhanced exposure |
| CARE | Apr 2026 | CARE AA | Stable | Reaffirmed; facility reduced from Rs 200 Cr to Rs 140 Cr |
| ICRA | Apr 2026 | [ICRA]AA- | Positive | Reaffirmed; non-fund based A1+ |

### Key Rating Concerns

1. **CBI/ACB Investigation (Jan 2026):** Search operations at 3 company locations; CMD Harendra Singh named in FIR. Interim protection granted by court. All operations and banking continue uninterrupted. This is a significant governance overhang.
2. **Rising Leverage:** Overall gearing rose from 0.32x (FY24) to 0.58x (9M FY26) per CARE. Interest coverage fell from 10.15x to 4.88x. Total consolidated debt at Rs 4,934 Cr (Mar 2026) vs Rs 4,092 Cr (Mar 2025).
3. **Working Capital Intensity:** Gross current asset days rose from 171 (FY24) to 218 (FY25). Driven by higher receivables/unbilled revenue from HAM projects and delays in solar project debt disbursement. CARE cites the discontinuation of monthly payments under Atma Nirbhar Bharat scheme as an aggravating factor.
4. **Execution Risk in New Segments:** BESS (battery sourcing, technology, forex risk), solar, and transmission are unproven for HGIEL. Management quality in executing these new verticals is a key monitorable.
5. **Margin Pressure:** CARE expects PBILDT margins to moderate to ~14% in the medium term (from 15.8% in FY25). Input cost volatility (bitumen prices linked to West Asia geopolitics) flagged as a risk.
6. **Rating Triggers (Downside):** Debt/PBILDT exceeding 1.5-1.6x or TOL/TNW exceeding 1.0x on a sustained basis; aggressive addition of new HAM/renewable energy projects.

### Positive Rating Factors
- AA-/AA ratings from both ICRA and CARE with Stable/Positive outlook
- Monetization proceeds (~Rs 1,149 Cr) expected to improve leverage
- Order book of 3.0x revenue provides strong visibility
- Demonstrated asset recycling track record (4 HAM projects sold earlier to Highways Infrastructure Trust)

## 7. Key Monitorables

1. **CBI/ACB Investigation Resolution:** The single biggest overhang. Any adverse development (chargesheet, contract debarment) would be catastrophic for the stock. Positive closure would remove a significant discount.
2. **HAM Monetization Close:** Rs 1,149 Cr expected in FY27 from Neo Infra deal. Successful closure is critical for debt reduction and equity release.
3. **Execution Ramp-Up:** Rs 55,914 Mn in new orders post-Mar 2026 need to translate to revenue. Execution cadence on Welspun Pune-Shirur (Rs 3,931 Cr single project) will be closely watched.
4. **BESS/Solar Execution:** All 3 BESS projects (735 MW) and solar projects are at 0-8% completion. Timely completion within cost estimates will determine whether the diversification thesis holds.
5. **Working Capital & Debt:** Normalization of working capital (receivables, unbilled revenue) post-HAM monetization. Consolidated debt trajectory — particularly whether TOL/TNW holds below 1.0x.
6. **Margin Trajectory:** Q4 FY26 standalone EBITDA margin at 9.4% is the lowest in recent history. Whether this is a trough or a new normal needs monitoring.
7. **Order Inflow Run-Rate:** FY26 saw order book shrink from Rs 1.53 Lakh Cr (Mar 2025) to Rs 1.01 Lakh Cr (Mar 2026, before post-FY26 additions). Sustained order inflow is essential to maintain growth.
8. **Promoter Holding:** Promoters hold 71.8% (down from 74.5% in Sep 2024). FII holding declined from 2.9% to 1.4%. DII holding declined from 12.0% to 10.7%. Public shareholders increased from 13.3% to 16.2%. Institutional exodus is a red flag.

## 8. Summary

H.G. Infra is a high-quality EPC operator with a 20%+ ROE track record, now trading at distressed valuations (P/E 12.4x, -48% 1Y price decline) due to a confluence of earnings compression, governance overhang (CBI investigation), and working capital stress. The order book at Rs 1.57 Lakh Cr (including post-FY26 wins) is large relative to the Rs 5,235 Cr revenue base and diversified across roads, railways, and energy.

The bull case rests on: (a) CBI resolution, (b) HAM monetization unlocking cash flows and reducing leverage, (c) margin recovery as legacy old-order book burns off and new orders at better pricing flow through, and (d) BESS/transmission diversification providing a new growth vector.

The bear case rests on: (a) prolonged or adverse CBI outcome, (b) persistent working capital strain keeping debt elevated, (c) margin pressure from input cost inflation and competition, and (d) execution risk in new segments.

**Investment stance:** The risk-reward is asymmetric — downside from current levels is partially protected by the order book and asset base (BV Rs 501/share, CMP Rs 564 = 1.13x P/B), while resolution of the CBI overhang and successful monetization could trigger significant re-rating. However, the stock remains a high-risk proposition until the CBI matter is resolved and working capital trends show meaningful improvement. Suitable only for investors with high risk tolerance and a 2-3 year horizon.
