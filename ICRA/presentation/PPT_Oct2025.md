**Ratings & ancillary services: Business environment and outlook**

**Environment**
*   **Macro Outlook:** Remains uncertain due to geopolitical tensions and steep U.S. tariffs on Indian exports. However, favorable agri trends have kept inflation subdued, prompting the RBI to cut rates by 100 bps to support growth.

**Bond Market Performance (H1 FY2026)**
*   **Growth:** Bond issuances grew by 30.7% YoY (vs. 4.6% in PY).
*   **Drivers:** Issuances surged to an all-time high in Q1 FY2026 supported by rate cuts. Yields rose in Q2 FY2026 as the RBI adopted a neutral stance, leading to a dip of 10.9% in bond issuances.
*   **Commercial Paper (CP):** Outstanding stayed at an elevated level for the last four quarters, despite a sequential dip due to a steep yield curve with favorable short-term interest rates and working capital demand from securities firms.

**Bank Credit Outstanding (H1 FY2026)**
*   **Growth:** Grew by 10.4% YoY as of September (vs. 12.9% in PY).
*   **Trends:** Bank credit slowed considerably in Q1 FY2026 as large borrowers preferred cheaper bonds. Lending picked up in Q2 FY2026 as bond yields rose.
*   **NBFCs:** Credit to NBFCs had an overhang of delinquency concerns in certain asset segments.

**Securitisation Market (H1 FY2026)**
*   **Market Volumes:** Estimated to have grown by ~9% in H1 FY2026 YoY (vs. 39% in PY).
*   **Asset Classes:** The dominant asset class continues to be vehicle loans (similar to FY2025), followed by home loan & loan against property. Share of PTC remained higher than DA.
*   **Pool Sales:** Subdued as banks were less concerned about the credit-to-deposits ratio.

**Outlook**
*   **Domestic Consumption:** Remains strong, supported by tax cuts, rate reductions, and favorable monsoons. Tariff uncertainty may weigh on exports and delay private capex until an India-US trade deal is finalised.
*   **Bonds:** Issuances dependent on interest rate movements; quicker transmission would benefit bonds. Softening global interest rates would benefit External Commercial Borrowing but impact domestic credit growth. CPs likely to remain at similar levels and benefit in case of further rate cuts.
*   **Bank Credit:** Dependence on ability to reprice deposits (likely in Q3 FY2026). Any further interest rate cut by RBI would slow down bank credit as Bonds would get preferred for their quicker transmission. MSME segment dependence on bank loans expected to continue supporting this segment.
*   **Securitisation:** Volumes dependent on pickup in NBFC AUMs benefiting from upbeat domestic consumption outlook. FY2026 volumes estimated in range of Rs ~2.5 trillion.

**Environment (Market Conditions & Drivers)**
*   **Tariff & Geopolitics:** Tariff uncertainty and geo-political headwinds have led to a continued cautious trend in spending.

**Topic: Domestic drivers to support growth in FY2026, even as external outlook remains uncertain**

**Rural Demand & Agriculture:**
*   **Outlook:** Upbeat in the near term.
*   **Drivers:** Healthy trends in kharif sowing, reasonable increase in Minimum Support Prices (MSPs) for the upcoming rabi season, and GST rate rationalisation.
*   **Risk:** Episodes of excess rainfall in September-October 2025 have raised concerns regarding yields of standing crops.

**Urban Consumption:**
*   **Growth Drivers:** Combination of GST rate rejig, sizeable income-tax relief, 100 bps rate cuts (leading to lower borrowing costs), and moderation in food inflation.
*   **Risk Factors:** Current steep US tariffs and penalties could lead to job losses in labour-intensive sectors, weighing on urban consumption.

**Government & Private Capex:**
*   **Govt Capex:** Budgeted to rise by **6.6%** in FY2026.
    *   **Trend:** Frontloaded in the first 5 months (5M) of FY2026; implicitly pegged to moderate on a Year-on-Year (YoY) basis in the remainder of the fiscal year.
    *   **Impact:** May weigh upon growth in H2 FY2026.
*   **Private Capex:** Ongoing tariff-related uncertainty is likely to delay private capex plans.

**Exports:**
*   **Merchandise Exports:** Negative implications due to the imposition of steep US tariffs.
    *   **Mitigation:** Materialisation of a bilateral trade deal may contain the extent of the downside in Q4 FY2026.
*   **Services Exports:** Growth in some segments likely to remain healthy.
*   **IT Services Risk:** Potential taxes on outsourcing are a key monitorable factor for the IT service export segment.
