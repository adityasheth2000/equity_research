**ICRA’s Business Segments**

This slide outlines the corporate structure and service offerings of ICRA Limited, segmented into two primary business verticals.

**1. Ratings & Ancillary Services**
*   **Entities:** Includes ICRA ESG Ratings, ICRA (An Affiliate of Moody's), and ICRA Nepal.
*   **Core Offerings:**
    *   **Rating, Research and Other Services:** Covers ratings for Corporate Debt, Financial Sector, Structured Finance, Infrastructure, InvITs, Mutual Fund Scheme Ratings, Public Finance Ratings, and Expected Loss Ratings.
    *   **Research:** Industry Research, Economy Research, and Credit Perspectives.
    *   **ESG Ratings/scores:** Includes ESG Impact ratings/scores, ESG Transition rating/scores, ESG Combined Ratings/scores, Core ESG Impact/Transition/Combined ratings/scores.

**2. Research & Analytics (R&A)**
*   **Entities:** Includes Fintellix, ICRA Analytics, and D2K Technologies.
*   **Sub-segments:**
    *   **KnowTech (Tech-enabled Data & Knowledge Services):** Focuses on offshore tech-enabled data management, analytical services, and digital transformation assistance across industries. Specific capabilities include data operations, financial intelligence, ESG analytical services, and digital transformation.
    *   **BankTech (Risk, Regulatory & Supervisory Technology):** Provides advanced tools and consulting for credit, market, and operational risk. Key products include regulatory reporting, supervisory solutions, EWS (Early Warning Systems), IRS, ECL (Expected Credit Loss), engineering services, and model validation.
    *   **CapTech (Market Data, Valuation & Investment Intelligence):** Offers fixed-income data and valuations, mutual fund research, and portfolio tracking. Products include rating tracker, performance data & research of live MF schemes, Fixed Income indices, bond valuation, and customized research.

**Topic: West Asia conflict poses downside risks to India’s growth outlook (Source: ICRA Research)**

*   **Rural Demand & Agriculture Outlook:**
    *   Rural demand is expected to become cautious due to constrained remittances from migrants in sectors affected by the West Asia crisis.
    *   Below-normal rainfall forecasts are anticipated to hurt rural sentiments and weaken the overall demand outlook.
    *   The Southwest monsoon rainfall distribution and magnitude remain key determinants for agricultural outcomes over the coming quarters.
    *   Key near-term monitorables include El Nino conditions during the monsoon season and negative implications of the West Asia conflict on fertilizer prices and availability.

*   **Government Capex & Investment:**
    *   The Government of India's (GoI) capital expenditure (capex) is budgeted to rise by 11.5% in FY2027, which supports investment activities.
    *   However, expectations of fiscal slippage due to the West Asia crisis could constrain discretionary spending.
    *   Ongoing conflict and tariff-related uncertainties may weigh on private capex plans, particularly for export-oriented segments.

*   **Urban Demand & Inflation Risks:**
    *   GST rate rationalization and interest rate cuts are viewed as positive factors for urban demand.
    *   Risks to urban household incomes and demand include a potential uptick in inflation caused by poor monsoons, fallout from the West Asia crisis, and a dull outlook for export-oriented sectors.

*   **Exports & Global Trade:**
    *   Geopolitical tensions in West Asia are expected to adversely impact shipping lines and demand in the region, weighing on India’s merchandise exports in FY2027.
    *   The outlook for services exports remains cautious due to geopolitical uncertainty affecting global growth.

## Page 15

**Ratings & ancillary services: Business environment and outlook**

**Macroeconomic Environment (FY2026)**
*   **Growth Drivers:** Economic momentum moderated; rate cut cycle commenced in February 2025; GST rate cuts boosted activities.
*   **Credit Growth:** Boosted by the above factors despite high export tariffs and geopolitical conflicts.
*   **Market Shifts:** Bond yield volatility drove opportunistic shifts from bonds to bank credit.

**Bond Market Data & Outlook**
*   **FY2026 Performance:** Issuance grew 8.8% YoY (compared to 11.1% in PY).
    *   Q1FY2026 saw highest-ever quarterly issuance, though it moderated later due to rising/volatile yields.
*   **Commercial Paper (CP):** Outstanding grew only 4% YoY in FY2026 (tight liquidity). Competition for growth increased from Certificates of Deposit (CD).
*   **Outlook:** Volatile bond issuances expected due to macro uncertainty. Elevated yields may benefit bank credit. High currency hedging premiums may limit foreign currency borrowings. Short-term CP rates remain elevated with limited structural drivers.

**Bank Credit Data & Outlook**
*   **FY2026 Performance:** Bank credit grew 15.9% YoY (compared to 10.9% in PY).
    *   Supported by corporate segment demand pick-up; retail and NBFC lending remained strong.
*   **Margins:** Profitability margins under pressure due to high deposit rates amidst elevated Credit-to-Deposit ratios.
*   **Outlook:** Improved competitive position vs. bonds and expectations of no further rate cuts should boost lender appetite. Relaxation in Liquidity Coverage Ratio (LCR) norms will provide resources. Risks include elevated input costs and supply chain disruptions. Government credit guarantee schemes may support growth.

**Securitisation Data & Outlook**
*   **FY2026 Performance:** Market volumes estimated to have grown ~5% YoY.
    *   **NBFCs/HFCs:** Marked by 25% growth in volumes.
    *   **Banks:** Significantly reduced sell-down volumes (partly offset by large ticket isolated transactions).
    *   **Asset Classes:** Vehicle loans dominant; MBS share declined; MFIs regained traction.
*   **Outlook (FY2027):** Securitisation expected to be ₹2.6 – 2.7 lakh crore, driven largely by NBFCs and HFCs projecting high-teens growth. Priority sector lending obligations expected to provide structural support.

**Page Title:** Research & Analytics: Business environment and outlook

**Topic:** Strategic positioning, regulatory drivers, and growth outlook for the Research & Analytics segment.

**Segment Analysis (KnowTech):**
*   **Environment:** Enterprises are piloting Gen-AI-led automation, resulting in more measured discretionary spend and longer outsourcing decision cycles. Automation is moderating volumes in knowledge process and software development projects, shifting focus toward higher-value, transformation-led engagements.
*   **Outlook Strategy:** Repositioning offerings with AI-powered capabilities; diversifying beyond the captive base to address evolving demand. Deepening partnerships on business transformation, legacy system migration, and embedding new technologies.

**Segment Analysis (BankTech):**
*   **Environment:** Regulatory complexity is expanding (model governance, AI governance, data quality), sustaining compliance-linked demand for risk and reporting solutions. RBI’s ECL transition effective 1st April 2027 is driving investment in ECL solutions, data infrastructure, and model validation.
*   **Outlook Strategy:** Scaling demand cycle around ECL and related solutions (EWS, asset classification, internal rating, regulatory reporting). Pursuing deeper integration across the combined entity and further international revenue expansion. Leveraging cross-sell and upsell opportunities across credit lifecycle coverage.

**Segment Analysis (CapTech):**
*   **Environment:** SEBI Mutual Funds Regulations, 2026, are strengthening requirements around portfolio transparency, stress testing, and market-abuse deterrence, supporting demand for analytics. Regulatory shifts, including new investment structures like Specialized Investment Funds (SIFs), support demand for robust market data and valuation frameworks.
*   **Outlook Strategy:** Building on regulatory momentum to optimize technology platforms for unified, scalable, and regulation-aligned market data, valuation, and analytics solutions. Leveraging deep regulatory coverage in the mutual fund ecosystem to strengthen demand for valuation frameworks and data-led reporting.
