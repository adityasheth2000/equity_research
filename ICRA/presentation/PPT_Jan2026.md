## Page 5

**Domestic drivers to support growth, even as external outlook remains uncertain**

**1. Rural Demand Outlook (Near Term)**
*   **Status:** Upbeat.
*   **Drivers:** Robust kharif output and healthy trends in rabi sowing.

**2. Government & Private Capex**
*   **Government Capex:** Implicitly set to contract in H2 following frontloading seen in H1 FY2026; this may weigh upon overall growth in H2.
*   **Private Capex:** Ongoing US tariff-related uncertainty is likely to delay private capex plans.

**3. Trade & Exports**
*   **Merchandise Exports:** The imposition of steep US tariffs has adverse implications for India’s merchandise exports in the near term, particularly across labour-intensive sectors.
*   **Services Exports:** Growth in services exports for some segments is expected to remain healthy despite merchandise headwinds.

**4. Urban Consumption Drivers**
*   **Key Factors:** Combination of GST rate rejig, sizeable income-tax relief, 125 bps policy rate cuts leading to lower borrowing costs, and moderation in food inflation.
*   **Outlook:** Expected to augur well for urban consumption.

### Management Commentary & Key Drivers

- **Q3 FY2026 Performance Highlights:**
  - Sharp growth in bank credit segment, driven by GST rationalisation and lower funding costs.
  - Bond issuances moderated YoY due to firm yields, but sequential activity improved — led by NBFCs.
  - Commercial paper activity softened due to tight liquidity.
  - Securitisation remained supported by gold loan, microfinance, and vehicle asset classes.

- **Research & Analytics Segment:**
  - Strong momentum in Market Data and Risk Management (beyond acquisition impact).
  - Knowledge Services recorded broad-based growth despite residual ESG-related impacts.

- **Segment Results Note:**
  - Include amortisation of intangibles from Fintellix acquisition.
  - Exclude one-time Labour Code impact.

**Social Listening / Media Coverage Analysis**

*   **Domestic Aviation Sector Outlook:** ICRA estimates the domestic aviation industry is projected to post a net loss between **Rs 17,000 - 18,000 crore** in the current fiscal year (FY26), driven by lower-than-expected growth.
*   **Macroeconomic Growth Commentary:**
    *   Aditi Nayar of ICRA describes India's economy growing at **8.2%** in the July-September quarter as an "upside surprise" that surpassed expectations.
    *   Management notes this growth appears inflated by a large **"discrepancy segment"** and expects a downward revision going forward.
*   **Capex Trends (Large vs. SME):**
    *   **Large Companies:** Capex estimated to be growing in **double digits (12% to 15%)**.
    *   **SMEs:** Capex observed growing in single digits (**4-5%**).
*   **Sector Specifics:**
    *   **Steel & Cement:** Described as "inching up" to address weak domestic demand.
    *   **Export Focused Businesses:** Characterized as "circumspect."
