## Page 12

**Page Title:** Ratings & ancillary services: Business environment and outlook

**Macro Environment (General)**
*   **Geopolitical/Risk Factors:** Uncertain macro outlook due to geopolitical tensions and steep U.S. tariffs on Indian exports.
*   **Inflation/Interest Rates:** Favorable agricultural trends kept inflation subdued, prompting the RBI to cut rates by 100 bps to support growth.
*   **Outlook Risks:** Tariff uncertainty may weigh on exports and delay private capex until an India-US trade deal is finalized.
*   **Growth Drivers:** Domestic consumption remains strong, supported by tax cuts, rate reductions, and favorable monsoons.

**Segment Analysis: Bond Market**
*   **Performance Metrics (H1 FY2026):**
    *   YoY Growth: 30.7% (Previous Year: 4.6%)
    *   Trend: Issuances surged to an all-time high in Q1 FY2026 supported by rate cuts.
    *   Yields: Rose in Q2 FY2026 as RBI adopted a neutral stance; dipped to 10.9% in bond issuances.
    *   Commercial Paper (CP): Outstanding stayed at elevated levels for the last four quarters despite a sequential dip due to a steep yield curve.
*   **Outlook/Guidance:**
    *   Future issuance dependent on interest rate movements.
    *   Quicker transmission of rate cuts would benefit bonds.
    *   Softening global interest rates could benefit External Commercial Borrowing but impact domestic credit growth.
    *   CPs expected to remain at similar levels; potential benefit if further rate cuts occur.

**Segment Analysis: Bank Credit Outstanding**
*   **Performance Metrics (H1 FY2026):**
    *   YoY Growth: 10.4% (as of Sept) (Previous Year: 12.9%)
    *   Trend: Slowed considerably in Q1 FY2026 as large borrowers preferred cheaper bonds over bank loans.
    *   Q2 FY2026: Bank lending picked up as bond yields rose.
    *   NBFC Segment: Credit had an overhang due to delinquency concerns in certain asset segments.
*   **Outlook/Guidance:**
    *   Large borrower segment credit depends on ability to reprice deposits (likely in Q3 FY2026).
    *   Further RBI rate cuts might slow bank credit as bonds become preferred due to quicker transmission.
    *   MSME segment dependence on bank loans is expected to continue supporting this segment.

**Segment Analysis: Securitisation**
*   **Performance Metrics (H1 FY2026):**
    *   Market Volumes: Estimated ~9% YoY growth (Previous Year: 39%).
    *   Asset Class Mix: Dominant class is vehicle loans (similar to FY2025), followed by home loans & loan against property.
    *   Ratio Trends: Share of PTC (Private Trust Certificates) remained higher than DA (Debt Absorption?).
    *   Pool Sales: Subdued as banks were less concerned about the credit-to-deposits ratio.
*   **Outlook/Guidance:**
    *   Volumes dependent on pickup in NBFC AUMs.
    *   Upbeat domestic consumption outlook should support volumes.
    *   **FY2026 Estimate:** Securitisation volumes estimated to be in the range of Rs ~2.5 trillion.

**Research & Analytics: Business environment and outlook**

**Environment**
*   **Macro/Geopolitical:** Tariff-related uncertainty and geo-political headwinds have led to continued caution in spending.
*   **Outsourcing Industry Pressures:** Facing pressure from Gen AI, rising Global Capability Centers (GCCs) in India, and the proposed U.S. HIRE Act which imposes a 25% tax on foreign service payments.
*   **Client Needs:** Deep domain and Tech expertise are emerging as critical requirements across client segments.
*   **Mutual Funds (MF):** Strong AUM growth observed with a peak of Rs 75 lakhs crore in August 2025 (13% Y-o-Y growth).
*   **Regulatory Drivers:** Regulatory approval of Structured Investment Funds (SIFs) and steady AUM growth are driving demand for advanced solutions in risk analytics, market alerts, stress testing, and reporting.
*   **ESG:** Customized Research offerings and ESG Tools have seen good market traction.
*   **Banking/NBFC Regulation:** Increasing regulatory oversight covering Expected Credit Loss (ECL), Model Governance, and Operational risk.
*   **Risk Management Trends:** Banks and NBFCs are increasingly focusing on sophisticated Early Warning System (EWS) tools.
*   **Technology Adoption:** AI-enabled solutions are likely to see better acceptance.

**Outlook**
*   **Geographic Strategy:** Near-term focus on global markets beyond the US is key.
*   **Service Focus:** Continued focus on Data Management, Technology Services, and value-added segments related to process optimization.
*   **Strategic Capabilities:** Research support coupled with expertise in structured finance will remain critical.
*   **Product Launches:** Launch of Customized Fixed Income Indices in collaboration with FTSE to drive entry into new markets.
*   **Market Penetration:** Penetration into global markets remains a focus area.
*   **Data Demand:** Increasing regulatory oversight will drive demand for market data services.
*   **Differentiation:** Ability to deliver technology-led solutions using AI will be a key differentiator.
*   **M&A / Expansion:** Acquisition of Fintellix to help expand offerings in the Risk Solution space.
*   **Integrated Solutions:** Integrated workflow solutions on Risk Scoring, EWS, asset classification combined with ECL will strengthen IAL’s market position.
*   **Retail Growth:** Growing retail asset base will drive demand for sophisticated data models.

## Page 14

**Topic: Domestic growth drivers and macroeconomic outlook for FY2026 (Source: ICRA Research)**

**1. Rural Demand Outlook**
*   **Positive Drivers:** Healthy trends in kharif sowing, reasonable increase in Minimum Support Prices (MSPs) for the upcoming rabi season, and GST rate rationalisation.
*   **Risk Factors:** Episodes of excess rainfall in September-October 2025 have raised concerns regarding yields of standing crops.

**2. Urban Consumption Drivers & Risks**
*   **Positive Drivers:** Combination of GST rate rejig, sizeable income-tax relief, 100 basis points (bps) rate cuts leading to lower borrowing costs, and moderation in food inflation.
*   **Risk Factors:** Steep US tariffs (and penalties) could lead to job losses in labour-intensive sectors, potentially weighing on urban consumption.

**3. Government & Private Capex**
*   **Government Capex:** Budgeted to rise by **6.6%** in FY2026.
    *   **Trend:** Frontloaded in the first 5 months (5M) of FY2026; implicitly pegged to moderate on a Year-on-Year (YoY) basis for the remainder of the fiscal.
    *   **Impact:** May weigh upon growth in H2 FY2026.
*   **Private Capex:** Ongoing tariff-related uncertainty is likely to delay private capex plans.

**4. Export Sector (Merchandise & Services)**
*   **Merchandise Exports:** Negative implications expected due to the imposition of steep US tariffs.
    *   **Mitigation:** Materialisation of a bilateral trade deal may contain the downside in Q4 FY2026.
*   **Services Exports:** Growth in some segments likely to remain healthy.
    *   **Key Monitorable:** Potential taxes on outsourcing specifically for the IT service export segment.
