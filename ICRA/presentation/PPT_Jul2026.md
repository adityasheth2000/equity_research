This slide outlines the macroeconomic environment for India as projected by ICRA, which serves as a key external factor influencing corporate earnings and valuation.

*   **Real GDP Growth:**

    *   FY2027e: 6.7% (Noted with downside risks)
*   **Nominal GDP Growth:**
    *   FY2026: 8.9%
    *   FY2027e: 13.0%

**2. Inflation Metrics**
*   **CPI Inflation:**

    *   FY2026: 0.7%

**3. Monetary Policy (Repo Rate)**
*   **Outlook:** Status quo likely in August 2026.

**5. External Account**
*   **Current Account Balance / GDP:**
    *   FY2026: -0.6%
    *   FY2027e: -0.9%

## Page 7

**West Asia conflict, weak monsoon poses downside risks to India’s growth outlook in FY2027**

*   **Rural Demand & Agriculture:**
    *   Rural demand is expected to turn cautious due to below-normal rainfall hurting rural sentiments.
    *   Southwest (SW) monsoon deficit is approximately 15% so far.
    *   Healthy volume and distribution of rainfall remain key to buffering agricultural outcomes for the next few quarters.

*   **Urban Demand & Inflation:**
    *   GST rate rationalisation and stable interest rates may support urban demand for some categories.
    *   Risks include an uptick in inflation caused by the poor monsoon and fallout from the West Asia crisis.
    *   Export-oriented sectors impacted by the conflict face a weak outlook, likely weighing on household income and demand.
    *   A potential "back-ended" rate hike in FY2027 could dampen demand in the latter part of the fiscal year.

*   **Government & Private Capex:**
    *   Government of India (GoI) capex is budgeted to rise by 14.3% in FY2027.
    *   Fiscal slippage due to the West Asia crisis could constrain discretionary spending.
    *   Ongoing conflicts and domestic risks (weak monsoon, tariff uncertainties) are expected to weigh on private capex plans, particularly in export and manufacturing segments.

*   **Exports & Services:**
    *   Geopolitical tensions in West Asia are adversely impacting raw material availability and regional demand.
    *   High insurance and shipping costs are expected to weigh on India's merchandise exports in FY2027.
    *   Outlook for services exports remains cautious due to geopolitical uncertainty affecting global growth.

**Management Commentary & Strategic Initiatives**

*   **Overall Performance:** ICRA delivered strong quarterly performance supported by healthy growth in Ratings and sustained momentum in Risk & Analytics (R&A).
*   **Ratings Segment Drivers:**
    *   Performance aided by bank credit growth.
    *   Demand led mainly by the Industries and NBFC segments.
    *   Bond issuances were lower year-on-year due to elevated yields, though they improved towards quarter-end amid better liquidity.
    *   Securitisation volumes were driven entirely by NBFCs (newer/smaller entities using this route for cheaper funding).
*   **Risk & Analytics (R&A) Drivers:**
    *   Growth driven by Fintellix acquisition.
    *   Strong demand for risk, data, and regulatory technology solutions.
    *   Steady KnowTech performance despite automation-led ramp-downs.
*   **M&A Activity:** ICRA acquired the remaining stakes in D2K Technologies India Private Limited and Fintellix India Private Limited, making both its wholly-owned subsidiaries.
