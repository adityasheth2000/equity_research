**1. Regulatory Environment (CAFÉ Norms)**
*   **Target Roadmap:** CAFE 2027 / CAFE III regulations apply from **FY28 to FY32**.
*   **CO2 Emission Targets:** Fleet-level CO2 target tightens from **~88.4 g/km to ~71.5 g/km**.
*   **Strategic Driver:** Regulations serve dual government purpose of reducing oil imports and controlling pollution.

**2. EV Penetration & Powertrain Trends**
*   **Range Gap:** Entry-level EVs currently offer a range of **250–300 KM**, while the ideal target range is **400–500 KM**.
*   **Efficiency Impact:** A **10% weight reduction** can improve EV range by **13.7%**.
*   **Technology:** Potential for structural batteries to reduce weight by **10%**.
*   **Product Strategy:** Shift towards **powertrain agnostic lightweighting components** to address multiple powertrains efficiently.

**3. Material & Safety Trends**
*   **Key Materials:** Focus on **Ultra-high-strength steel** & **aluminum**.
*   **Safety Correlation:** Lightweighting is essential to balance safety with weight reduction; these materials improve **crashworthiness**, **handling**, and **stability**.
