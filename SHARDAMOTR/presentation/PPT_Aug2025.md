The slide outlines specific CO2 emission targets under CAFÉ (Corporate Average Fuel Economy) norms, driving the demand for automotive lightweighting.

| Phase / Target | Year Range / Expectation | CO2 Target (g/km) | Trend |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Phase 1** | 2017 – 2022 | 130 | Baseline |
| **Phase 2** | Post 2022 | 113 | Reduction |
| **Café III** | Expected 2027 | 91.7 | Reduction |
| **Café IV** | Expected 2032 | 70 | Significant Reduction |

**EV Penetration & Powertrain Dynamics**
*   **Powertrain Strategy:** Industry focus is shifting toward "powertrain agnostic lightweighting components."
*   **EV Range Constraints:**
    *   Current Entry-level car range: 250–300 KM.
    *   Ideal Range target: 400–500 KM.
*   **Sensitivity Analysis (Weight vs. Range):** A **10% weight reduction** can improve EV range by **13.7%**.
*   **Component Innovation:** Potential for "structural batteries" to reduce vehicle weight by 10%.

**Safety Regulations & Material Specifications**
*   **Role of Lightweighting:** Essential to balance safety with weight reduction.
*   **Key Materials:** **Ultra-high-strength steel** and **aluminum**.
*   **Performance Benefits:** These materials improve crashworthiness, handling, and stability.

**Strategic Context for Industry**
*   **Government Drivers:** Regulation serves a dual purpose of reducing oil imports and controlling pollution.
*   **Fuel Consumption:** Primary driver remains reducing fuel consumption via lower CO2 emissions.
