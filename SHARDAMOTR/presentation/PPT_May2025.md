## Page 15

**Slide Title: Lightweighting emerges as one of the key megatrends for Auto OEMs globally**

**Topic: Drivers of Lightweighting**

**1. CAFÉ Norms (CO2 emission regulation)**
*   **Primary Goal:** Reduce fuel consumption by lowering CO2 emissions.
*   **CAFÉ Norms CO2 target roadmap:**
    *   **Phase 1 (2017 – 22):** Targeted reduction from 130 g/km.
    *   **Phase 2 (Post 2022):** Targeted reduction from 113 g/km.
    *   **Café III (expected 2027):** Target of 91.7 g/km.
    *   **Café IV (expected 2032):** Target of 70 g/km.

**2. Multiple Powertrains & EV Penetration**
*   **Strategic Focus:** Shift toward **powertrain agnostic lightweighting** components to preserve efficiency across multiple powertrain types.
*   **EV Range Analysis:**
    *   Current Entry-level cars offer a range of **250–300 KM**, while the ideal range is **400–500 KM**.
*   **Weight Efficiency:**
    *   **10% weight reduction** can improve EV range by **13.7%**.
    *   Potential for **structural batteries** to reduce weight by 10%.

**3. Advanced Safety Regulations**
*   **Materials & Safety:** **Ultra-high-strength steel & aluminum** are highlighted as materials that can improve crashworthiness.
*   **Performance:** Lightweight materials exhibit better **handling and stability**.
*   **Balance:** Lightweighting is essential to balance safety with weight reduction.
