## Rationale
The continuation of positive outlook reflects expectation that SMIL will maintain its robust business risk profile, driven by strong market position in exhaust and suspension systems for passenger cars, utility vehicles, LCVs, MHCVs and vans, along with pan-India presence and sustained healthy profitability.

Revenue remained stable at Rs 2,837 Cr in FY25 (vs Rs 2,810 Cr in FY24) due to decrease in market share with exhaust customers because of product diversification strides in suspension and global verticals. However, operating margin improved to over 14% owing to cost efficiency measures. Sustained improvement in revenue along with stable OPM >12% remains monitorable.

## Key Strengths
1. **Established market position**: ~30% value market share in PV & LCV exhaust systems. 3 decades of manufacturing experience.
2. **Strong relationships with OEMs**: Preferred supplier of independent front suspension systems. Caters to both domestic and international markets.
3. **In-house R&D**: Design and testing capabilities for exhaust systems (ATS, Emission, NVH).
4. **Efficient working capital management**: >Rs 700 Cr unencumbered cash (Mar 2025). Nil utilisation of fund-based limit for 12 months through Jan 2025. Receivables and inventory at 40-50 days each.
5. **Strong financial risk profile**: TOLTNW ratio 0.53x (Mar 2025). Networth Rs 1,214.51 Cr. No long-term debt. Interest coverage 73.54x.

## Key Weaknesses
1. **Customer concentration**: Top 2 customers in exhaust business ~75% of total revenue in FY25. Exhaust systems >85% of revenue.
2. **Susceptibility to RM price increases & OEM pricing pressure**: Limited bargaining power with OEMs. Price revisions happen with a lag.

## Liquidity
- Annual cash accrual expected Rs 250-300 Cr against nil term debt obligation
- Cash & bank balance: ~Rs 198.9 Cr (Mar 2025)
- Liquid investments: ~Rs 707 Cr in shares, debentures, MFs (Mar 2025)
- Current ratio: 2.03x (Mar 2025)

## Outlook: Positive
Healthy growth in revenue and sustained operating margin will strengthen credit risk profile.

## Rating Sensitivity
**Upgrade triggers**: Sustained revenue increase + OPM maintaining at 12-13%; Sustenance of healthy financial risk profile
**Downgrade triggers**: Revenue degrowth + OPM declining below 10%; Large debt-funded capex/investments with TOLTNW >1.5x
