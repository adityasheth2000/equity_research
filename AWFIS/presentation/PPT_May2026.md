*   **Operating EBITDA:** Rs. 152 Cr (31% YoY growth).

*   **Annual Revenue:** Rs. 1,493 Cr (24% YoY growth).
*   **Co-working Business Revenue Growth:** 35% YoY.
*   **Operating EBITDA:** Rs. 550 Cr (37% YoY growth).
*   **Operating EBITDA Margins:** 36.8%.
*   **PBT:** Rs. 72 Cr.
*   **ROCE:** 60% (Industry-leading).

*   **New Centres Added:** 41.
*   **New Operational Seats:** 30K.
*   **Total Signed Network:** 266 centres.
*   **Total Seats:** ~184K.
*   **Geography:** 18 cities (spanning Tier 1 and Tier 2 markets).
*   **Client Base:** ~3.5K customers.
*   **Client Concentration:** Multi-centre clients now account for ~48% of the client base.
*   **Cohort Mix:** The 500+ seat cohort contributes 37% of the total portfolio.

**Client Composition & Demand Drivers**
*   **GCC & Enterprise Focus:** Growth anchored by sustained enterprise and GCC (Global Capability Center) demand.
*   **GCC Contribution:** 100+ unique GCC clients contribute 23% of rental revenue.
*   **Fortune 500:** Now a structural and growing part of the revenue base.

**Strategic Initiatives & Supply Strategy**
*   **Strategy:** Capital-light supply strategy continues to evolve.
*   **Asset Mix:**
    *   **MA (Managed Asset):** Remains the backbone.
    *   **SL (Service Lease):** Deployed selectively for premium enterprise mandates.
    *   **Partial MO Structures:** Scaling.
    *   **Partnerships:** Advanced discussions with marquee developers for strategic workspace partnerships.
    *   **Note:** The mix is a function of demand quality, never a fixed target.

**Outlook (FY27)**
*   **Status:** Business entering FY27 in its strongest position.
*   **Foundation:** Healthy occupancy, expanding margins, industry-leading returns on capital.
*   **Pipeline:** Quality pipeline already committed.
*   **Management Commentary:** "We enter FY27 with clarity of strategy, depth of execution, and full confidence in what lies ahead."
