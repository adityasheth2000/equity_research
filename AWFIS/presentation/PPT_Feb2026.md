*   **Awfis Advantage**
    *   **Low CAPEX Expansion:** Enabling rapid scale and growth.
    *   **Higher ROCE:** Capital light, high returns.
    *   **Low Risk Profile:** A shared-risk structure with landlords supports a resilient business model designed for long-term tenures.
