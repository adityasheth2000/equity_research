*   **Operating EBITDA:** Grew 44% YoY
*   **Revenue:** Expanded 28%
*   **PAT (Profit After Tax):** Rose 49%

**Business Metrics & Portfolio Composition**
*   **Total Portfolio:** India’s largest flexible workspace footprint.
*   **Centers:** 247 centers
*   **Seats:** ~170k seats
*   **Geographic Reach:** Across 18 cities
*   **Client Base:** Around 3.4k clients
*   **High-Value Clients (Cohorts >500 seats):** Represent 34% of the total portfolio, indicating high stickiness and maturity.
*   **Premium Asset Mix:**
    *   21 Gold centres
    *   5 Elite centres
    *   Located in Grade A+ developments across key business districts.
*   **Tier 2 City Expansion:** Supply in Tier 2 markets increased by ~28% YoY.

**Strategic Initiatives & Outlook**
*   **Value Migration Strategy:** Moving up the value curve by focusing on Grade A buildings and premium locations to capture GCCs (Global Capability Centers), large enterprises, and premium clients.
*   **Client Diversification:** Noting entry of new mid-sized GCCs and expansion of long-standing clients to cater to the full spectrum of requirements.
*   **Corporate Restructuring (Awfis Transform):**
    *   Awfis Transform (Design & Build vertical) is being subsidiary-d.
    *   Goal: Gain flexibility to scale independently and present standalone financials.
    *   Strategy: Evolve as a distinct growth vertical targeting new sectors like retail, hospitality, and commercial segments to broaden the customer base.
*   **Pan-India Presence:** Continued expansion beyond metro cities into Tier 2 markets for balanced growth.

**Competitive Advantages**
*   **Scale & Execution:** Deep operational expertise developed over a decade.
*   **Integrated Ecosystem:** Combines core co-working business, allied services, and specialized design/build expertise (via Awfis Transform).
*   **Market Positioning:** Well-positioned to capture opportunities in both established (Tier 1/Gold/Elite) and emerging markets.
