> *Interpretation*: Demand growth has stalled between 9M FY’25 and 9M FY’26, indicating potential saturation or macroeconomic headwinds affecting electricity demand.

---

### **Average Selling Prices (MCP = Market Clearing Price, Rs./unit)**

#### **Day-Ahead Market (DAM MCP)**
- **9M FY’24**: ₹5.36  
- **9M FY’25**: ₹4.48 (-16.4% YoY)  
- **9M FY’26**: ₹3.85 (-14.0% YoY)  

#### **Real-Time Market (RTM MCP)**
- **9M FY’24**: ₹5.13  
- **9M FY’25**: ₹4.26 (-17.0% YoY)  
- **9M FY’26**: ₹3.56 (-16.4% YoY)  

> *Trend*: Both DAM and RTM prices have declined consistently over the period, with RTM consistently trading at a slight discount to DAM. This reflects increased sell-side liquidity — confirmed by the footer note stating “+43% YoY” improvement in sell-side liquidity driving price declines.

---

### **Key Insights for Equity Research**

1. **Volume Growth Stagnation**: Flat volume growth in 9M FY’26 may signal limited near-term revenue upside unless pricing stabilizes or new segments emerge.
   
2. **Price Compression**: Sustained decline in ASPs across both markets suggests competitive pressure or oversupply dynamics. For IEX, this could compress transaction fees if priced per unit or impact total traded value.

3. **Sell-Side Liquidity Surge (+43% YoY)**: A positive structural development — more sellers imply deeper markets, better execution quality, and potentially higher trader participation. May support long-term platform stickiness despite short-term margin pressure from lower prices.

4. **Market Mechanism Differentiation**: DAM remains premium-priced vs RTM, suggesting traders value certainty/intraday flexibility. IEX’s ability to maintain this spread indicates strong product differentiation.

5. **Data Source Credibility**: Data sourced from CEA (Central Electricity Authority) and IEX itself — high reliability for industry benchmarking.

---

### **Risk Factors Implied**
- Continued price erosion could reduce exchange revenue if fee structures are volume- or value-based.
- Regulatory changes affecting settlement cycles, participant eligibility, or pricing caps could disrupt current dynamics.
- Macro slowdown impacting industrial/commercial power demand further.

---

### **Management Commentary / Footer Insight**
> “Demand growth flat in 9M FY’26; Prices declined due to improved sell side liquidity (+43%YoY)”

This confirms management acknowledges the volume stagnation but frames the price decline as a sign of healthy market maturation (liquidity depth), not weakness. Likely intended to reassure investors that underlying market fundamentals remain robust despite headline price drops.
