**Topic: New Market Models: FDRE & RTC, BESS**

**Firm Dispatch RE (FDRE) & Round The Clock (RTC) RE**
*   **Mandatory Inclusion:** FDRE mandates the inclusion of Energy Storage Systems (ESS) to provide dispatchable renewable energy power (e.g., assured peak power, load following).
*   **RTC Definition:** Defined as round-the-clock renewable supply complemented by supply from other generation technologies.
*   **Liquidity Impact:** Excess generation (~20%) from FDRE and RTC tenders is expected to increase sell liquidity at exchanges.

**Chart Data: FDRE - Sample Profile**
*   **Visual Description:** A blue area chart illustrating a 24-hour generation profile.
*   **Data Points:**
    *   **Baseline:** Constant level at ~150 units (y-axis scale 100-1500).
    *   **Daytime Dip:** Significant reduction in output during mid-day hours, dropping to a low of approximately 600-700 units.
    *   **Evening Ramp:** Steep upward trend starting around 18:00-19:00, returning to the 1500 unit baseline.
    *   **Interpretation:** Demonstrates the "duck curve" phenomenon where solar generation drops sharply in the evening while demand peaks, necessitating storage or complementary generation.

**Battery Energy Storage Systems (BESS)**
*   **Market Requirement:** According to CEA, BESS storage of 47GW / 236 GWh is required by 2032.
*   **Trading Mechanism:** Charging occurs during Non-Peak hours; Discharging occurs during Peak Hours via Exchanges.

**Other Developments (Tenders & Awards):**
*   **VGF Based BESS Tranches:**
    *   Tranche 1: 13,200 MWh (FY24 to FY27).
    *   Tranche 2: 30,000 MWh (FY25 onwards).
*   **Q4 FY’26 Awards:**
    *   **NVVN:** Awarded 250 MW (2-Hour, 2 Cycle) tender at Rs. 1.77/unit.
    *   **GUVNL:** Awarded 335 MW (2-Hour, 2 Cycle) tender at Rs. 2.06/unit.
    *   **SJVN-Haryana:** Awarded 250 MW (2-Hour, 2 Cycle) tender at Rs. 1.93/unit under VGF scheme.
*   **Merchant Trades:**
    *   **Dec'26:** First merchant BESS trades scheduled at IEX from Juniper Green Energy, followed by ACME Solar and Adani Green.

**Reducing Battery Capex Costs**
*   **Price Trend:** AP Transco awarded a 1,000 MW BESS tender in Nov 2025 at Rs 1.45/unit, noted as the lowest discovered price.

## Page 33

**Page Title: Growth Levers**

This slide outlines macro-level industry growth drivers and specific market opportunities for IGX (Indian Gas Exchange), focusing on policy targets, infrastructure expansion, domestic production increases, and regulatory shifts that create demand for gas trading.

### 1. Government’s target for gas sector
*   **Natural Gas Share:** Targeted increase in the energy basket share from ~6% to 15%.
*   **Infrastructure Expansion:**
    *   **National Gas Grid:** Development of "One Nation One Gas Grid."
    *   **CNG Stations:** Expansion from 8,600 to 18,300 stations.
    *   **PNG Connections:** Increase from 16 million to 120 million connections.

### 2. Infrastructure Development
*   **LNG Terminals:** Capacity expected to grow from current 55.7 MMTPA to 70+ MMTPA within 3 years.
*   **Transmission Pipeline:** Network length expected to expand from ~25,000 km to 35,000+ km.
*   **City Gas Distribution (CGDs):** Following the completion of the 12A CGD bidding round, coverage is authorized for ~100% of the population and area (excluding islands).

### 3. Domestic Gas Production
*   **ONGC:** Aims to ramp up production from the KG-D5 block from 2.7 MMSCMD to ~10 MMSCMD.
*   **RIL:** Aims to increase production in the KG-D6 Block from ~30 MMSCMD to ~34-35 MMSCMD.
*   **New Fields:** New fields expected to come online including DSF II, Daman Upside, KG basin, Kutch-Saurashtra Blocks, Mahanadi basin, and CBM blocks.

### 4. Market Developments & Regulatory Changes
*   **Tariff Structure:** Implementation of Single zone/Entry-Exit pipeline tariff.
*   **Taxation:** Natural Gas included under GST.
*   **Regulatory Framework:** Implementation of new Access Code Regulations and Incorporation of TSO (Transport System Operator).
*   **Clean Fuel Mandates:** Bans on polluting fuels; introduction of carbon tax mechanisms to incentivize clean fuel usage.
*   **CBG Blending:** Starting FY26, mandatory blending of Compressed Biogas (CBG) at 1%, scaling up to 5% by FY29, intended to mainstream CBG into the gas supply chain.

### 5. IGX Specific Outlook & Guidance
*   **Total Consumption Growth:** Gas consumption expected to increase from present ~200 mmscmd to 400 mmscmd by 2030.
*   **IGX Market Share:** IGX share in overall gas consumption expected to increase from present 3% to 4-5% by 2030.
    *   **Volume Target:** ~250 Mn MMBTU by 2030.
    *   **Growth Rate:** CAGR of ~36%.
