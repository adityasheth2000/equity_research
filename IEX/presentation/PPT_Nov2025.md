This slide presents a comparative analysis of India’s installed electricity generation capacity as of September 2025 versus projections for FY 2030, sourced from the Central Electricity Authority (CEA) and Ministry of Power (MoP). It highlights a structural shift toward non-fossil fuel sources.

---

**FY 2030 CEA Projections (Total: 849 GW)**  
*Projected breakdown by source:*

| Source          | Capacity (GW) | % Share |
|-----------------|---------------|---------|
| RE & Hydro      | 507           | 60%     |
| Thermal Cap     | 327           | 38%     |
| Nuclear         | 15            | 2%      |

---

**Key Observations & Implications for Equity Research:**

- **Non-Fossil Fuel Milestone Achieved Early**: The slide notes that India will achieve 50% share of non-fossil fuel in installed capacity by Sep’25 — five years ahead of its 2030 target. This suggests accelerated policy execution or private investment momentum in renewables/hydro/nuclear.
  
- **Growth Visibility**: Total installed capacity expected to grow at ~CAGR of 5.4% over 5 years (from 501 GW to 849 GW), driven almost entirely by RE & Hydro (adding 260 GW vs. +82 GW for thermal, +7 GW for nuclear).

- **Sectoral Shift**: Non-fossil fuels are projected to dominate with 60% share by FY30, up from 49% in Sep’25. Thermal remains significant but declining in relative terms.

- **Nuclear Expansion Modest**: Nuclear capacity grows only from 8 GW to 15 GW — indicating limited near-term contribution despite strategic importance.

- **Implication for IEX/Market Participants**: As renewable penetration increases, market dynamics may shift toward more volatile pricing due to intermittency, potentially increasing demand for balancing services, storage integration, or flexible trading mechanisms — relevant for exchanges like IEX.

- **Risk Factor**: Over-reliance on projected CEA numbers without considering grid absorption, land acquisition delays, or supply chain constraints could lead to overshoots or undershoots. Also, regulatory changes affecting subsidy structures or auction timelines could alter trajectory.

**Topic: Growth Driver 2: New Products & Regulatory Developments**

**1. 11-Month Contract, Green RTM, Peak Power Contract**
*   **DEEP Platform Volume:** Currently ~40 BU (Billion Units). Trade up to 11 months is expected to shift DEEP platform volume to Exchanges.
*   **Regulatory Status:** Awaiting CERC approval on petition.
*   **Green RTM:** Petition in CERC for launching Green RTM heard; public comments closed. Final order reserved. This creates an additional market for RE developers and obligated customers to meet sustainability goals.
*   **Peak Power:** Petition filed with CERC for peak DAM (Day-Ahead Market) and RTM segments to facilitate trading during high-demand hours.

**2. LPSC Rules (Long-Term Power Sales Contracts)**
*   **Un-requisitioned (URS) Power:** As per LPSC Rules, generators are mandated to offer URS power on exchanges. State Govt owned generators are also required to offer URS power.
*   **Volume Impact:** Additional sell available in both RTM and DAM, expected to increase further.

**3. Optimisation Potential**
*   **Utilities/C&I:** Opportunity for utilities and Commercial & Industrial (C&I) sectors to replace high variable cost power by Exchange procurement.
*   **Solar Hours:** Additional buy demand particularly during solar hours.

**4. REC Potential, VPPAs (Renewable Energy Certificates & Virtual Power Purchase Agreements)**
*   **REC Fungibility:** Shift to a Single RECs market instead of separate Solar and Non-Solar segments.
*   **Compliance:** Stricter penalties by MoP (Ministry of Power), under Section 26(3) Energy Conservation Act for non-compliances.
*   **Trading Mechanics:** Floor price of REC eliminated and fortnightly trading of RECs enabled.
*   **Inventory Size:** REC Inventory is ~3.4 crore certificates.
*   **VPPAs:** Draft guidelines issued for Virtual Power Purchase Agreements. Recognizes power exchanges as authorized platforms for sale of RE power under VPPAs in the collective segment of exchanges.

**5. Carbon Markets**
*   **Regulation:** CERC issued draft regulations for trading of Carbon Credit Certificates for obligated and non-obligated entities through power exchanges.
*   **Market Size:** GEI targets notified for 271 obligated entities across sectors.

**New Market Models: FDRE & RTC, BESS**

**Firm Dispatch RE & Round The Clock RE (FDRE & RTC)**
*   **Mandatory ESS:** FDRE mandates the inclusion of Energy Storage Systems (ESS) to provide dispatchable renewable energy power (e.g., assured peak power, load following).
*   **RTC Definition:** Defined as round-the-clock renewable supply complemented by supply from any other generation technology.
*   **Excess Generation:** Approximately 20% excess generation from FDRE and RTC tenders is available to increase sell liquidity at Exchanges.
*   **Sample Profile Data:** A chart titled "FDRE- Sample Profile" illustrates a load curve with capacity ranging between ~600 MW and 1500 MW over a 24-hour period.

**Battery Energy Storage Systems (BESS)**
*   **Demand Outlook (CEA):** Central Electricity Authority projects a requirement for 47 GW / 236 GWh of BESS storage by 2032.
*   **Operational Mechanism:** Charging (Non-Peak hours) and Discharging (Peak Hours) can be executed through Exchanges.

**Other Developments & Tenders**
*   **VGF Based BESS Tranches:**
    *   **Tranche 1:** 13,200 MWh; Fiscal Years 2024 to 2027.
    *   **Tranche 2:** 30,000 MWh; Fiscal Year 2025 onwards.
*   **Recent Awarded Tenders (Q2 FY'26):**
    *   **SJVN / KPTCL:** Awarded tender of 375 MW / 1,500 MWh (2-Hour, 2 Cycle) @ Rs. 3.59 Lac/MW/Month.
    *   **SJVN / KPTCL:** Awarded tender of 150 MW / 300 MWh (2-Hour, 2 Cycle) @ Rs. 2.54 Lac/MW/Month.
    *   *Note:* Both awarded under the VGF scheme.

**Reducing Battery Capex Costs**
*   **SECI Tender (2023):** Price discovered was Rs 10.83 lacs/MW/Month for a 500 MW tender.
*   **RUVNL Tender (Oct 2025):** Awarded a 1,000 MW / 2,000 MWh BESS tender at Rs 1.78 Lacs/MW/Month.

## Page 33

**Page Title: Growth Levers**

This slide outlines the macroeconomic and sector-specific tailwinds driving growth for Indian Gas Exchange (IGX). It details government targets, infrastructure expansion, domestic production ramp-ups, and regulatory changes that collectively support increased natural gas consumption.

### 1. Government’s Target for Gas Sector
*   **Natural Gas Share:** Increasing share of Natural Gas in the energy basket from current ~6% to 15%.
*   **National Gas Grid:** Development of "One Nation One Gas Grid."
*   **CNG Stations:** Expansion from 7,000 to 17,500 stations.
*   **PNG Connections:** Increase from 13 million to 120 million connections.

### 2. Infrastructure Development
*   **LNG Terminals:** Capacity expected to grow from current 52.7 MMTPA to 70+ MMTPA within 3 years.
*   **Transmission Pipeline:** Network expected to grow from ~25,000 km to 35,000 km+ in coming years.
*   **City Gas Distribution (CGDs):** With completion of the 12A CGD bidding round, coverage is authorized for ~100% of population and area (except islands).

### 3. Domestic Gas Production
*   **ONGC:** Aims to ramp up gas production from the KG-D5 block from 2.7 MMSCMD to ~10 MMSCMD.
*   **RIL:** Aims to increase production in KG-D6 Block from ~30 MMSCMD to ~34-35 MMSCMD.
*   **New Fields:** Expected to come online include DSF II, Daman Upside, KG basin, Kutch-Saurashtra Blocks, Mahanadi basin, and CBM blocks.

### 4. Market Developments & Regulatory Changes
*   **Tariff Reforms:** PNRB's proposed UFT (Uniform Tariff Framework) reforms of pipeline tariffs notified; awaiting implementation.
*   **GST:** Natural Gas brought under GST.
*   **Access Code:** Implementation of new Access Code Regulations and Incorporation of TSO (Transport System Operator).
*   **Pollution Control:** Mandates on ban on use of polluting fuels; carbon tax mechanisms to push use of clean fuels.
*   **CBG Blending:** Starting FY26, mandatory blending of Compressed Bio-Gas (CBG) at 1%, scaling up to 5% by FY29. This will mainstream CBG into the gas supply chain.

### 5. Volume Outlook & IGX Market Share
*   **Total Gas Consumption:** Expected to increase from present ~200 mmscmd to 400 mmscmd by 2030.
*   **IGX Market Share:** Expected to increase from present 2% to 4-5% by 2030.
    *   **Volume Target:** ~250 Mn MMBTU.
    *   **Growth Rate:** CAGR of ~36%.
