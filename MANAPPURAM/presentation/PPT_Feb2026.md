### Slide Topic: Manappuram Today / Company DNA / Growth Strategy

**Market Position & Scale Metrics**
*   **Market Position:** #2 Listed lender in gold loans in India (core product) with nation-wide reach.
*   **Branch Network:** 5,000+ branches.
*   **Workforce:** c. 40,000 employees (consolidated basis).
*   **Customer Base:** 2.6 million active customers.
*   **AUM Proxy (Gold Safekeeping):** Safekeeping of c. 58.9 Metric Tonnes (MT) of household gold jewellery on behalf of customers.

**Strategic Initiatives & Expansion**
*   **M&A:** Acquired and scaled Asirvad Microfinance.
*   **Product Innovation:** Pioneer in online gold loan product ('OGL') and cellular vaulting mechanism.
*   **Diversification:** Calibrated approach to growth in other loan products:
    *   Small ticket home loans
    *   Used vehicles for self-employed customers
    *   MSME loans
*   **Growth Strategy:**
    *   Create market for gold loans as a mainstream, convenient, and affordable product.
    *   Become a "One stop shop" for meeting customer’s borrowing and protection needs (small ticket loans to underbanked customers).

**Financial Health & Risk Management**
*   **Capitalization:** Well capitalized with a **24.6% Tier 1 ratio**.
*   **Liquidity Management:** Strong Asset Liability Management (ALM) and access to diversified sources of funds.
*   **Risk Culture:** Conservative credit and risk management culture with strict adherence to processes, including timely auctions.
*   **Operational Rigor:** Perfected management of appraisal, custodial, and valuation risks inherent in gold lending.
*   **Technology Focus:** Increasing focus on technology for sourcing, underwriting credit, and managing risk.

**Governance**
*   Board-driven governance process.
