### Slide Title: Vision & Company DNA | Manappuram Today

**Market Position & Scale**
*   **Core Business Ranking:** #2 lender in gold loans in India (core product).
*   **Network:** Branch network of 5,000+ branches.
*   **Workforce:** Employee strength of 50,000+ on consolidated basis.
*   **Customer Base:** Serving 2.6 mn active customers.
*   **Asset Under Management (Safekeeping):** c.56 MT of household gold jewellery on behalf of customers.

**Operational Strategy & Business Model**
*   **Product Innovation:** Pioneer in process innovation with "online gold loan product ‘OGL’" and "cellular vaulting mechanism."
*   **Diversification:** Acquired and scaled Asirvad Microfinance.
*   **Product Mix Expansion:** Calibrated growth strategy into other relevant loan products for the customer base, specifically:
    *   Small ticket home loans
    *   Used vehicles for self-employed customers
    *   MSME loans
*   **Brand Proposition:** Trusted brand offering safekeeping services.
*   **Technology:** Increasing focus on technology for sourcing and underwriting credit, as well as managing risk.

**Financial Health & Risk Management**
*   **Capitalization:** Well capitalized with a 30.91% Tier 1 ratio.
*   **Funding:** Strong Asset Liability Management (ALM) and access to diversified sources of funds.
*   **Risk Culture:** Conservative credit and risk management culture with strict adherence to processes, including "timely auctions."
*   **Operational Rigor:** Perfection of appraisal, custodial, and valuation risks inherent in gold lending.

**Growth Strategy**
*   **Primary Objective:** Create market for gold loans as a mainstream, convenient, and affordable product.
*   **Strategic Goal:** Evolution into a "one stop shop" for meeting customer’s borrowing and protection needs (ranging from small ticket loans to underbanked customers).
