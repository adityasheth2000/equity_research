**Agarwal Industrial Corporation Limited (AICL) Business Overview**

**Business Segments & Operational Model**
The company operates across three primary verticals, demonstrating a vertically integrated business model from shipping to road logistics:

1.  **Vessels and Chartering:**
    *   **Customers:** 3rd Party Customers and Oil Refineries/Intermediaries (Middle East).
    *   **Cargo:** Handles shipping of VG 30, VG 40, and Bitumen.
2.  **Bulk Bitumen and Allied Products:**
    *   **Process:** Imports via Ports and Storage (India) → Manufacturing Facilities and Storage (India).
    *   **Products:** Processes into Allied Bitumen Products and Bulk Bitumen (sold to non-PSU and PSU customers).
3.  **Logistics (Road Transport):**
    *   **Bitumen:** Serves Industrial, Construction, and Road Construction Customers.
    *   **LPG:** Sources LPG from Refineries in India.

## Agarwal Industrial Corporation Limited (AICL) Investment Case

**Market Leadership in Bitumen Industry**
*   **Company Position:** Largest private-sector bitumen trading and product manufacturing company in India. Operates an integrated model with shipping vessels and fleet logistics.
*   **Import Volumes:** Imported 486,546 MT of bitumen from Middle Eastern refineries/intermediaries during FY2025.
*   **Growth:** 9.2% YoY increase in imports (FY2025).
*   **Revenue Mix:**
    *   72% from bulk sales to road construction firms (direct and via PSUs).
    *   28% from allied products.
*   **Diversification:** LPG logistics division holds a significant market position and offers operational synergies with bitumen logistics.

**PAN India Manufacturing Facilities**
*   **Manufacturing Base:** 7 manufacturing facilities.
*   **Product Portfolio:** Paving Grade, Industrial Grade, Crumb Rubber Modified Bitumen, and other allied products.
*   **Capacity:** Combined trading and manufacturing capacity of 35,125 MTPA.
*   **Supply Chain Logic:**
    *   PSU customers receive bulk bitumen from port storage.
    *   Non-PSUs are served from both manufacturing and port locations.
*   **Industry Trends:** Increasing focus on sustainable practices and modified bitumen products.

**Integrated Bitumen Manufacturing and Logistics Platform**
*   **Fleet Assets:** Owns 11 shipping vessels with a total capacity of 113,549 MT.
    *   *Usage:* Transportation of petroleum products and third-party chartering.
*   **Logistics Efficiency:** 60% of bulk bitumen supplied to AICL via its own shipping vessels.
*   **Port Infrastructure:** 7 ports with storage capacity of 30,500 MT.
*   **Distribution Network:** 350+ owned bitumen logistics fleet (trucks) to supply non-PSU and PSU customers. Supported by low-cost workshops and petrol pumps.
*   **Strategic Moat:** Vessel and fleet ownership provides logistical advantages in sourcing and ensures delivery reliability.

**Attractive Infrastructure Sector Dynamics**
*   **Growth Drivers:** India’s bitumen market expected to outperform cement for roads, driven by government connectivity programs (Bharatmala and PMGSY).
*   **Expansion Strategy:**
    *   **Geographic Expansion:** Plans to enter the bitumen market in North India to diversify customer base.
    *   **Capex (South India):** Capital investment of **Rs. 40 Cr** in a new 40,000 MT storage terminal at Mangalore Port to strengthen supply chain.
    *   **Capex (East India):** New manufacturing facility of **Rs. 5 Cr** in Guwahati to address eastern states road construction and infrastructure projects.

**Strategic Advantages and Outlook**
*   **Competitive Edge:** Integrated scale (vessels, storage, logistics) allows for competitive bidding, high customer service, and reduced throughput rates.
*   **Barrier to Entry:** Integrated infra-ancillary platform creates high barriers to entry.
*   **Future Visibility:** Positioned to capitalize on India’s infrastructure spending.
*   **Market Opportunity:** Bitumen consumption in India is one of the lowest among the top 10 countries by road network, indicating significant near-term growth opportunities.

## Page 13

**Slide Title:** Bitumen Product Range (2/2)

**Business Scope & Product Diversification**
The company offers a range of bitumen-based products with specific value-added features and end-use applications:

**1. Polymer Modified Bitumen (PMB)**
*   **Product特性:** Bitumen enhanced with polymers to improve chemical and physical characteristics.
*   **Advantages:** Higher softening point, reduced brittleness, improved elastic recovery, enhanced adhesion, and resistance to stripping/cracking/rutting.
*   **End-Use Industry:** Infrastructure projects requiring durability under extreme climatic and traffic conditions.

**2. Bitumen Emulsion**
*   **Product Description:** Dewatered bitumen stabilized by emulsifiers; prepared at high temps but applied at ambient temperatures.
*   **Advantages:** Can be used on wet aggregates, allows for quick repairs, better tack coats, season-agnostic application, reduced air pollution, and long storage stability.
*   **End-Use Industry:** Road construction and maintenance.

**3. Byproducts / Waterproofing Materials (Value-Added Diversification)**
The company produces engineered materials for water ingress, corrosion, and structural stress protection. Specific SKUs include:
*   **Bitubond:** Cold-applied adhesive for bitufelt (used on metal, masonry, transport roofs).
*   **Bituplast:** Plastic waterproofing compound for sealing cracks/joints.
*   **Bituminous Black:** Anti-corrosive paint (safe for steel/tanks).
*   **Bitukote:** Water-based coating for insulated pipes, roofs, and buried structures.
*   **Bituprimer:** Primer for bonding on concrete, masonry, and metal.
*   **Cable Compound:** Bitumen insulator for cable joints.
*   **Bitufelt:** Reinforced felt for universal roof waterproofing (rot- and fire-resistant).
