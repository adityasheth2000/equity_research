## Page 15

**Bitumen Product Range (2/2)**

*   **Polymer Modified Bitumen (PMB)**
    *   **Product Overview:** PMB is bitumen enhanced by adding polymers. These modifiers alter chemical and physical characteristics to improve performance under varying temperatures, weather, and heavy traffic.
    *   **Key Advantages:** Increases softening point, reduces brittleness, improves elastic recovery, enhances adhesion to aggregates, boosts water resistance, and provides better resistance to stripping, low-temperature cracking, fatigue, and rutting.
    *   **End-Use Industry:** Primarily used in infrastructure projects requiring durability under extreme climatic and traffic conditions.

*   **Bitumen Emulsion**
    *   **Product Overview:** Dispersions of bitumen in water stabilized by emulsifiers. Prepared at high temperatures and applied at ambient temperatures. Enables bitumen to act as a binder with mineral aggregates, providing mechanical strength in road construction.
    *   **Key Advantages:** Can be used on wet aggregates, enables quick repairs, offers better tack coats, works in any season without heating, reduces air pollution, and has long storage stability.
    *   **End-Use Industry:** Primarily used in road construction and maintenance across various environmental conditions.

*   **Byproducts / Waterproofing Materials**
    *   **Product Overview:** Products engineered to protect against water ingress, corrosion, and structural stress, serving diverse construction and industrial needs.
    *   **Product Portfolio:**
        *   *Bitubond:* Cold-applied adhesive for bitufelt; ideal for metal, masonry, and transport roofs.
        *   *Bituplast:* Plastic waterproofing compound; seals cracks, joints, and hard-to-reach areas.
        *   *Bituminous Black:* Anti-corrosive paint; safe for steel, tanks, and humid environments.
        *   *Bitukote:* Water-based coating; shields insulated pipes, roofs, and buried structures.
        *   *Bituprimer:* Primer for strong bonding on concrete, masonry, and metal surfaces.
        *   *Cable Compound:* Bitumen insulator for cable joints, offering thermal and moisture resistance.
        *   *Bitufelt:* Reinforced felt for universal roof waterproofing; rot- and fire-resistant.
