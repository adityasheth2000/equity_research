## Detailed Rationale

CRISIL Ratings has upgraded the long-term bank facilities of Agarwal Industrial Corporation Limited (AICL) to **'CRISIL A/Stable'** from **'CRISIL A-/Stable'**. This upgrade reflects a sustained improvement in AICL's credit profile. Key operational milestones include the sustenance of revenue above Rs. 2125 crores in Fiscal 2024, with revenues expected to reach more than Rs. 2250-2300 crores for Fiscal 2025, driven by volume increases and healthy orders in the Bitumen and shipping segments. Operating margins improved to 8.18% in Fiscal 2024 and are expected to sustain above 9% for Fiscal 2025.

Consequently, liquidity has strengthened, with cash accruals of Rs. 136 crores in Fiscal 2024 and expected accruals of more than Rs. 150 crores in the medium term, adequate to meet repayment obligations of Rs. 59-73 crores. The financial risk profile remains comfortable with adequate debt protection metrics. The rating also reflects the promoters' extensive experience, diversified revenue profile, prudent risk management, and strong financials. However, these are partially offset by exposure to end-user industry cyclicality, raw material price volatility, and supplier concentration risk.

---

## Key Rating Drivers — Strengths

*   **Established Market Position in the Bitumen Industry:**
    *   Promoters possess over four decades of business experience and strong relationships with suppliers and customers.
    *   Strategically located manufacturing and storage units reduce transportation costs.
    *   Significant scale increase with a CAGR of 33% over the past three years ending Fiscal 2024.
    *   Revenue grew from Rs. 904 crores in Fiscal 2021 to Rs. 2125 crores in Fiscal 2024.
*   **Diversified Revenue Profile with Established Clientele:**
    *   Integrated solutions including bitumen manufacturing, trading, and transportation (LPG, bitumen).
    *   Shipping chartering business offsets freight expenses (moderation of 60-70%).
    *   Transported 60% of bitumen through own vessels in FY2024.
    *   Established customer and supplier network supports repeat business across segments.
*   **Strong Financial Risk Profile:**
    *   Net worth increased to Rs. 506 crores as of March 31, 2024 (from Rs. 398 crores in 2023).
    *   Gearing and TOLANW ratios were 0.68x and 1.20x, respectively (March 31, 2024).
    *   Debt protection metrics remain healthy with interest coverage of 8.33 times and Net Cash Accruals to Total Debt (NCATD) of 0.39 times for Fiscal 2024.
*   **Prudent Risk Management Strategies:**
    *   Average inventory maintained is low at around 16-24 days over the past three years.
    *   Majority of purchases are order-backed.
    *   Counterparty risk mitigated by large, diversified clientele and long-term client relationships (3-4 decades).
    *   Foreign exchange exposure passed on to customers.

---

## Key Rating Drivers — Weaknesses

*   **Susceptibility of Operating Margin to Raw Material Price Volatility:**
    *   Bitumen is a derivative of crude oil, leading to extreme price volatility.
    *   Operating margins have ranged from 6.3% to 8.2% in the past five fiscals through Fiscal 2024 due to inability to fully or immediately pass on cost increases.
*   **Exposure to Cyclicality in End-User Industry:**
    *   End-users in infrastructure, industrial gases, and power industries are cyclical.
    *   Economic slowdowns can stretch debtors; debtors more than 6 months stood at Rs. 66.28 crores as of March 31, 2024.
*   **Exposure to Supplier Concentration Risk:**
    *   High supplier concentration limits price negotiation capability.
    *   Majorly procures bitumen from suppliers like AGEE EXIM DMCC and PETRO ADDICHEM FZC.

## Liquidity

*   **Assessment:** Strong.
*   **Cash Accruals:** Expected to be Rs. 154-182 crores annually against repayment obligations of around Rs. 59-73 crores over the medium term.
*   **Cash & Cash Equivalents:** Rs. 57.65 crores (as of September 30, 2024).
*   **Liquid Mutual Funds:** Rs. 33.17 crores (as of September 30, 2024).
*   **Bank Limit Utilisation:** Average utilization of fund-based limits was 43% over the 12 months ended October 2024.
*   **Sufficiency:** CRISIL Ratings expects internal accruals, cash equivalents, and unutilized bank lines to be sufficient to meet repayment obligations and incremental working capital requirements over the medium term.

---

## Outlook & Rating Sensitivity

**Outlook: Stable**

CRISIL Ratings believes the group will benefit from extensive promoter experience, diversified businesses, and low debt levels.

**Upward Factors:**
*   Significant improvement in scale of operations.
*   Sustained operating margins above 10%, leading to higher cash accruals.
*   Improvement in debt protection metrics (Debt to EBITDA < 1.5 times).
*   Sustenance of financial risk profile and strong supplier relationships.

**Downward Factors:**
*   Decline in net cash accruals below Rs. 75 crore on account of revenue or margin decline.
*   Significantly higher-than-expected debt-funded capex leading to gearing above 0.75 times.
*   Substantial increase in working capital requirements or large debt-funded capex weakening liquidity and financial profile.
