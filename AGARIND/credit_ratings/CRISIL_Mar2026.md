## Detailed Rationale

Crisil Ratings has revised its outlook on the long-term bank facilities of Agarwal Industrial Corporation Limited (AICL) to 'Negative' from 'Stable', while reaffirming the rating at 'Crisil A'. The revision reflects moderation in the group's operating performance, marked by a decline in revenues and profitability margins in the 9-month period ended December 2025. Revenues declined by around 20% and operating profitability by around 260 basis points (bps) year-on-year for April-Dec 2025. This was driven by moderate demand for bitumen as early and extended monsoons slowed construction activity, as well as a decline in realizations. Additionally, the shipping segment was impacted by geopolitical disruptions affecting shipping movements, particularly given the company's exposure to the Middle East, a major supplier base.

While revenue and profitability may remain monitorable due to ongoing Middle East situations, AICL's credit profile is supported by a strong financial risk profile, adequate liquidity, extensive promoter experience, and an established market position in bitumen products. These strengths are partially offset by exposure to cyclicality in the end-user industry, susceptibility of margins to raw material/forex volatility, and supplier concentration risk.

***

## Key Rating Drivers — Strengths

*   **Established Market Position:** The group benefits from over four decades of business experience, understanding of bitumen market dynamics, and established relationships with suppliers and customers. Strong facilities locations have helped reduce transportation costs.
*   **Scale and Growth:** A combination of steady demand and strong realizations led to a CAGR of 15% for revenues for the past three years ending FY 2025. Revenues grew from Rs. 904 crores (FY 2021) to Rs. 2,401 crores (FY 2025), though current fiscal revenues moderated to Rs. 1,247 crores (9M FY 2026).
*   **Diversified Revenue Profile:** AICL is not limited to bitumen trading/manufacturing but also provides integrated transportation solutions (60-70% bitumen transported via own vessels, offsetting freight expenses), LPG cylinder transport, windmill power generation, and ship chartering.
*   **Comfortable Financial Risk Profile:**
    *   Networth strengthening via continuous accretion to reserves, expected at Rs. 660-680 crores as on March 31, 2026.
    *   Gearing ratio expected to remain healthy at 0.5-0.65 times (Mar 31, 2026) from 0.69 times (Mar 31, 2025).
    *   Interest coverage ratios expected at 4.4-4.6 times for Fiscal 2026.
*   **Prudent Risk Management:**
    *   Low inventory holding with average inventory of 23-33 days over the past 3 years ending FY 2025.
    *   Mitigated counterparty risk via large, diversified clientele with long relationships (3-4 decades).
    *   Ability to pass on foreign exchange exposure to customers.

***

## Key Rating Drivers — Weaknesses

*   **Margin Volatility:** The operating margin is susceptible to raw material price volatility (bitumen is a crude oil derivative).
    *   Operating margins have ranged from 6.3% to 8.6% over the past six fiscals through FY 2025.
    *   Current fiscal margins are expected to moderate to around 6-7% due to high crude price volatility and geopolitical situations.
*   **Cyclicality in End Markets:** The entity caters to infrastructure, industrial gases, and power industries, which are cyclical.
    *   Economic slowdowns lead to stretched debtors from small clients. Sizeable debtors > 6 months were noted at Rs. 68 crores (Mar 31, 2025) and Rs. 56.94 crores (Sep 30, 2025).
    *   Early/extended monsoons have currently slowed construction activity, leading to softer demand.
*   **Supplier Concentration Risk:** The company procures the majority of its bitumen from the Middle East, with 80-85% of sourcing from top 3 suppliers. This limits price negotiation capability.

***

## Liquidity

*   **Cash Accruals:** Annual cash accrual expected to be Rs. 100-150 crore against yearly term debt obligations of Rs. 65-79 crore over the medium term.
*   **Bank Limit Utilisation:** Moderate, at around 81% average for the last 6 months through December 2025.
*   **Current Ratio:** Moderate at 1.14 times as on March 31, 2025.
*   **Cash & Equivalents:** High liquidity position with Cash and Cash Equivalents of Rs. 44.5 crores and Liquid Mutual Funds of Rs. 37.52 crores as on September 30, 2025.
*   **Assessment:** Internal accruals, cash & cash equivalents, and unutilized bank lines are expected to be sufficient to meet repayment obligations and incremental working capital requirements over the medium term.
