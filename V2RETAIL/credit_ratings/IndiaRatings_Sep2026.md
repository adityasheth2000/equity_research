# India Ratings Upgrades V2 Retail's Bank Loan Facilities to 'IND A'/Positive/'IND A1'
**Date:** Sep 01, 2026 | V2 Retail Limited | Diversified Retail

## Rating Action
- Bank loan facilities (INR 1,250 Mn): Upgraded to IND A/Positive/IND A1 from IND A-/Stable/IND A2+
- Bank loan facilities (INR 500 Mn): Assigned IND A/Positive/IND A1
- Total rated limits: INR 1,750 Mn

## Key Highlights & Rationale
- Scale growth of over 60% YoY, driven by sustained store network expansion, strong SSSG, efficient inventory management.
- EBITDA rose to over INR 4,544 Mn in FY26.
- Established position in India's value retail segment, geographical diversification in tier-2 and smaller cities.
- Asset-light expansion model: closed in-house manufacturing unit in 2025 and transitioned to asset-light retailing. Manufacturing outsourced to third-party vendors, V2 controls design and product development.
- SSSG moderated to 10% in FY26 on high base (FY25: 30%, FY24: 31%).
- Sales per sq ft per month declined to INR 931 in FY26 (FY25: INR 1,020) due to 136 new stores added (+70% network increase).
- Consolidated ASP increased to INR 326 in FY26 (FY25: INR 295), Average Bill Value to INR 922 (FY25: INR 854).
- Revenue contribution from own brands rose to 90% in FY26 (FY25: 80%, FY24: 60%).
- Network: 325 stores across 225 cities and 25 states (3.5 mn sq ft) as of March 31, 2026. Crossed 400 stores in August 2026 (381 stores / 4.07 mn sq ft as of June 30, 2026).
- FY27 target: Add 170 net stores in FY27, ~200 stores in FY28. Added 56 net stores in 1QFY27.
- FY26 Consolidated Revenue: INR 30,670 Mn (+63% YoY from INR 18,845 Mn in FY25).
- FY26 EBITDA Margin: 14.85% (FY25: 13.7%).
- FY26 Interest coverage: 4.71x (FY25: 3.8x).
- External debt: INR 2,294 Mn in FY26 (FY25: INR 1,264 Mn) due to higher working capital limits.
- Net leverage (incl lease liabilities/EBITDA): 2.17x in FY26 (FY25: 3.3x). Net leverage excl leases: 0.5x.
- Major debt comprises lease liabilities (74% of total debt as of Mar 2026 vs 87% in Mar 2025).

## Key Risks & Rating Sensitivities
- Inventory days (on COGS) surged to 194 in FY26 (FY25: 154) due to higher stocking amid West Asia supply chain uncertainties.
- Working capital cycle elongated to 130 days in FY26 (FY25: 80 days).
- Aggressive store expansion: adding 170 stores in FY27 and 200 in FY28 can cause slower ramp-up, subdue profitability, and stretch free cash flow. Ind-Ra expects CFO to turn negative over medium term due to working capital requirements and capex.
- High competition from value apparel retailers (Zudio, Style Baazar, City Kart, Vishal Mega Mart) and e-commerce.
- Discretionary spending risk in tier-2/3 cities.
