**Key Equity Research Takeaways:**
*   **Strategic Pivot:** The company is executing a clear strategy to shift revenue mix from raw material sales (Iron Ore) to Value Added Products (VAP).
*   **Rapid Growth in VAP:** VAP revenue share doubled from Q2FY25 (26%) to Q2FY26 (47%). Notably, there was a sharp acceleration in Q2FY26 compared to Q1FY26 (13% -> 47%).
*   **Margin Accretion:** The EBIT contribution from VAP is growing faster than its revenue contribution, particularly evident in Q2FY26 where revenue split (47%) roughly equals EBIT split (47%). This indicates that Value Added Products carry significantly higher operating margins compared to raw Iron Ore. For instance, in Q1FY26, VAP accounted for 13% of revenue but only 3% of EBIT; by Q2FY26, it contributed 47% of revenue and 47% of EBIT.
