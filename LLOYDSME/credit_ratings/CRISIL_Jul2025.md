## Detailed Rationale

Crisil Ratings has assigned its 'Crisil AA/Stable' rating to the bank facilities and non-convertible debentures (NCDs) of Lloyds Metals and Energy Ltd (LMEL). The rating reflects LMEL’s strong and improving business risk profile, supported by its leading position in the iron ore mining industry and significant cost advantages derived from its allocation-based mine model, which results in lower royalty payments compared to auctioned mines. The company has also achieved forward integration into downstream products.

The rating factors in a strong financial risk profile, supported by a prudent capital structure, healthy cash accrual, and limited reliance on debt. However, these strengths are partially offset by the concentration of mining operations in a single region, project risks associated with large capital expenditure (capex), and the inherent risks associated with the cyclical nature of the steel industry.

Significant operational transformation occurred following the onboarding of Thriveni Earthmovers Pvt Ltd (TEMPL) as a mine developer and operator (MDO) in fiscal 2022. The Surjagarh iron ore mine ramped up to full capacity of 10 MTPA in fiscal 2025. Following this, the company received environmental clearance for expanding mining capacity to 55 MTPA in June 2025. The company is also transitioning into an integrated steel player with an estimated capex rollout of Rs 32,700 crore through fiscal 2029.

---

## Key Rating Drivers — Strengths

*   **Largest Private Iron Ore Mine:** LMEL operates the largest single-location private iron ore mine in India. Environmental clearance permits a capacity of 55 MTPA (including BHQ), making it India’s largest iron ore mine by permitted capacity.
*   **Cost Advantages (Allocation-Based Mine):** Surjagarh is an allocated mine, exempting LMEL from paying revenue share or auction premiums. Royalty costs are approximately **20% of Average Selling Price (ASP)**, compared to ~40% for PSUs and 150%+ for auctioned mines.
*   **First-Mover in BHQ Beneficiation:** The company is pioneering large-scale beneficiation of Banded Hematite Quartzite (BHQ). It holds 706 MT of BHQ reserves and is setting up 45 MTPA of beneficiation capacity via a JV with Sinosteel, ensuring feedstock continuity.
*   **Strategic Acquisition of MDO Business:** LMEL acquired ~80% stake in Thriveni Earthmovers Infra Pvt Ltd (TEIPL) from TEMPL. This vertical integration is expected to augment revenue by ~Rs 4,000 crore annually and reduce iron ore mining costs by ₹400-500/tonne.
*   **Strong Financial Risk Profile:** The company maintains a conservative capital structure. As of March 31, 2025, consolidated networth was Rs 6,402 crore, total debt was Rs 1,004 crore, gearing was 0.16 times, and gross debt-to-Ebitda was 0.51 times.

---

## Key Rating Drivers — Weaknesses

*   **Geographic Concentration:** Mining and plant operations are concentrated in Gadchiroli and Chandrapur districts of Maharashtra, which have witnessed sociopolitical unrest. Disruptions here could impact operations.
*   **High Capex Intensity:** The company is undertaking a large capex plan of Rs 32,700 crore till fiscal 2029. Execution delays, cost overruns, or adverse commodity cycles pose risks to returns and cash flow.
*   **Susceptibility to Steel Price Cycles:** Transitioning into an integrated steel player increases exposure to cyclicality in demand and prices for end-user segments like real estate and construction.
*   **Regulatory and Legal Risks:** Changes in iron ore royalty structure or adverse outcomes in environmental, land, or promoter-related litigations could impact the business risk profile.

## Liquidity

*   **Assessment:** Strong.
*   **Cash Accruals:** Expected to generate net cash accrual of **Rs 9,000–10,000 crore cumulatively** over the next two fiscals.
*   **Equity Inflow:** Rs 1,770 crore expected from balance equity warrant money in Q4 of fiscal 2026.
*   **Cash Balances:** Exceeds **Rs 50 crore** as of May 2025.
*   **Bank Limit Utilisation:** Unutilised fund-based working capital lines of **Rs 400 crore** available.
*   **Repayment Obligations:** Company faces repayment obligations of Rs 4,000 crore over the next two fiscals (including debt at TEIPL).
*   **Capex Funding:** LMEL follows a philosophy of adjusting capex pace to cash accrual; ~60-70% of internal cash accrual is earmarked for capex to limit debt requirements.

## Outlook & Rating Sensitivity

*   **Outlook:** **Stable**
*   **Rationale:** Risk profile supported by successful ramp-up of mining operations (55 MTPA clearance), cost-efficient backward-integrated business model, and strong cash accrual.

| Factors | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Upward Factors** | • Considerable ramp-up in revenue (cAGR > 20%)<br>• Sustained operating margins > 35%<br>• Maintenance of healthy financial risk metrics alongside growth |
| **Downward Factors** | • Sluggish revenue growth and operating margins dropping below 20%<br>• Material deviations in capex funding discipline leading to **Net Debt/EBITDA > 1.0–1.2 times**<br>• Adverse regulatory events or legal challenges |
