## Detailed Rationale

Crisil Ratings has reaffirmed its 'Crisil AA/Stable' rating on the long-term bank facilities and non-convertible debentures (NCDs) of Lloyds Metals and Energy Ltd (LMEL), and assigned a 'Crisil A1+' rating to its short-term bank facilities. The ratings reflect LMEL’s strong and improving business risk profile, anchored by its leading position in the iron ore mining industry and significant cost advantages derived from its allocation-based mine, which results in lower royalty payments.

Financial risk profile remains healthy, aided by a prudent capital structure and healthy cash accruals. However, these strengths are partially offset by the concentration of mining operations in a single region (Maharashtra), exposure to high capital expenditure (capex) project risks, and susceptibility to cyclicality inherent in the steel industry.

For the first nine months of fiscal 2026, LMEL reported an on-year revenue expansion of 101% to Rs 11,093 crore, driven by volume ramp-up and improved evacuation via the slurry pipeline. Consolidated EBITDA margin improved by ~180 basis points to 32.4%. Over the medium term, the company is expected to witness strong revenue growth supported by monetization of its Rs 42,000 crore capex program (FY2024–2030) aimed at building an integrated steel plant.

## Key Rating Drivers — Strengths

*   **Largest Private Iron Ore Mine with Rapid Ramp-up:**
    *   Surjagarh mine in Maharashtra achieved rated capacity of 10 MTPA by FY25 following the appointment of Thriveni Earthmovers and Infrastructure Pvt Ltd (TEMPL) as the Mine Developer and Operator (MDO).
    *   Received environmental clearance in June 2025 to expand capacity to **55 MTPA** (including 45 MTPA Banded Hematite Quartzite), making it the largest single-location iron ore mine in India by permitted capacity.
    *   Reserves of **863 MT** (JORC-compliant), comprising 157 MT of Direct Shipping Ore (DSO) and 706 MT of Banded Hematite Quartzite (BHQ).
    *   **Cost Advantage:** As an allocation-based mine, royalty outgo is ~20% of the average selling price (ASP), significantly lower than ~40% for public sector mines and >150% for auctioned captive mines. Operational control over MDO operations is projected to lower consolidated mining costs by Rs 400–500 per tonne.

*   **First-mover in BHQ Beneficiation:**
    *   Pioneering large-scale beneficiation of BHQ (historically underutilised).
    *   Establishing 45 MTPA of beneficiation capacity via a strategic JV with Sinosteel Equipment and Engineering Co Ltd.
    *   Pilot outcomes show iron grade >66% with 38-40% yield, ensuring feedstock continuity for future steelmaking.

*   **Strategic Acquisition of Thriveni’s MDO Business (TEIPL):**
    *   Completed acquisition of ~80% stake in Thriveni Earthmovers and Infra Pvt Ltd (TEIPL) in July 2025.
    *   Augments LMEL’s revenue by **Rs 6,000–7,000 crore** (annualised basis).
    *   Provides operational control, margin uplift, and cost efficiency. Facilitates backward integration and mitigates related-party concerns.

*   **Healthy, Moderately Robust Financial Risk Profile:**
    *   Consistent policy to earmark **60-70% of internal cash accruals** for capex.
    *   Networth of ~Rs 7,000 crore as of September 30, 2025.
    *   Equity funding raised: Rs 1,218 crore (QIP) and Rs 2,723 crore (Preferential issue).

## Key Rating Drivers — Weaknesses

*   **Susceptibility to Geographic Concentration, Regulatory, and Legal Risks:**
    *   Operations concentrated in **Gadchiroli and Chandrapur districts** of Maharashtra, regions historically affected by sociopolitical unrest.
    *   **Leverage Increases:** Following consolidation of debt at the MDO level (TEIPL), gearing stood at 1.17 times and net debt to Ebitda at ~2.0 times as of September 30, 2025.
    *   **Litigation:** LMEL and promoters are party to various litigations (environmental, land, etc.). No material financial liability is expected, but regulatory compliance remains monitorable.
    *   **Promoter Background:** Dual-promoter structure involving B Prabhakaran (Thriveni) and Gupta family; group under Gupta family has experienced financial distress in the past (though no outstanding liabilities currently).

*   **High Capex Intensity and Execution Risk:**
    *   Capex of **Rs 42,000 crore** planned till fiscal 2030 (~Rs 9,600 crore completed till December 2025).
    *   Investments include international assets (copper and cobalt in Africa via Nexus Holdco FZCO and CHEMAF Group) and expansion in Singapore, South Africa, and Bougainville.
    *   Risks of capex overrun, delays in commissioning, or sluggish operational ramp-up, specifically for international facilities.

*   **Susceptibility to Steel Price Cycles:**
    *   Transition into an integrated steel producer increases exposure to cyclicality in the steel sector.
    *   Demand is linked to domestic/global economies and end-user segments (real estate, civil construction, engineering).

## Liquidity

*   **Liquidity Assessment:** **Strong**
*   **Cash Accruals:** LMEL is likely to generate annual net cash accruals of **Rs 7,000–8,000 crore** in fiscals 2027 and 2028.
*   **Debt Obligations:** Debt repayment obligation is projected at **Rs 2,000–3,000 crore** annually (including TEIPL debt).
*   **Capital Expenditure:** Plan for capex of Rs 7,500–8,500 crore annually for downstream projects and global expansion.
*   **Cash Reserves & Limits:** Liquidity is supported by cash and bank balances of ~Rs 1,200 crore and unutilised fund-based working capital and overdraft lines as of December 2025.

## Outlook & Rating Sensitivity

### Outlook: **Stable**
Credit risk profile is expected to remain stable over the medium term, supported by successful ramp-up of mining operations (clearance up to 55 MTPA), cost-efficient backward-integrated business model, and strong cash accrual.

### Upward Factors
*   Considerable ramp-up in revenue with a compound annual growth rate (CAGR) of more than 20% over the medium term.
*   Diversification of revenue profile with successful project ramp-up while benefitting from strong operating margins (sustained >35%).
*   Sustenance of healthy financial risk profile and debt protection metrics during growth opportunities.

### Downward Factors
*   Sluggish revenue growth and operating margins declining to below 20% due to slower-than-expected project ramp-up.
*   Change in capex funding philosophy or moderating debt protection metrics (Net debt/Adjusted Ebitda exceeding 1.0–1.2 times on a sustained basis).
*   Adverse regulatory events or legal challenges impacting business or financial risk profiles.

## Company & Subsidiaries

### Company Information
*   **Incorporation:** 1977
*   **Base:** Maharashtra (India)
*   **Status:** Integrated metal and mining company. India's largest private merchant miner of iron ore by current production run rate.
*   **Key Operations:** Surjagarh iron ore mine (Permitted capacity 55 MTPA), DRI plants (340,000 TPA), 34-MW captive power, 85-km slurry pipeline (Hedri to Konsari), 4-MTPA pellet plant at Konsari.
*   **Reserves:** >863 MT of JORC-compliant iron ore reserves.
*   **Financial Performance (9M FY26):** Operating Income Rs 11,093 Cr; PAT Rs 2,299 Cr.
*   **Acquisition:** Completed acquisition of 79.82% stake in TEIPL (Mine Developer and Operator business of TEMPL) in July 2025.
