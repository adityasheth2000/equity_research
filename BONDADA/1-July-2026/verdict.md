## Company Overview

Incorporated in 2012, Bondada Engineering is an EPC and O&M company serving telecom and solar energy sectors. Listed on BSE SME (543971), migrating to main board in FY27. Promoter holding: 61.6% (zero pledged). 9 subsidiaries covering solar EPC, BESS, telecom, railways, defence, and products.

## Financial Health

**Strengths:**
- **Zero long-term debt**, net cash surplus ~₹100 Cr
- **OCF turned positive** in FY26 (+₹125 Cr) after FY25's -₹158 Cr
- **Debtor days improved** from 125 → 87 days over 12 months
- **ROE: 35.7%**, ROCE: 38.7% — exceptional for EPC
- **CRISIL "A Stable"** rating (upgraded from A-)
- **No promoter pledges**

**Concerns:**
- **Q4 FY26 margin dip**: OPM fell to 10.5% (steel/cable prices up 17-18%, some low-margin billing). Management calls it a one-off
- **Negative EPS growth 5Yr** (-2.16%) due to IPO share dilution, though trailing EPS has recovered to ₹18.23
- **Working capital still heavy**: Receivables ₹792 Cr (28% of revenue), inventory ballooned to ₹536 Cr
- **China dependence for BESS cells** (50% of BESS cost, no domestic alternative)

## Key Risks

1. **Growth deceleration**: 96% → 81% → guided 60-70%. At ₹4,500+ Cr base, sustaining >50% growth gets harder
2. **Solar EPC commoditization**: 7.5% PAT margins — thin profit pool, raw material sensitivity shown in Q4 FY26
3. **Execution risk**: 7.8 GW portfolio to commission in 2-2.5 years; BESS is a new segment with zero track record
4. **Client concentration**: PSU-heavy revenue (NTPC, NLC, MAHAGENCO) — policy/approval risks
5. **BESS competition**: Reverse auctions driving prices below viability; Bondada has stayed away from some tenders
6. **Dilution risk**: IPP/BESS equity needs (~₹250 Cr for first phase), potential QIP/preferential issue
7. **SME to Main Board**: Migration may bring valuation discovery and liquidity adjustments

---

## Bull Thesis

- **~₹19,000 Cr total revenue visibility** (order book + L1 + IPP) — ~6.7x FY26 revenue
- **Improving fundamentals**: OCF positive, debtor days falling, zero long-term debt
- **Multiple growth engines**: Solar EPC (core), BESS/Data Centres/Defence/Railways (emerging), Products (steady)
- **At 10-11x forward P/E**, attractive entry at 35% discount to 52-week high
- Management has delivered 14 years without default; CEO (Retd. Rear Admiral) brings defence/PSU credibility

## Bear Thesis

- Revenue growth rate is the key valuation driver — decelerating to 60-70% could compress multiples further
- Solar EPC alone doesn't justify 36% ROE long-term — current high returns are leverage of a small equity base post-IPO
- BESS and Defence are unproven — execution risk in new segments
- Margin profile (7.4% PAT) leaves little room for errors in a commodity cost cycle
- SME liquidity and governance perception discount vs main board peers
