# Bondada Engineering Ltd — Verdict

**Date:** 12 July 2026 | **CMP:** Rs 305 | **Mkt Cap:** Rs 3,407 Cr | **P/E:** 16.7x | **52W:** Rs 503 / 215

## 1. Company Overview

Bondada Engineering Ltd (BSE SME: 543971) is a Hyderabad-based EPC and O&M services provider incorporated in 2012. The company operates four core verticals — **Renewable Energy (79% of FY26 revenue), Telecom (14%), Railways, and BESS** — with emerging forays into Data Centres and Defence. It deploys an asset-light EPC model supplemented by selective IPP/BOO (Build-Own-Operate) projects for annuity revenue.

**Business Model Fragmentation:**

| Model | Nature | Segments |
|---|---|---|
| **EPC & Projects** | Lump-sum/turnkey, 15-24 month cycle | Solar, Telecom Towers, Railways (Kavach) |
| **O&M** | Recurring annuity (3-5 year contracts) | Solar plants, Telecom networks/towers, Data Centres |
| **IPP / BOO** | Capital-intensive, 12-25 year PPA | Solar IPP (2 GW AP), BESS BOO (850 MWh) |
| **Manufacturing** | Captive + 3rd party supply | Towers, Solar MMS, AAC Blocks, uPVC, LED, BLDC Motors |

**8 Subsidiaries** cover the value chain: Bondada Renewable Energy (Solar/Wind EPC), Bondada Green Engineering (Towers/MMS/Cable Trays), GreenBond RE Park (IPP/BESS), Bondada Managed Services (O&M), Bondada EcoBuild (AAC/uPVC), Bondada E&E (LED), Atpole Technologies (BLDC Motors), Bondada Dynamics (Defence).

## 4. Segment Analysis

### Renewable Energy (79% of FY26 Revenue)

- **Solar EPC**: 1.3 GW commissioned, ~6.6 GW under execution, L1 for ~2.5 GW. Total pipeline ~10.4 GW.
- **Solar IPP (AP 2 GW)**: GO from AP government. 9,000 acres land mapped. 250 MW Phase 1 starting next FY. Full deployment over 4-5 years. Revenue: Rs 9,000 Cr EPC over 5 years, then Rs 1,000 Cr/yr annuity for 25 years.
- **Margins**: PAT ~7.5% on EPC, ~10% on O&M. EBITDA margins 11-12% on EPC (per screener data).
- **Clients**: Adani, NLC, NTPC, MAHAGENCO, TNGECL. Minimal state government exposure ("very least dependent on state governments" — CMD).

### BESS (Emerging Star)

- **Under Construction**: 850 MWh (TNGECL 400 MWh + APTRANSCO 450 MWh), BOO model, 18-month execution.
- **TNGECL**: Financial closure via Canara Bank Rs 507 Cr (Rs 399 Cr TL + Rs 108 Cr BG). COD: 31 Dec 2026.
- **APTRANSCO**: Blueprint ready.
- **BOO IRRs**: 16-17% currently, expected to moderate to 13-14%.
- **EPC EBITDA margins**: 12-13%.
- **Market Opportunity**: India BESS target 130-132 GWh by 2032. Current deployment <1 GWh. TAM: Rs 2.5-3 lakh Cr over 6-7 years. BESS capacity requirement CAGR: 46-47% to FY32.
- **Management expects 100%+ CAGR in BESS order book and execution over next 5 years.**
- **Risk**: 100% cell import dependency from China (50% of battery cost). Technology evolving rapidly. Company exploring backward integration into assembly.

### Telecom (14% of Revenue)

- **EPC**: Tower erection, OFC laying. BSNL 4G saturation project (1,536 towers installed, 1,506 on-air — largest telecom contract at Rs 1,156 Cr).
- **O&M**: Jio Telangana Circle — managing 35,000 route km fibre + 35,000 towers. Airtel Southern Circles added. 5-year contracts.
- **Track record**: 13,000+ towers deployed pan-India over 14 years.
- **O&M Book**: Rs 500-600 Cr in telecom O&M over next 5 years.

### Railways & Others

- **Railways**: Rs 193 Cr OB. Kavach passive infra for 452 sites across 1,500 KMs for South Central Railway. Also pursuing signalling, crash barriers.
- **Products (7% of revenue)**: Manufacturing across subsidiaries. PAT ~10%. Green Building Materials, LED, BLDC Motors. Backward integrated uPVC extrusion (powder-to-profile, European standards).
- **Data Centres**: Currently O&M for Microsoft Hyderabad facility. MoU with Bryanston Inc. for connection-ready DC parcels in Hyderabad/Vizag. Strategy: land + ecosystem first, own only after anchor customer. Revenue recognition from Q3 FY27.
- **Defence**: First order from BEL for critical components (Rs 4 Cr). 3 M&A targets under due diligence (IP-centric acquisitions). CapEx: Rs 75-100 Cr via internal accruals. Revenue recognition from Q3 FY27.

Management has been explicit and disciplined:
- Only 4 GW of 25 GW target is asset-heavy (2 GW IPP + 2 GW BESS BOO). Rest is EPC.

## 8. Risk Assessment

### High Severity
1. **Working Capital Intensity**: Debtor days at 102, inventory days at 134, CCC at 138 days. Rs 1,328 Cr locked in receivables + inventory (65% of assets). FY25's -Rs 158 Cr CFO shows how quickly cash can swing negative if collections slip. FY26 improvement to +Rs 125 Cr must sustain.

2. **Execution at Scale**: Revenue at Rs 2,843 Cr, order book Rs 7,147 Cr. Managing 6.6 GW solar under execution across 8+ states simultaneously, plus telecom, BESS, and railways — tests organisational bandwidth. Management acknowledged "always HR and human resources and then labour is always an issue."

3. **BESS Technology & Supply Chain Risk**: 100% dependency on Chinese battery cells. Lithium-ion technology evolving rapidly; newer chemistries (sodium-ion, solid-state) could disrupt. BOO model locks in technology for 12-14 years.

### Medium Severity
4. **Client Concentration**: Top 3 states (Maharashtra, Gujarat, Tamil Nadu) = 58% of OB. RE = 79% of revenue. Adani/NTPC/NLC concentration in RE. A disruption in solar tendering or state-level policy change would be material.

5. **Order Book-to-Revenue Conversion Gap**: OB of Rs 7,147 Cr at 2.5x revenue with 18-24 month execution implies ~Rs 3,500-4,800 Cr annual revenue run-rate. FY26 at Rs 2,843 Cr lagged the implied OB burn rate — some projects face delays.

6. **SME Exchange Constraints**: No institutional participation, limited analyst coverage, lower liquidity. Mainboard migration is planned but timeline uncertain.

7. **Promoter Dilution Overhang**: Steady 63.33% → 61.57% trend. ESOP vesting (25% remaining from Apr 2026). Management open to further equity dilution for IPP/defence.

### Low Severity
8. **Increasing Competitive Intensity**: Solar EPC is a crowded space. Margins (11.5% EBITDA) are healthy but larger players (L&T, Tata Power Solar, Sterling & Wilson) have deeper balance sheets. BESS BOO IRRs expected to compress from 16-17% to 13-14%.

9. **Accounting Regime Change**: Ind AS adoption from FY25 creates some comparability friction with historical data.

## 10. Verdict

Bondada Engineering is an exceptional EPC growth story riding India's energy transition capex. The business has demonstrated capacity to scale from Rs 224 Cr (FY19) to Rs 2,843 Cr (FY26) while expanding margins from 9% to 11.5% EBITDA. The Rs 7,147 Cr order book, diversified across solar, BESS, telecom, and railways, provides 2.5x revenue visibility.

At 16.7x P/E with 38.7% ROCE, the valuation is undemanding relative to both the growth profile and peer set. The stock's -39% correction from highs partially prices in working capital and execution concerns.

**The differentiator** is BESS — Bondada has secured 850 MWh of BOO projects when India's total installed BESS capacity is <1 GWh. If the company executes on even a fraction of the 132 GWh national target, the annuity revenue stream could transform the business profile from pure EPC to a hybrid EPC + utility model.

**Watchpoints for conviction:**
1. Sustained positive CFO beyond FY26 (one quarter doesn't make a trend)
2. SCC (CCC) trending back toward 90 days (management's stated target)
3. BESS COD milestones (TNGECL by Dec 2026, APTRANSCO by mid-2027)
4. Mainboard migration announcement
5. Adani follow-on orders (3-4 GW pipeline)

**Bottom line:** A high-quality EPC compounder temporarily undervalued due to SME listing discount, working capital optics, and post-hype correction. The risk-reward is favourable for investors comfortable with EPC-sector working capital dynamics.
