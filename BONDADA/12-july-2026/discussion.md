## 1. What the Company Does (Simple Words)

Bondada is a **construction company for infrastructure projects** — not buildings or roads, but solar farms, telecom towers, and battery storage systems.

### Core Businesses

**Solar Power Plants (~79% of revenue)**
They build large solar farms for customers like Adani, NTPC, and state governments. Full scope: land preparation, steel structures, wiring, grid connection. Post-commissioning, they maintain the plants (O&M) for 3-5 years.

**Telecom Towers (~14% of revenue)**
They erect telecom towers, lay fibre optic cables underground, and maintain entire networks. They manage Jio's entire Telangana circle — 35,000 towers and 35,000 km of fibre. Track record: 13,000+ towers deployed over 14 years.

**Railways, BESS & Manufacturing (remaining 7%)**
- **Railways**: Building ground infrastructure for Kavach (India's automatic train protection system) — erecting towers along railway tracks for safety signalling.
- **BESS (Battery Energy Storage)**: Building large battery installations that store solar/wind power for use when the sun isn't shining. Newest, fastest-growing business.
- **Manufacturing**: In-house production of steel structures, AAC blocks, uPVC windows, LED lights, BLDC motors — mostly captive, increasingly sold externally.

### How They Expand Into New Businesses

The pattern is skill reuse from one vertical to the next:

```
Telecom (2012) → Solar EPC (2017) → Railways (2024) → BESS (2025) → Data Centres / Defence (2026+)
```

- **Telecom → Solar**: Same skills — erecting structures, managing field teams, dealing with government/PSU clients. Different structures, same execution playbook.
- **Telecom → Railways**: Railway Kavach needs telecom towers along tracks. Bondada had the credentials from telecom, so they qualified.
- **Solar → BESS**: Same clients (DISCOMs, state power companies), same tender process. Already knew the buyers who now need batteries.
- **O&M → Data Centres**: Already maintain Microsoft's Hyderabad data centre (HVAC, power, security) — same skill set as telecom tower O&M.
- **Manufacturing → Defence**: BLDC motor and electronics subsidiary (Atpole) provides entry point. Hired retired Navy Admiral as Group CEO for defence sector relationships.

## 2. Current Capacity & Utilization

For an EPC company, "capacity" isn't a factory output number — it's project management bandwidth, labour availability, and working capital.

| Business | Current Capacity | Utilization in FY26 |
|---|---|---|
| **Solar EPC** (commissioning rate) | ~1 GW/year executable | 492 MW commissioned (~50%) |
| **Solar EPC** (under execution) | ~6.6 GW work-in-progress | Projects take 15-24 months; not equipment-constrained |
| **Telecom Towers** (BSNL) | 1,536 towers installed, 1,506 on-air | BSNL order ~Rs 1,156 Cr, largely executed |
| **BESS** (under execution) | 850 MWh (2 projects: TNGECL + APTRANSCO) | 100% of current capacity booked |
| **Manufacturing** | Multiple plants in Hyderabad/Vizag; Rs 169 Cr order book | 7% of revenue; ~80-90% utilization |
| **O&M** (Solar + Telecom) | Rs 400-450 Cr solar + Rs 500-600 Cr telecom contracts | Steady annuity; headcount-driven, not "capacity" per se |

### Difference Between "Commissioned" vs "Under Execution"

**"Under Execution" (6.6 GW)** = ALL projects currently being built — the backlog/work-in-progress. Won contracts, construction is ongoing. These are multi-year projects (15-24 months each).

**"Commissioned" (492 MW)** = How much of that backlog was FINISHED and handed over in FY26. The customer's solar farm is live, Bondada booked the revenue, retention money gets released.

**Why only 492 MW out of 6.6 GW?**
1. Most of the 6.6 GW was won DURING FY26 — projects started mid-year, still in early stages
2. EPC execution is back-loaded — heavy construction happens in the last 6-8 months
3. Monsoon seasonality — Q2 (Jul-Sep) rains slow solar construction
4. Partial billing may exist but hasn't reached "commissioned" status

### Putting It Together

```
New orders won in FY26:     ~4,000 MW
Work already in progress:   ~2,600 MW
─────────────────────────────────────
Total cooking (under exec): ~6,600 MW
Finished & delivered:         492 MW
Still cooking → FY27:       ~6,100 MW (carried forward + new FY27 wins)
```

The 6.6 GW means the kitchen is full and revenue is locked in. The 492 MW just tells you delivery pace. As projects mature, this pace should accelerate. Management targets 1.5-2 GW commissioning in FY27.
