## 2. Key Metric Deep Dives

### 2.1 Revenue: Rs 2,843 Cr vs Rs 3,040-3,142 Cr Guidance

| Guidance | Amount | Method | Actual | Shortfall |
|:--|:--|:--|:--|:--|
| Oct 2025 PPT | Rs 3,142 Cr | 2 × FY25 (Rs 1,571 Cr) | Rs 2,843 Cr | -Rs 299 Cr (-9.5%) |
| Nov 2025 concall | Rs 3,040 Cr | H1 Rs 1,216 ÷ 0.40 | Rs 2,843 Cr | -Rs 197 Cr (-6.5%) |

Even the softer concall guidance was missed. H2FY26 revenue = Rs 2,843 - Rs 1,216 = Rs 1,627 Cr, which is only 57.3% of full year, not the promised 60%.

**What went wrong:** Solar commissioning significantly undershot (492 MWp vs 1,000 MWp target), directly translating to lower EPC revenue recognition. Revenue mix remained concentrated — RE at 79%, telecom at 14%, products at 7%.

### 2.2 Order Book: Rs 7,147 Cr vs Rs 8,000-9,000 Cr

Order book actually *declined* from H1's run-rate-adjusted figure. The Oct PPT projected Rs 6,250-7,000 Cr inflows at 25% strike rate from Rs 25,000 Cr tenders. Management raised the target in Nov to Rs 8,500-9,000 Cr citing Rs 2,600 Cr L1 pipeline. 

**What went wrong:** The L1 pipeline of Rs 2,600 Cr didn't fully convert. New order wins in H2 slowed significantly. OB consumed by execution without compensatory inflows. Management cited AP 2 GW IPP (~Rs 9,000 Cr) as excluded — but even including that, the OB didn't reach guidance on core EPC orders.

### 2.3 Solar EPC Commissioning: 492 MWp vs 2,100/1,000 MWp

This is the single worst miss in the entire scorecard. Oct PPT projected 2.1 GW FY26 commissioning. Nov concall revised to "1 GW standalone in FY26." Actual: 492 MWp.

| Guidance Date | Target | Actual | Achievement |
|:--|:--|:--|:--|
| Oct 2025 | 2,100 MWp | 492 MWp | 23% |
| Nov 2025 (revised) | 1,000 MWp | 492 MWp | 49% |

Even after a 50%+ downward revision between Oct and Nov, the company delivered less than half. This raises serious questions about:
- Project execution bandwidth
- Land/site readiness assumptions
- Client-side delays not factored into guidance
- Whether the Oct 2025 guidance was ever realistic

### 2.4 Cash Conversion Cycle: 138 Days vs 90 Days Target

This is the most concerning metric in terms of management credibility:

| Metric | FY25 | H1FY26 (Reported) | FY26 (Actual) | Direction |
|:--|:--|:--|:--|:--|
| Cash Conversion Cycle | 110 days | 89 days | **138 days** | Worse |
| Debtor Days | 125 | 107 | 102 | Better |
| Inventory Days | 94 (FY25 avg?) | 52 | **134** | Much Worse |
| Creditor Days | 108 (FY25 avg?) | 70 | 97 | Worse |

Management stated on Nov 2025 concall: *"We expect to continue the same kind of working capital efficiency and effectiveness even in the second half year."* The reality was the opposite — CCC blew out by 49 days from H1 to full year. 

The H1FY26 improvement was partly seasonal (project completions trigger milestone payments, depleting debtors) and partly real. But the FY26 full-year CCC of 138 days was worse than FY25's 110 days — meaning **working capital efficiency actually deteriorated YoY, not improved**.

Inventory at Rs 536 Cr (134 days) is the smoking gun — solar EPC projects have large WIP that doesn't convert to billing until commissioning milestones. Lower commissioning translates directly to bloated inventory.

### 2.5 CFO: +Rs 125 Cr — Genuine Achievement

After FY25's -Rs 158 Cr drain, achieving positive CFO was a critical milestone and was delivered. However, CFO/Operating Profit at 45% means 55% of operating profit is still tied up in working capital.

Component analysis:
- PAT: Rs 211 Cr
- Non-cash (D&A): Rs 95 Cr
- Trade Receivables increase: Rs 257 Cr (absorbed cash)
- Inventories increase: Rs 335 Cr (absorbed cash)
- Payables increase: Rs 158 Cr (released cash)
- Net CFO: +Rs 125 Cr

The positive CFO was partly funded by stretching payables, not purely from operational efficiency.

## 3. Pattern Analysis

### 3.1 Consistent Over-Promise Areas

**Revenue and Growth Multiples:**
- Oct 2025 PPT: "2x FY25, 3x FY27" — bold, simple, memorable
- Nov 2025 concall: 40:60 split (implying lower) — still missed
- Pattern: Management uses ambitious growth multiples (2x, 3x, 7x) that capture investor imagination but don't survive contact with execution

**Solar Commissioning:**
- Oct PPT: 2.1 GW FY26 — off by 77%
- Pattern: Solar targets appear aspirational rather than operational. Only 492 MWp commissioned vs guidance of 1.0-2.1 GW. The company commissioned only 1.3 GW cumulative since inception, yet guided 2.1 GW for a single year.

**Order Book Visibility:**
- Management consistently projects OB based on pipeline × strike rate but ignores timing risk (L1 conversion delays, PO issuance delays). Rs 2,600 Cr L1 pipeline became the basis for raised guidance (Rs 9,000 Cr mentioned) but didn't convert.

### 3.2 Areas of Honesty/Delivery

**CFO:** Management was transparent about FY25's -Rs 158 Cr CFO ("we have operating cash flow negative of almost Rs 140 crore negative" — CFO on concall) and set a credible FY26 target (+ve by March) that was achieved.

**Working Capital Detail:** The CFO gave precise H1FY26 metrics (107 debtor days, 52 inventory days, 89 CCC) and didn't hide the H1 stats. The problem was the projection that this would sustain.

**EPS:** The "Rs 16 simple / Rs 20+ with 40:60" EPS guidance bracket was reasonable and actual Rs 18.23 landed comfortably between conservative and optimistic scenarios.

**Defence Entry:** Management was upfront that this was nascent (*"not much done work... maybe next one quarter or two quarters we'll come back"*), and delivered the first BEL order on time.

### 3.3 Did Management Soften Guidance Between Oct PPT and Nov Concall?

**Yes, but inconsistently:**

| Metric | Oct PPT | Nov Concall | Direction |
|:--|:--|:--|:--|
| Revenue | Rs 3,142 Cr | Rs 3,040 Cr (implied) | Slightly lower |
| Order Book | Rs 8,000 Cr | Rs 8,500-9,000 Cr | **Raised** |
| Solar Commissioning | 2.1 GW | 1 GW | **Halved** |
| Working Capital | Not given | 90 days CCC, sustain | Specific target given |
| BESS | 800 MWh | 850 Cr order book / CAGR 100% | Consistent |

The Oct PPT was prepared before H1FY26 results, so by the Nov concall management had actual H1 data. The halving of solar commissioning (2.1 GW → 1 GW) was an honest course correction, but even 1 GW proved unachievable.

The raising of OB guidance (Rs 8,000 → 8,500-9,000 Cr) on the back of L1 pipeline was premature and contributed to credibility damage when it wasn't met.

## 5. Red Flags in Guidance Communication

### 5.1 The "2x, 3x, 7x" Framing
Oct 2025 PPT prominently featured: "FY26: 2X, FY27: 3X, FY30: 7X over FY25." This linear extrapolation framework is simplistic for a project-based EPC business where revenue is lumpy and depends on order inflow timing, land readiness, grid connectivity, and client coordination — none of which are linear.

### 5.2 OB Guidance Raised THEN Missed
The Oct PPT said ~Rs 8,000 Cr. Two weeks later (Nov concall), the CFO said Rs 8,500-9,000 Cr is *"more visible and which is more likely to happen."* Actual: Rs 7,147 Cr. Raising mid-year guidance and then missing it is worse than missing an initial target.

### 5.3 CCC "Expect to Sustain" Statement
CFO on Nov concall: *"We expect to continue the same kind of working capital efficiency and effectiveness even in the second half year."* Full year CCC bloomed from 89 to 138 days. Either management doesn't understand their own working capital dynamics, or they were painting an optimistic picture for investors. Either is a concern.

### 5.4 CMD's Risk Assessment — Overconfidence
CMD on Nov concall when asked about execution risks: *"I have not seen... I have not stuck because of this reason, because of this geopolitical reason or because of some other external reason."* This suggested execution infallibility. FY26 results proved otherwise — solar commissioning missed by 51-77%, OB missed by 11-21%, CCC worsened. A humble "we're working on it" would have aged better.

### 5.5 Inventory Explosion Unexplained
Inventory went from Rs 201 Cr / 94 days (FY25) to Rs 536 Cr / 134 days (FY26). That's Rs 335 Cr of cash absorbed. The May 2026 PPT did not feature a dedicated working capital slide, and the Nov concall's assurance that efficiency would sustain was contradicted. Management has not publicly addressed the inventory balloon.

### 5.6 AP 2 GW IPP — A Recurring Footnote
The Rs 9,000 Cr AP IPP pipeline is repeatedly cited but excluded from reported OB. It's been "land pooling commenced" since Oct 2025. Revenue conversion remains distant. This is a legitimate long-term pipeline but its repeated mention risks becoming a deflection from core OB shortfalls.

| **OVERALL** | **BELOW PAR — Credibility damaged, not destroyed** |

### Narrative

Bondada's management is **ambitious and transparent in disclosure but unreliable on forward projections**. The pattern is consistent: set aggressive headline targets at PPT events (2x revenue, 2.1 GW solar, Rs 8,000 Cr OB), modestly revise down on concalls when confronted with actual data, and still miss even the revised numbers.

The single most damaging data point is **solar EPC commissioning: 492 MWp delivered vs 2,100 MWp guided**. This is not a marginal miss — it's a category error. Even if the 2.1 GW was aspirational, the revised 1 GW was missed by half.

The **working capital narrative** also strains credibility. Management claimed the H1 improvement to 89 days CCC was sustainable; full year landed at 138 days (worse than pre-improvement 110 days). The inventory build-up suggests significant execution bottlenecks that management either didn't see or didn't disclose.

**Mitigating factors:**
- CFO turnaround was genuine and delivered
- EPS guidance was reasonable
- BESS entry was credible and bank-financed
- Management remains accessible and answers direct questions
- The scale of growth (Rs 224 Cr → Rs 2,843 Cr in 8 years) commands some tolerance for lumpy execution

**Bottom line:** Management deserves credit for the business they've built but warrants a **guidance discount**. Investors should haircut forward guidance by 15-25% and build in 3-6 month execution lag on project timelines. The "2x, 3x, 7x" framing should be ignored in favour of analysing the actual bid pipeline, L1 conversion rates, and execution bandwidth.

**Trust formula going forward:** Believe what management has done (reported financials), verify what they say they will do (forward guidance). The Nov concall's "40:60 H1:H2" pattern is a better indicator of underlying revenue trajectory than the PPT's growth multiples.
