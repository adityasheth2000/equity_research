**Industry Growth Drivers**

*   **2025 Market Outlook:** Post-strike recovery in 2025 is expected to drive a surge of backlog-driven demand from major studios, creating a "catch-up wave" of VFX-intensive content.
*   **Budget Composition:** Hollywood productions are shifting towards high VFX dependency, with major studios allocating up to **40% of total budgets** to visual effects in tentpole films and episodic content.
