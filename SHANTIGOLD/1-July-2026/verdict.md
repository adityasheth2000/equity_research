## Business Summary

Shanti Gold is a B2B manufacturer of 22KT CZ studded cast gold jewellery, supplying to organized retailers like Kalyan, Joyalukkas, Lalithaa, and Alukkas. FY26 revenue was ₹2,019 Cr (+82.5% YoY), PAT ₹140 Cr (+159% YoY). But FY26 profit was inflated by ~₹40-50 Cr of inventory gains from IPO proceeds deployed at low gold prices during a rally. **Sustainable core PAT margin is ~4%, implying normalized TTM PAT of ~₹90-100 Cr and a real P/E closer to 16-18x.**

## Risks

1. **Gold price dependency** — 71% of PAT variance explained by gold. A sustained gold bear market (₹1.2L/10g) would slash FY29 PAT by 30-40%.
2. **Execution risk** — 3x capacity ramp across 3 facilities with new product lines (plain gold, Mangalsutra, Turkish jewellery). None have been proven at scale.
3. **Margin compression** — Product mix shifting from high-margin CZ bridal (~20% premium) to low-margin machine-made plain gold (~6% premium). Blended margins decline.
4. **Related-party overlap** — Promoters run Utsav Jewellery (separate listed entity). No dividend despite repeated profits. Screener flags potential interest capitalization.
5. **Working capital intensity** — Debtor days rose from 42 to 49. Current ratio comfortable at 3.3x, but tripling revenue will stretch balance sheet.

## Verdict

| Scenario | FY29 PAT | FY29 P/E | Probability |
|---|---|---|---|
| Bear (gold ₹1.2L, slow ramp) | ₹335 Cr | 4.8x | ~10% |
| Base (gold ₹1.7L, median ramp) | ₹454 Cr | 3.5x | ~50% |
| Bull (gold ₹2.3L, fast ramp) | ₹603 Cr | 2.7x | ~10% |

**At 3.5x FY29E P/E, the market is pricing in significant pessimism.** The capacity math alone (volume CAGR ~45% from 1,748 kg to ~5,700 kg over 3 years) justifies a much richer multiple IF execution delivers. The core debate is not valuation — it's whether management can simultaneously:

1. Ramp 5,200 kg of new capacity across 2 new factories
2. Onboard new customers for plain gold/Mangalsutra product lines
3. Transition to GML hedging without earnings volatility
4. Expand exports from 4% to 10-20% via Dubai subsidiary

**If delivery is on track by Q2 FY27 (Marol producing 100 kg/month, Jaipur operational), the risk/reward skews decisively positive.** Until then, the stock is a bet on execution in a gold-price-inflated earnings base.
