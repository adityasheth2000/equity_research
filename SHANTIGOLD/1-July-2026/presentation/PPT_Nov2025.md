### Slide Topic: Rigorous Quality Control & Process

**Operational Metrics & Standards:**
*   **Quality Team:** The company employs a dedicated Quality Control & Tagging team comprising **24 highly skilled employees**.
*   **Compliance & Branding:** **100% of gold jewelry products** are BIS (Bureau of Indian Standards) Hallmarked to affirm purity and authenticity.
*   **Product Mix:** Gold purity testing is conducted for **22K/18K gold**.

**Quality Control Workflow (Jewelry Quality Control Process):**

| Step | Process Stage | Key Activities & Standards |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **01** | **Raw Material Inspection** | • **Gold Purity Testing:** XRF or fire assay.<br>• **Gemstone Verification:** Authenticity checks using GIA/IGI certification. |
| **02** | **Production Inspection** | • **Design Review:** Review of 3D CAD Designs for accuracy.<br>• **Dimensional Checks:** Verification of dimensions and gemstone placement.<br>• **Defect Inspection:** Cast pieces inspected for porosity or incomplete filling.<br>• **Cost Control:** Gold weight verification to control material usage. |
| **03** | **Stone Setting & Polishing** | • **Inspection:** Use of 10X Loupe to inspect prongs & bezels.<br>• **Alignment:** Ensure uniform height of stones.<br>• **Finish:** Confirm polish (high-shine or matte) matches design. |
| **04** | **Hallmarking & Final QC** | • **Hallmark Check:** Verification of hallmark stamping.<br>• **SKU Matching:** Check product tag and SKU alignment.<br>• **Packing Verification:** Ensure correct invoice, certificate, and box packing. |
