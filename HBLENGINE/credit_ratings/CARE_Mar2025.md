**Page Title: Key weaknesses, Liquidity, and ESG Risks Assessment**

**Company:** HBL (Hindustan Batteries Limited)
**Rating Agency:** CARE Ratings Ltd.

### **1. Key Financial Metrics & Operational Data**

*   **Working Capital Cycle:** Improved to **114 days** in FY24 (from 152 days in FY23).
*   **Investment in Associate (Tonbo Imaging):**
    *   Total committed investment: ₹150 crore.
    *   Invested as of March 31, 2024: **₹86.67 crore**.
*   **Foreign Exchange Gain/Loss:**
    *   FY24 Net Gain: **₹6.32 crore**.
    *   FY23 Net Gain: **₹8.85 crore**.
*   **Raw Material Cost Contribution:** Major raw materials contribute **~50% to 60%** of Total Operating Income (TOI).
*   **Lead Content in Battery Costs:** Lead comprises **~60%** of battery costs.
*   **Liquidity Position (as of March 31, 2024):**
    *   Free Cash & Bank Balance: **₹234 crore**.
    *   Scheduled Repayment Obligation for FY25: **₹20.79 crore**.
    *   Cash Accruals FY24: **₹321.45 crore**.

### **2. Business Risk Profile Assessment**

*   **Working Capital Intensity:** The business is working capital intensive due to an elongated operating cycle. High inventory holding periods are necessitated by integrated operations and domestic procurement strategies.
*   **Raw Material Volatility:** Significant exposure to price volatility for **Lead Calcium** and **Lead Sub-oxide**. Since lead is ~60% of battery costs, market-driven price escalations pose a risk to profit margins.
*   **Environmental/Regulatory Risk:** Operations involve **lead-recycling**, subjecting the company to stringent pollution control regulations due to high toxicity and environmental impact.
*   **FX Exposure:** While exposed to FX fluctuations via imports and exports, the company acts as a **net exporter**, mitigating risk through natural hedging.

### **3. Financial Risk Profile Assessment**

*   **Liquidity:** Assessed as **Strong**. Supported by strong cash accruals and low reliance on bank borrowings for working capital (average utilization of fund-based limits negligible for the last 12 months ended August 2024).
*   **Debt Protection:** Adequate cash generated from operating activities has been used to manage working capital requirements, making dependence on bank borrowings negligible.
*   **Associated Entity Risk:** Increase in investment in group/associate entities and the ability to generate commensurate benefits remains a critical factor for rating assessment.

### **4. Specific Risks Flagged**

*   **Raw Material Price Risk:** Fluctuations in volatile raw material prices impacting profitability.
*   **Ecological Considerations:** Inherent risks related to lead toxicity and increasing environmental advocacy/regulations.
*   **Associate Investment Return:** Potential risk regarding the valuation and return generation from the Tonbo Imaging investment.

### **5. Governance & ESG Details**

*   **Environmental:** ISO certified production facilities; maintained a **zero-liquid discharge record for two decades**; adheres to circular economy approach.
*   **Social:** Comprehensive OSHA system in place; specific safety training provided for hazardous materials, confined space entry, and emergency situations.
*   **Governance:** Oversight by Corporate Environment, Social, and Governance (ESG) Committee to ensure alignment with industry standards.

### **6. Covenants & Security**

*   **Assumptions/Covenants:** Not applicable.
