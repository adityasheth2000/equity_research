**Page Title: Outlook and Key Rating Drivers (Consolidated Analysis)**

**Outlook:** Positive
*   **Rationale:** Expected healthy growth in Turnover of Industry (TOI) driven by favorable demand for a diversified product portfolio and a strong order book. The company is expected to sustain operating margins in line with FY25, aided by product mix and market presence. Capital structure is considered prudent with comfortable liquidity.
*   **Revision Condition:** Outlook may be revised to 'Stable' if revenue growth and profitability fall short of expectations, impacting overall financial performance.

**Detailed Description of Key Rating Drivers:**

**Key Strengths:**

1.  **Improved Financial Performance (9MFY26):**
    *   **Turnover of Industry (TOI):** ₹2,698.71 crore (vs PY: ₹1,491.63 crore).
    *   **Growth:** 80% year-on-year growth.
    *   **Drivers:** Increase in order book position from railways; strong profitability from railway segment (including Kavach order execution) and export markets.
    *   **Margins:** PBILDT margin improved to 37.23%; Profit After Taxation (PAT) margin improved to 27.65%.
    *   **Segment Mix:** Decline in telecom contribution was offset by higher-margin revenue from exports and railway businesses.

2.  **Improved Order Book Position:**
    *   **Current Order Book:** ₹2,999 crore as on December 31, 2025 (vs ₹4,479 crore as on July 31, 2025).
    *   **Major Contracts:** Train Collision Avoidance System (TCAS) equipment for locomotives and trackside upgradation projects (execution timeline: 12-24 months).
    *   **Fresh Orders:** ₹1,375 crore secured in the current quarter for supplying, testing, and commissioning onboard Kavach equipment.
    *   **Visibility:** These orders are to be executed in the next 12 months, providing healthy revenue visibility.

3.  **Stable Financial Performance (FY25):**
    *   **Revenue:** Marginal decline from ₹2,240 crore (FY24) to ₹1,978 crore (FY25).
        *   *Reason:* Unforeseen delay in approval from RDSO towards CLW order and shift in market dynamics (telecom moving to Lithium ion).
        *   *Mitigation:* Offset by growth in Ni-Cad battery segment and diversified product profile.
    *   **Profitability:** Remained healthy despite revenue decline.
    *   **PBILDT Margin:** Improved to 20.35% (PY: 19.19%).
        *   *Drivers:* Higher volumes and better sales realization in industrial battery segment; cost absorption and operational efficiency.
    *   **Net Margin:** Improved to 13.98% (vs 12.52%), supported by low finance cost.
    *   **Exports:** Grew by 40%, supported by new Original Equipment Manufacturer (OEM) clients.

4.  **Diversified Revenue Profile:**
    *   **Segments:** Industrial batteries, Defence & Aviation batteries, Electronics, and Other products.
    *   **Primary Source:** Battery vertical remains significant; Electronics is the next major contributor.
    *   **Strategic Shift:** Actively reducing reliance on the Telecom sector. Revenue share from telecom declined in FY25. Focus shifted to higher-margin products like Ni-Cad and Lithium-ion batteries.
    *   **Geographic Expansion:** Exports expanding to Middle East, Asia Pacific (APAC), Africa, and Europe.
    *   **Railway Visibility:** Multi-year orders from railway sector including Kavach systems, TCAS, Train Management System (TMS), and signalling works.
    *   **Electronics Segment:** Declined by ~36% in FY25.

**Analytical Approach:** Consolidated approach.
*   **Scope:** Consolidated financials of HBL and its subsidiaries have been considered due to financial and operational linkages. Entities are commonly referred to as "HBL" in the analysis.
*   **Subsidiaries:** Consolidated entities listed under Annexure-6 (not included in this extract).

**Negative Factors (General Criteria Cited):**
*   Large debt-funded capex or acquisition impacting credit metrics, specifically debt to EBITDA exceeding 1x.
*   Declining TOI and profitability on a sustained basis.

**Topic: Key weaknesses**

*   **Working Capital Intensive Business:**
    *   **Operating Cycle:** Elongated due to integrated operations and raw material procurement (mostly domestic, some imported).

*   **Potential Risk Associated with Lead-Recycling Operations:**
    *   **Regulatory Risk:** Lead is toxic and environmentally impactful. Industry faces stringent pollution control regulations.
    *   **Environmental Advocacy:** Businesses face increasing pressure as environmental advocacy gains prominence.

**Topic: Risk Profile, Liquidity Position, and ESG Assessment**

**Exposure to Raw Material Price Volatility (Business Risk)**
*   **Cost Impact:** Raw materials constitute approximately 50% to 60% of Total Operating Income (TOI).
*   **Key Inputs:** Major raw materials include lead calcium and lead sub-oxide.
*   **Specific Risk:** Lead comprises ~60% of battery costs. The company faces price escalation risks due to market-driven volatility in these prices, which may impact profit margins.

**Foreign Exchange Fluctuation Risk**
*   **Exposure Source:** Imports of raw materials and exports of products/services.
*   **Mitigation Strategy:** As a net exporter, exposure is largely mitigated via routing foreign currency inflows through an Exchange Earners’ Foreign Currency (EEFC) account.
*   **Financial Impact:**
    *   **FY25:** Consolidated gain on FX fluctuations of ₹10.39 crore.
    *   **FY24:** Consolidated gain on FX fluctuations of ₹6.32 crore.

**Liquidity Position: Strong**
*   **Cash Accruals:** ₹322.71 crore (FY25).
*   **Scheduled Repayment Obligation:** ₹22.27 crore (for FY26), including short-term lease liabilities of ₹4.93 crore.
*   **Available Cash & Bank Balance (as of March 31, 2025):** ₹127.67 crore.
*   **Working Capital Efficiency:** Positive cash flows from operations; low reliance on working capital limits. Average utilization of fund-based working capital limits is negligible for the 12 months ended December 2025.
*   **Cushion:** Unutilised fund-based working capital lines provide sufficient cushion for short-term exigencies.

**Assumptions/Covenants**
*   Not applicable.

**Environmental, Social, and Governance (ESG) Risks**

*   **Environmental:**
    *   **Process Nature:** Lead manufacturing process is hazardous.
    *   **Sustainability Initiatives:** Adherence to circular economy approach with zero-liquid discharge systems, advanced waste recycling, and eco-friendly materials.
    *   **Energy:** Investment in solar power infrastructure and biomass-based energy solutions to reduce dependence on fossil fuels.
    *   **Outcome:** Lowered greenhouse gas emissions and improved energy efficiency.

*   **Social:**
    *   **Safety Standards:** Strict adherence to ISO 14001:2015 and ISO 45001:201.
    *   **Compliance:** Comprehensive OSHA system developed.
    *   **Training:** Safety measures training includes induction, handling hazardous materials, confined space entry, refresher training, and on-site emergency training.

*   **Governance:**
    *   **Oversight:** Corporate Environment, Social, and Governance (ESG) Committee oversees development and implementation of ESG strategies.
    *   **Alignment:** Ensures practices align with industry standards and drive sustainable growth across HBL operations.
