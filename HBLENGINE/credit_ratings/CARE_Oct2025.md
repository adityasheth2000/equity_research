**HBL Engineering Limited - Rating Action and Rationale (October 10, 2025)**

### **Rating Actions & Outlook**
*   **Long-term Bank Facilities:** Reaffirmed at **CARE A+**; Outlook revised from **Stable to Positive**.
    *   Facility Amount: ₹156.50 Cr (Reduced from ₹175.25 Cr).
*   **Long-term / Short-term Bank Facilities:** Reaffirmed at **CARE A+** (LT) / **CARE A1+** (ST); Outlook revised from **Stable to Positive**.
    *   Facility Amount: ₹350.00 Cr.
*   **Short-term Bank Facilities:** Reaffirmed at **CARE A1+**.
    *   Facility Amount: ₹671.00 Cr (Enhanced from ₹471.00 Cr).

### **Rationale for Rating Action**
The revision of the outlook to "Positive" reflects improved financial performance in Q1FY26, characterized by ~20% revenue growth compared to the previous year quarter and healthy profitability margins. Key drivers include:
*   **New Orders:** Commencement of supply of Kavach systems to Chittaranjan Locomotive Works (CLW) following approval for version 4.0 (V 4.0) from Research Designs and Standards Organisation (RDSO).
*   **Revenue Visibility:** New orders for installation of Kavach systems on locomotives and trackside upgradation provide medium-to-long term visibility.
*   **Segment Growth:** Growth in revenue from exports and defence segments.
*   **Operational Strength:** Strong net worth position supporting a healthy financial risk profile despite working capital requirements for CLW orders.

**Historical Context (FY25):** While FY25 revenue was marginally lower than projected due to telecom segment softness and RDSO delays, profitability remained healthy due to better margins in industrial batteries (Nickel-Cadmium and Lead acid) used in defence/industrial purposes and increased export contribution.

### **Business Risk Profile Assessment**
*   **Strengths:** Extensive promoter experience, diversified product portfolio with in-house developed technologies, and well-diversified revenue across sectors (telecom, railways, data centres, defence, aviation, automotive).
*   **Liquidity:** Strong liquidity position marked by sizeable unencumbered cash balances and operational cash flow generation against low debt obligations.
*   **Weaknesses/Risks:** Exposure to volatile raw material prices, working capital intensive operations, elongated operating cycle, foreign exchange fluctuation risk, environmental risk, and intense competition.

### **Financial Risk Profile & Metrics**
*   **Debt Funding:** Negligible reliance on external debt funding; Capex expansion (Lithium-ion battery division, electronic fuses, charging hubs, IT infrastructure) expected to be funded through internal accruals.
*   **Sensitivity Thresholds (Positive Factors):** Sustained Total Operating Income (TOI) over ₹2,800 crore and Profit Before Interest, Lease Rentals, Depreciation, and Taxation (PBILDT) margin over 18%.
*   **Sensitivity Thresholds (Negative Factors):** Debt-funded capex/acquisition impacting credit metrics, specifically Debt-to-EBIDTA exceeding 1x; Declining TOI and profitability on a sustained basis.

### **Analytical Approach & Subsidiaries**
*   **Approach:** Consolidated approach considering financial and operational linkages between parent HBL Engineering Limited and its subsidiaries.

**Topic: Business Risk Profile, Operational Metrics, and Financial Strength (Page 3)**

**Business Risk Profile Assessment:**
*   **Market Position & Diversification:** HBL is a well-known name in industrial batteries manufacturing with established presence in the electronics segment. Revenue base is diversified across telecom, railways, and defence sectors. Battery vertical remains a major revenue spinner followed by electronics.
*   **Customer Concentration:** Strong relationships with reputed clientele including Bharat Sanchar Nigam Limited, Indian Railways, and Indus Towers Limited. High volume of repeat orders received.
*   **Order Book Visibility:** As on July 31, 2025, Order Book stands at **₹4,479 crore** (up from ₹1,178.75 crore as on August 31, 2024). Includes high-value order from CLW for supply/installation of onboard TCAS equipment in 2200 locomotives (execution timeline: 12 months).
*   **Product Mix:** TCAS/TMS, Ni-Cad/Sintered batteries, Silver Zinc, Submarine/Lithium batteries, Valve Regulated Lead Acid batteries, railway signalling works, and defence electronics.

**Key Financial Metrics Cited:**
*   **Working Capital Cycle:** Elongated to **147 days in FY25** against **114 days in FY24**. Driven by delays in approvals for "Kavach" project resulting in higher inventory holding. Post-approval liquidation of inventory driving growth in Q1FY26.
*   **Cash Accruals (FY25):** ₹322.71 crore.
*   **Free Cash & Bank Balance (as of March 31, 2025):** ₹127.67 crore.
*   **Foreign Exchange Gain (FY25):** ₹10.39 crore (vs ₹6.32 crore in FY24) due to net exporter status and EEFC account routing.

**Financial Risk Profile Assessment:**
*   **Liquidity:** Rated **Strong**. Supported by strong cash accruals and low reliance on bank borrowings/working capital limits. Average utilization of fund-based working capital limits is negligible for the last 12 months ended July 2025.
*   **Repayment Obligations:** Scheduled repayment obligation of **₹22.27 crore** for FY26 (includes short-term lease liabilities of Rs. 4.93 crore).
*   **Unutilized Limits:** Sufficient unutilized fund-based working capital lines available to meet short-term exigencies.

**Specific Risks Flagged:**
*   **Raw Material Price Volatility:** Raw materials contribute ~50-60% of Total Operating Income (TOI). Major inputs (lead calcium, lead sub-oxide) are volatile. Since lead comprises ~60% of battery costs, price escalations may impact profit margins.
*   **Environmental/Regulatory Risk:** Lead-recycling operations involve toxicity concerns and stringent pollution control regulations. Company maintains compliance.
*   **Operational Risk:** Working capital intensive business with elongated operating cycle. Procures raw materials mostly from domestic market but some imports.
*   **Currency Risk:** Exposure exists due to imports/exports, but largely mitigated by net exporter status and EEFC accounts.

**ESG Risks / Environmental:**
*   Lead manufacturing process is hazardous.
*   Company follows circular economy approach (zero-liquid discharge systems, advanced waste recycling).
*   Invested in solar power infrastructure and biomass-based energy.

**Assumptions/Covenants:** Not applicable.
