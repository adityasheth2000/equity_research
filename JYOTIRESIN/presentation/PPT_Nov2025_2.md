**Topic: RECONCILIATION OF OPERATING CASH FLOW**

This slide provides a adjusted cash flow reconciliation, highlighting the difference between standard Cash Flow from Operating Activities (CFS) and an adjusted measure that accounts for Fixed Deposits (bank balances).

**Reconciliation Table (Rs mn)**

| Particulars | H1FY26 | H1FY25 |

| **Reconciliation of Cash Flow from Operations** | **205.2** | **216.3** |

**Key Notes & Definitions:**
*   **Adjusted Cash Flow:** The company calculates "Reconciliation of Cash Flow from Operations" at **205.2 mn** for H1FY26 and **216.3 mn** for H1FY25.
*   **Adjustment Rationale:** The table suggests significant cash flows in H1FY26 are tied to changes in bank balances/Fixed Deposits, rather than core operations, as evidenced by the massive swing from 4.1 mn (CFS) to 205.2 mn (Adjusted).
*   **Definitions:**
    *   \*Fixed Deposits having maturity beyond 12 months from the Balance sheet date.
    *   \*\*Fixed Deposits having maturity between 3 to 12 months from the Balance sheet date.
    *   *Note regarding accounting standards:* FDs up to 3 months form part of cash and cash equivalent at the end of Cash Flow.
