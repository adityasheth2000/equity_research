**Key Observations for Analyst:**
*   **Equity Growth:** Total Equity nearly doubled over the 2-year period (1,059.8 Mn in FY23 to 2,292.6 Mn in FY25), driven entirely by growth in "Other Equity" (likely Retained Earnings/APM), as Share Capital remained flat at 120.0 Mn.
*   **Asset Composition Shift:** There is a significant shift from Non-Current to Current assets. Total Non-Current Assets dropped by ~34% from FY23 to FY24 (primarily due to a drastic reduction in Bank FDs from 279.8 to 14.8), while Total Current Assets grew by ~133% over the same period.
*   **Liquidity Position:** The company has accumulated a significant cash war chest. Combined Cash, Bank Balances, and Financial Assets in Current Liabilities rose from ~309 Mn in FY23 to over 1.5 Billion in FY25.
*   **Receivables:** Trade Receivables have grown steadily (914.6 Mn in FY23 to 1,255.4 Mn in FY25), suggesting potential sales expansion or longer payment cycles.
*   **Working Capital:** Net Working Capital (Current Assets - Current Liabilities) has expanded significantly, moving from ~315 Mn in FY23 to ~1,784 Mn in FY25.
