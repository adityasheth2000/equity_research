### **Topic: Company History, Capacity Expansion, and Market Reach (Our Journey)**

**Founding and Early History**
*   **1994:** Company established by Chairman and first-generation entrepreneur, Mr. Jagdish Patel.
*   **1994-1995:** IPO conducted. Initial manufacturing setup at Santej, Ahmedabad over an area of 8,000 square yards.

*   **2006:** Launch of the **Euro 7000** brand (premium adhesive for retail).

## Slide Title: OUR COMPETITIVE EDGE

### Low-cost Manufacturing; Asset light
- **Manpower Costs:** Limited to < 15% of revenues.
- **Selling & Distribution Expenses:** Stood at approximately 16-17% of revenue.
- **Asset Turnover:** At 8x.
- **EBITDA:** Amongst the highest EBITDA per tonne vis-à-vis peers.
- **Balance Sheet & Cash Flow:** Working capital efficient; No Debt; Operating Cash Flow (OCF) & Free Cash Flow (FCF) positive.

### Diversified Product Portfolio
The company offers a wide range of products catering to different substrates with features including:
- **Anti Termite:** Water proof-Fast drying.
- **Wider Coverage:** Fungal resistance-Heat resistance.
- **Weather proof:** High fixing strength.
- **Applications:** Cold and hot press applications.
- **Materials:** Multi-purpose uses for Wood, PVC, and Acrylic.

### Our Network
- **Geographic Presence:** Operates in 14 states.
- **Distribution Channels:**
    - 65 distributors for smaller cities.
    - 56 branches for bigger cities (metros).
    - Reaches 13,000 retailers.
- **Sales Force:** 455 sales executives & business development executives.

### Our Carpenter Reward Model
- **Strategy:** One of the best and highest rewarding carpenter rewarding models in the industry.
- **Loyalty Program:** Reward & loyalty programs devised state-wise.
- **Digital Engagement:** Company app provided to carpenters for claiming and redeeming points (Slab systems created on usage basis).
- **Reach:**
    - Touched 3.5 lakh carpenters through various gatherings & programs.
    - 2 lakh carpenters are registered on the loyalty program.
