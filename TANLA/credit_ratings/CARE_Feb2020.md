### Business Risk Profile & Strategic Developments

*   **Market Position & Operations:**
    *   **Segments:** Operates primarily in Mobile VAS (Value Added Services - messaging, e-commerce, e-payments) & Software and Property Development.
    *   **Market Reach:** Established presence with major telecom operators and content aggregators.
    *   **Key Partners:** Contracts signed with **Airtel** and **Idea** (FY17) for ‘Fastrack’ platform. Contracts in place with **BSNL** for ‘Fastrack’. Negotiating with other Telcos like **MTNL**.
    *   **Subsidiary/Associate Acquisitions:**
        *   **Karix Mobile Private Limited (Karix):** Acquired from GSO Capital Partners (Blackstone). Karix has reach to over 1,500 enterprise clients across banking, insurance, automotive, DTH, retail, consumer products, e-commerce, m-commerce, and government.
        *   **Unicel:** Wholly owned subsidiary of Karix (included in acquisition).
        *   **Gamoofa Softtech Private Limited (Gamoofa):** Big data and AI-driven omni-channel marketing automation platform for B2C companies.
    *   **Proprietary Technology:** Launched **Trubloq** (World’s first blockchain enabled commercial communications stack) on **Feb 26, 2019**, at Mobile World Congress (MWC), Barcelona.
    *   **Regulatory Compliance:** Trubloq and infrastructure are designed to comply with TRAI regulation (**Telecom Commercial Communications Customer Preference Regulations (TCCCPR), 2018**). Tied up with 6 telecom operators (Airtel, Vodafone Idea, BSNL, Videocon, MTNL & TATA) to launch Trubloq.

*   **Management:**
    *   **Promoter:** Mr. Uday Kumar Reddy (Chairman & Managing Director).
    *   **Profile:** Management graduate with >2 decades experience in IT and Telecom Sector. Transitioned company from product-based solution to publicly traded Mobile VAS software leader.
    *   **Board:** Recently on-boarded 4 Independent Directors and 1 non-executive director.

*   **Key Risks & Challenges:**
    *   **Regulatory Risk:** Compliance with **TCCCPR 2018** mandates voice and text communications using blockchain technology.
    *   **Asset Impairment/Depreciation:** New technology and cloudification led to a need for re-assessment of useful life of technology assets.
        *   **Impact:** Additional provision of accelerated depreciation amounting to **Rs. 219.55 crore** in 9M FY20.
    *   **Profitability:** Reported Net Loss of **Rs. 122.04 crore** in 9M FY20 (and Standalone Loss of Rs. 141.04 crore) due to the aforementioned depreciation provisions, despite positive cash flows.

### Business Risk Profile
*   **Revenue Concentration:** High concentration in A2P messaging segment. VAS contributes ~96%-97% of total revenue for the past three years. Mobile VAS segment contribution is around 96%-100% during FY18–FY19.
*   **Competitive Threat (OTT):** Minimal threat assessed from Over-the-Top (OTT) players (WhatsApp, Facebook, Skype).
    *   *Rationale:* While OTT replaces P2P messaging, the A2P segment benefits indirectly as OTT requires two-factor authentication (SMS verification), acting as an indirect driver for A2P growth.
*   **Technological Obsolescence Risk:** A2P business exposed to obsolescence risk due to dynamic nature of technological innovation and alternative communication modes.

### Financial Risk Profile
*   **Leverage:** Low gearing (0.07x - 0.08x) and strong net worth provide financial flexibility and cushion against downturns.
*   **Liquidity:** Strong accural position against negligible repayment obligations. Unutilized bank lines are considered adequate for incremental working capital needs over the next one year.
