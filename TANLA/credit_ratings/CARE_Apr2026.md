#### Key Financial Metrics

*   **Revenue from Operations (FY25):** ₹ 4,028 crore
*   **Revenue Growth (FY25):** 2.5% (marginal improvement)
*   **PBILDT Margins:** 17%
*   **Cash and Bank Balance (as of Dec 31, 2025):** ~₹ 859 crore
*   **Debt Position:** Debt-free (negligible finance lease liabilities)
*   **Customer Tenure Concentration:** 50% of revenue derived from clients with 5+ years tenure.

#### Rationale for Rating Action

**Positive Drivers (Strengths):**
*   **Financial Risk Metrics:** Comfortable metrics with strong liquidity and debt-free status.
*   **Operational Stability:** Robust product development, stable operations, and high customer retention.
*   **Management:** Experienced promoters with a nearly two-decade track record.
*   **Market Position:** Leading Communication Platform as a Service (CPaaS) provider.
*   **Strategic Shift:** Proactive product development enabling a shift toward high-growth revenue channels, mitigating technological obsolescence.
*   **Partnerships:** Collaboration with Microsoft to develop "Wisely," a unified platform for digital interactions using blockchain technology.

**Key Constraints (Risks):**
*   **Pricing Power:** Ratings constrained by pricing pressure in the enterprise communication segment.
*   **Customer Dependence:** High dependence on telecom operators and over-the-top (OTT) vendors limits pricing power.
*   **External Risks:** Exposure to technology shifts, cybersecurity threats, and regulatory changes by the Telecom Regulatory Authority of India (TRAI).

#### Rating Sensitivities

**Positive Factors (Upgrade scenarios):**
*   Total Operating Income (TOI) growing 10% to 15% while maintaining operating margins over 18% on a sustained basis.
*   Revenue contribution from digital platforms contributing over 25% of overall revenues.

**Negative Factors (Downgrade scenarios):**
*   Significant fall in revenue or profitability by over 20% year-over-year (y-o-y).
*   Going for significant external term debt resulting in moderation of solvency position.
*   Notable deterioration in liquidity with cash and bank balance falling by over 50% compared to December 2025 levels.

#### Analytical Approach

*   **Basis:** Consolidated.
*   **Scope:** Carey Ratings has considered the consolidated business and financial risk profiles of Tanla and its subsidiaries, linked through a parent-subsidiary relationship. Entities consolidated for FY25 are listed under Annexure-6 (not provided in this text).

#### Specific Risks Flagged

*   **Regulatory:** Regulatory changes by TRAI could impact operational and financial performance.
*   **Technological:** Technology shifts and cybersecurity threats.
*   **Commercial:** Pricing pressure in the enterprise communication segment.

### Business Risk Profile & Rationale
*   **Market Position:** Strong market share in India's CPaaS segment (~35%). Core business is long-tenured clients (5+ years), enhancing cash flow stability.
*   **Customer Diversification:** Revenue derived from BFSI, eCommerce, healthcare, travel, and public services.
*   **Operational Risks:**
    *   **Pricing Pressure:** High dependence on mobile carriers (91% of 9MFY26 revenues from enterprise communication segment).
    *   **Gross Margin Constraints:** Minimal at 18-20% in the enterprise segment due to high transaction fees paid to Tanla and pass-through SMS cost nature.
    *   **Switching Costs:** High costs/complexity for clients to switch platforms (Application to Person messaging is core infrastructure).

### Financial Risk Profile Assessment
*   **Liquidity:** Strong position with significant cash balance (~₹859 crore) and negligible fund-based working capital utilization.
*   **Leverage:** Debt-free (Term debt is nil). Very low gearing (0.02x).
*   **Protection Metrics:** Strong cash balance relative to liabilities.

### Specific Risks Flagged
*   **Raw Material/Input Risk:** Dependence on mobile carriers and other suppliers for network and data. Limited control on pricing as SMS termination rates are set by telcos.
*   **Technological/Risk:**
    *   **Obsolescence:** Rapidly evolving digital landscape poses a threat for Cpaas solutions.
    *   **Cyber Security:** Risk of system breaches and loss of confidential data (mitigated by Information Security Policy).
