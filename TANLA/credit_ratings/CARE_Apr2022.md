### Detailed Rationale & Key Rating Drivers

The revision in ratings is driven by:
*   **Improved Financial Performance:** During FY20 (April 01 to March 31) and 9MFY22.
*   **Operational Synergies:** Benefits from recent acquisitions.
*   **Capital Structure:** Strong net worth and favourable capital structure with robust debt coverage indicators.
*   **Management:** Experienced promoters & management team with a long track record.
*   **Market Position:** Established market position in Application to Person (A2P) messaging segment with tie-ups for the majority of telecom players in India.
*   **New Products:** Launch of two new platforms in FY21.
*   **Liquidity:** Strong liquidity position.
*   **Constraints:** Ratings are constrained by a concentrated revenue profile, high technological obsolescence risk, and regulatory risk.
