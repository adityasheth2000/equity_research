**JSLL’s Unique Capital-light Approach**

**1. Low CAPEX per bed**
*   **Setup Cost:** ₹3-4 lakh per bed for a typical 100-bed facility.
*   **Asset Strategy:** Setup occurs at custom-made leased facilities rather than owned land/buildings.
*   **Equipment:** Limited investments in specific assets like panchakarma beds and equipment.

**2. Mix of Own & Franchise Assets**
*   **Footprint Breakdown:** 35 out of 117 facilities are operated under franchisees.
*   **Operational Control:** Investment and day-to-day operations for franchisees are managed by the franchise partners.
*   **Revenue Linkage:**
    *   Ayurveda doctors at franchisee centers are on JSLL’s payroll.
    *   Ayurveda medicines are exclusively supplied by JSLL (creating a captive supply chain).

**3. Unique Hub-and-Spoke Model**
*   **Referral Mechanics:** Clinics and day-care facilities act as a sales funnel, referring patients to nearby hospitals.
*   **Volume Drivers:** Outpatient Department (OPD) footfall drives Inpatient Department (IPD) volumes.

**4. Capital-light Business Model Results**
*   **Return Efficiency:** Superior ROCE of 71% (Note: Footnote indicates *3Y Average ROCE*).
*   **Growth Strategy:** Enables the ability to scale rapidly.
*   **Balance Sheet:** Maintaining a robust, debt-free balance sheet.

This slide outlines the strategic growth logic and competitive advantages of Jeena Sikho Lifecare Limited (JSLL). It describes a self-reinforcing cycle ("Flywheel") that links patient satisfaction to operational scale and financial returns.

**Growth & Value Cycle Components:**
*   **Patient Satisfaction & Marketing:** Quality services and effective treatment create satisfied patients, who in turn generate word-of-mouth marketing.
*   **Volume & Scale:** Increased word-of-mouth leads to higher patient volumes, which drives a larger scale of operations.
*   **Efficiency & Cost:** Larger scale and efficiency allow for more cost-effective treatment.
*   **Infrastructure & Capability:** Cost-effectiveness and scale enable the investment in more facilities, better doctors, and higher operating efficiencies.

**Key Financial & Strategic Outcomes:**
The cycle is designed to drive three core outcomes highlighted in the center of the diagram:
*   **Higher Growth**
*   **Better ROCE** (Return on Capital Employed)
