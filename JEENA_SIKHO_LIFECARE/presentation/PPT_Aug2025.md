**Slide Title:** JSLL’s Flywheel Effect / Unique Business Model Positioning

**Strategic Narrative & Business Model:**
The slide outlines a self-reinforcing operational cycle ("Flywheel Effect") designed to drive company performance through patient satisfaction and scale. The cycle is described as follows:
*   **Quality & Output:** Quality services and effective treatment lead to **satisfied patients**.
*   **Acquisition:** Satisfied patients generate the "best word-of-mouth marketing."
*   **Volume & Scale:** Increased patient volumes drive a **larger scale of operations**.
*   **Cost Structure:** Larger scale and efficiency allow for **cost-effective treatment**.
*   ** reinvestment:** The efficiency gains enable investment in **more facilities, better doctors, and higher operating efficiencies**.

**Key Strategic Outcomes (Inner Cycle):**
The model is positioned to drive three specific positive outcomes:
1.  **Higher Growth**
2.  **Better ROCE** (Return on Capital Employed)
3.  **Satisfied Patients** (Serving as the foundational driver)

**Competitive Advantages / Moats:**
*   **Word-of-Mouth:** Reliance on patient satisfaction for low-cost customer acquisition.
*   **Operational Efficiency:** A virtuous cycle where scale reduces costs, allowing for further reinvestment in quality/facilities.
*   **Talent/Facility Expansion:** Ability to attract better doctors and expand facilities due to improved cost structures.
