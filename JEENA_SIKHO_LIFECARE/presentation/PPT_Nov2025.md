**JSLL’s Unique Capital-light Approach**

**1. Low CAPEX per Bed**
*   **Setup Cost:** ₹3-4 lakh per bed (based on a typical 100-bed facility).
*   **Facility Structure:** Setup occurs at custom-made leased facilities.
*   **Investment Scope:** Limited investments restricted to panchakarma beds and equipment.

**2. Mix of Own & Franchise Assets**
*   **Franchise Penetration:** 35 out of 117 total facilities are under franchisees.
*   **Asset Types:** Typically, clinics and day-care centers are operated by franchisees.
*   **Operational Control:**
    *   Investment and day-to-day operations are run by franchisees.
    *   Ayurveda doctors at franchisees are on JSLL's payroll.
    *   Ayurveda medicines are exclusively supplied by JSLL (ensuring revenue capture).

**3. Unique Hub-and-Spoke Model**
*   **Patient Funnel:** Clinics and day-care facilities feed the sales funnel of hospitals in the vicinity.
*   **Conversion Driver:** OPD footfall drives IPD (In-Patient Department) volumes.

**4. Capital-light Business Model Outcomes**
*   **ROCE:** Superior ROCE of **71%** (*Note: 3-Year Average ROCE*).
*   **Scalability:** Ability to scale rapidly due to low capital requirements per asset.
*   **Balance Sheet Strength:** Maintains a robust, debt-free balance sheet.
